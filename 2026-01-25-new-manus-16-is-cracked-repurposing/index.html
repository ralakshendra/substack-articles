How To Build A Content Repurposing Engine With Manus AI

If you’re not familiar, Manus can search the web and compile research, create spreadsheets and documents, and connect to over 8,000 apps through integrations all with a few simple prompts.

Which means if a task is repetitive or requires a bunch of manual steps, you can hand it off and let it run while you work on something else. And if you have loads of content sitting on the internet doing a fraction of what it could for your business, Manus removes the bottleneck.

You don’t need a massive catalog either.

You can absorb years of someone’s best thinking in minutes.

Instead of watching 50 hours of podcast interviews to figure out how a creator structures their frameworks or tells stories — Manus can extract it all and hand you the patterns.

Let’s break it down.

Step 1: Ask Manus To Sweep The Internet For Your Content

I started with a simple prompt:

“Search YouTube for videos where Dickie Bush is a guest. Give me the Channel, Title, and URL for each video in a table.”

Manus came back with 11 links.

There’s no way I could’ve listed all of those from memory. Manus searched YouTube, compiled the results, and handed me the full list. I double-checked the links to make sure it wasn’t hallucinating. The accuracy surprised me.

For comparison, I ran the same prompt using Claude Research. It found a few additional appearances, but in some cases it returned channel links instead of direct video links, and occasionally it matched the wrong channel entirely.

I told Manus about the missing videos I found and moved on.

Next, I wanted the transcripts.

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!DhwH!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc54656e5-54f4-4f11-87db-47332ea6bcf1_1688x1660.png)

Step 2: Extract The Raw Text From Every Asset In Your List

At first, I assumed I’d have to open each video and copy-paste the transcript manually.

Even then, having the links already saved would’ve been a big time saver. But then I remembered I was working with a highly capable AI Agent. So I suggested asked Manus if we could use transcription tool.

Manus tried to use an API call for a Youtube Transcript tool, but said I needed to make an API key

Here’s what I did:

  • I went to

https://www.youtube-transcript.io/

  • Created a free account

  • And generated my API key.

Three clicks later I fed it the API key and it took all 19 links and made the API call to pull the transcripts.

Now, I had 40+ hours of content and 264,000 words waiting to be repurposed.

But the more content you feed an AI, the better it understands your voice and frameworks.

So I didn’t stop with videos.

I exported my newsletters, tweets from the last 5 years, and my Ship 30 for 30 video transcripts. In total, my content added up to 65 videos, 20 newsletters, and 1,323 tweets—715,936 words.

Once all my content was loaded into Manus, prompting became straightforward.

Which is what I did in this next step.

Step 3: Repurpose Your Stories, Frameworks, And Insights As Social Content

Now with everything in one place, I had two goals:

  1. Extract the stories, frameworks, and insights from all my transcripts

  2. Create new social content for X and LinkedIn

So, I asked Manus to:

“Summarize the 20 most valuable frameworks in the video transcripts “
