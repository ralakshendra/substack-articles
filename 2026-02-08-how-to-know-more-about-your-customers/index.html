    Step 1: Get a Rapid Snap Shot of Your Market

    What this prompt does:

    • Generates a rapid overview of your three marketing fundamentals: target customer, product positioning, and core problems solved

    • Uses publicly available information (website, competitor sites) to fill knowledge gaps

    • Creates a strategic foundation before deeper research begins

    How to use it:

    • Fill in what you already know about persona, product, and problems in the [Insert Here] context blanks

    • Provide your brand’s website URL and 1-2 competitor URLs

    • Run the prompt to get AI-generated insights on any gaps

    *Make sure to use an AI that has Deep Research functionality — you’ll need it shortly. I like Gemini personally.

    How to organize the information:

    • Save as a single-page reference document (3-4 short paragraphs) in Google Docs or Notion

    • Use this as your briefing doc before running the remaining four prompts

    • Update it as you learn more from deeper research

    Here’s the prompt:

    #Role
You are a strategic marketing analyst trained in rapid onboarding and positioning discovery. You
specialize in distilling complex business inputs into clear summaries that guide deeper research.

#Goal
Generate a short, high-clarity writeup that answers the three most important questions at the start
of any research project: Who are we targeting, what are we selling, and what problems are we
solving?

#Context
Start with these three blanks. If you already know the answers, paste them below. If not, run this
prompt to get a quick head start using public information like the client’s homepage, product
listings, or competitor analysis.
My ideal customer persona is: **[insert here]**
The product I want to sell is: **[insert here]**
The problems my product is solving: **[insert here]**

#Task
Analyze the brand’s website and (if available) one or two competitor sites. Use this research to
write a few short paragraphs that clearly and confidently answer the three context questions.
Keep language simple and human. Avoid marketing fluff.
Include:

    Step 2: Find What Your Buyers Are Saying

    What this prompt does:

    • Extracts 100 verbatim customer quotes from Reddit organized into five emotional categories (fears, frustrations, wants, beliefs, joys)

    • Captures authentic language, slang, typos, and emotional intensity

    • Generates a persona sketch based entirely on real customer voices

    How to use it:

    • CRITICAL: Use ChatGPT or Gemini’s Deep Research feature for this prompt

    • Paste your persona, product, and problems from Prompt 1 into the context section

    • Let Deep Research scrape Reddit for recent conversations (last 6 months)

    • WARNING: AI can hallucinate Reddit quotes and sources — always verify quotes by clicking through to the actual subreddit threads before using them in copy

    How to organize the information:

    • Create five tabs in your Google Doc (one per emotion category)

    • List 20 quotes per tab with subreddit/thread citations

    • Keep the persona sketch at the top of your master document

    • Bold your top 10-15 most powerful quotes to potentially use for headlines, bullets, stories, etc…

    Here’s the prompt:

    #Role
You are a world-class market research analyst with over 10 years of experience specializing in
extracting exact customer language from Reddit. You excel at uncovering deep, actionable
insights and capturing authentic, emotionally charged quotes that reflect my ideal customer
persona's raw emotions, struggles, and desires.

#Goal
I aim to gather 100 verbatim, word-for-word quotes from Reddit that my target customers use
when discussing their fears, frustrations, wants, beliefs, and joys. I also want a detailed persona
sketch, fully informed by your analysis of these authentic customer voices.

#Context
My ideal customer persona is: [insert details about your ideal customer persona].
The product I want to sell is: [insert details about your product].

    Step 3: Get Inside Your Buyer’s Head

    What this prompt does:

    • Builds a comprehensive psychographic profile across four dimensions: Identity, Problems, Dreams/Desires, and Obstacles

    • Collects thematic quote clusters (not just random quotes) to reveal patterns in how customers think and speak

    • Produces minimum 80 total quotes organized by theme with pattern notes

    How to use it:

    • Feed in your refined persona, product, and problems

    • Let AI identify themes within each of the four categories

    • Review theme clusters and pattern notes to understand not just what customers say but why they say it

    • Again: Verify all Reddit quotes and sources — hallucinations happen

    How to organize the information:

    • Create four more tabs in your Google Doc (Identity, Problems, Dreams, Obstacles)

    • Under each section, list themes as subheadings

    • Group 2-4 quotes under each theme with sources

    • Highlight and comment certain patterns you may be seeing

    Here’s the prompt:

was written from inside the community.

    Step 4: Exposure Your Buyer’s Secret Motivations

    What this prompt does:

    • Maps customer questions across Schwartz’s five awareness stages (Unaware → Most Aware)

    • Reveals surface-level desires and drills three levels deep to uncover core motivations

    • Exposes what customers will say publicly vs. what they hide vs. what they can’t articulate

    How to use it:

    • Input your persona, product, and problems

    • Get three strategic tables that show the customer journey from ignorant to ready-to-buy

    • Use the “So I can...” ladder to connect product features to deep human needs

    • Reference the Will/Won’t/Can’t Tell table to write copy that addresses unspoken objections

    How to organize the information:

    • Save as three separate tabs or table sections:

      1. Market Awareness Levels (questions by stage)

      2. Market Desires Expansion (3-level “So I can...” ladder)

      3. What Will/Won’t/Can’t Tell (public vs. private motivations)

    • Cross-reference desires with your product features for messaging angles

    Here’s the prompt:

    #Goal
Use Eugene Schwartz’s research methodology to surface the deepest market desires and
awareness-level questions of our target audience. Findings will help us write copy that feels

insider-authentic.

#Context
My ideal customer persona is: [insert concise description]
The product I want to sell is: [insert product overview]
The problems my product is solving are: [insert primary pain points]

#Task
1. Market Awareness Levels (Schwartz lens).
Build a table with five columns: Most Aware, Product Aware, Solution Aware, Problem
Aware, Unaware.
For each column list the key questions a prospect in that stage is likely asking. Use
language they would naturally use. Limit to the past six months of real discussions for
inspiration.
2. Market Desires Expansion.
Identify five to ten explicit desires this audience expresses.
Create a “So I can ...” ladder for each desire, asking “why” three times to reveal deeper
motives.
Present the results in a table with four columns: Desire, So I can ... (level 1), So I can
... (level 2), So I can ... (level 3).
3. What Will / Won’t / Can’t Tell Table.
For every desire above, capture three candid viewpoints:
• Will Tell – what they are happy to say out loud
• Won’t Tell – what feels too private or embarrassing
• Can’t Tell – hidden or subconscious drivers they may not recognise
Present this in a table with four columns: Desire, Will Tell, Won’t Tell, Can’t Tell.

    Step 5: Match Your Buyer’s Desires With What You’re Selling

    What this prompt does:

    • Extracts every product feature from official product pages/specs

    • Translates each feature through a four-level ladder: Why it exists → Performance benefit → Emotional benefit → Core human desire

    • Connects hard facts to feelings and aspirations

    How to use it:

    • Provide product page URLs and any spec sheets

    • Let AI catalog every feature (materials, measurements, capabilities, etc.)

    • Review the completed ladder to see how each spec connects to dominant emotions and desires

    How to organize the information:

    • Create a tab in your Google doc to include the generated spreadsheet that has these 7 columns: Product Name | Feature | Why Included | Performance Benefit | Benefit of Benefit | Dominant Emotion | Matching Desire

    • Sort by strongest emotional connection or most compelling desire

    • Use this to write multi-layered copy that speaks to both logic and emotion

    • Reference back to Prompts 2-4 to match benefits with actual customer language

    Here’s the prompt:

    #Goal
Reveal and dimensionalize every meaningful feature of the product so we can translate hard facts
into layered benefits that resonate on a practical, emotional, and desire level, using Eugene
Schwartz’s classic research lens.

#Context
Brand / product family: [insert name]
Primary customer persona: [insert short description]
Core problem the product solves: [insert summary]
Product page link(s): [paste one or more URLs to the official product pages, spec sheets, or
videos]

Market Research AI Prompts by Alin Dragu 8

#Task
1. List raw features
• Visit the product page link(s) provided above plus any referenced spec sheets or videos.
• Capture every concrete feature in a bullet list. Do not add any benefit language yet.
2. Build the Feature-to-Benefit ladder
For each feature identified:
a. Note Why this feature exists in plain language.
b. Translate that reason into a Performance / Benefit starting with “so you can...”.
c. Ask “so you can...” once more to reveal the Benefit of the Benefit (the deeper payoff).
d. Identify the Dominant Emotion the customer feels when that deeper payoff is achieved.
e. Match the emotion to one high-level human Desire (security, freedom, status, belonging,
etc.).
3. Quality control
• Ensure wording comes from real product facts; never invent specs.
• Check that no sentence contains a hyphen or em dash used as punctuation.

#Output
Provide a single table in this exact column order, ready to paste into a spreadsheet:
| Product Name | Feature (what it is) | Why the Feature Is Included | Performance / Benefit (“so
you can...”) | Benefit of the Benefit (“so you can...”) | Dominant Emotion | Matching Desire |
List every feature from step 1 in its own row and complete every column before moving to the
next feature.
