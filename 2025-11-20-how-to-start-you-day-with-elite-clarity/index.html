    How to start your day with elite clarity

    Give this 5-minute prompt a try!

    Author: Nicolas Cole
    Date: 2025-11-20

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!bEt9!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5390e363-69ec-451e-abc0-839233578c0d_2000x1428.png)

    I used to start every day in pure zombie mode:

    • Sucked into 24-hour news

    • Doomscrolling social media

    • Replying to every notification

    And before I knew it…

    6 months flew by, and I had done NONE of the steps I knew I needed to do write consistently online.

    But then?

    I found this simple 5-minute journal prompt called the Morning Kickstart. And now, every day starts with extreme clarity (so I can effortlessly take action towards my goals).

    So, if you’ve been struggling to build your daily writing habit, give this a try!

    It only has 3 questions:

    Question 1: What’s top of mind?

    Every morning when I wake up, I have dozens of things swirling around in my head.

    • Dreams I had

    • Workouts to try

    • Ideas to write about

    • Errands to complete

    • People to reach out to

    This clogs my brain and keeps me from thinking clearly about the things that matter.

    So I start by simply listing out 10 bullet points of anything I’m thinking about.

    I’ll add a little bit of commentary to each, quickly realizing they aren’t important.

    (Despite feeling urgent just a few moments before.)

    And after only a few, quick minutes:

    • My mind is clear

    • My brain feels “awake”

    • I’m ready to move on to the day ahead

    Which leads to the next question…

    Question 2: How am I feeling?

    Nothing fancy here.

    I’m just bringing a little self-awareness to my morning state. This way, I’m not going through the day on autopilot. If I’m not feeling good for too many days in a row, I know I need to make a change.

    And the opposite holds true as well.

    If I’m feeling good for several days in a row, I know I need to double down on that routine.

    Now, for my last question:

    Question 3: What am I excited about that’s coming up?

    Finally, I end the session on a high note with a rush of dopamine and serotonin.

    I brain dump a list of everything that’s coming up I’m excited about across any time horizon:

    • Today

    • This week

    • This month

    • The next year

    • The next decade

    • The next 50 years

    Sometimes I only talk about today.

    Other times I only talk about the next 50 years.

    Either way, this floods me with gratitude and good energy as I picture all the events on the horizon.

    This is why I’m so bullish on journaling.

    Not only is it helping me scale my $600k/month business to 8-figures.

    But when I was working my Wall Street job, it also helped me:

    • Start publishing online

    • Build my daily writing habit

    • Learn how to attract loyal readers

    And now?

    I can point every good thing in my life over the last 5 years back to writing every morning for 90 minutes.

    So, I hope this 5-minute Morning Kickstart journaling prompt helps you build your daily writing habit!
