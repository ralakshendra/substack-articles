    Hey there Digital Writers!

    I've built 2 newsletters to 6 figures in revenue.

    Between Category Pirates and Write With AI, I’ve written 500+ issues—sometimes 2 or 3 deep dives in a single week. I couldn't have kept up without a flexible template that saved me hours on every post.

    These are the 7 sections that I mix and match in every newsletter I write::

    1. Intro

    2. Curated List

    3. Sponsor Spot

    4. Deep Dive

    5. Conclusion

    6. Poll

    7. P.S.

    Today, I’m going to show you how you can put these same building blocks to work for your newsletter with the help of AI. In each section, I'll give you a template to follow. Then, at the end, we'll put it all together into one mega-prompt you can use to generate a complete newsletter draft in minutes.

    Let’s begin with Section #1.

    Section #1: The Opener

    Start your newsletter with a punchy opener that sets the tone for the rest of the post.

    If the first line doesn’t grab your reader’s attention, they’ll be quick to move on to the next email in your inbox. Use a question, a strong opinion, or a striking contrast. The goal is to grab attention and give readers a reason to keep scrolling.

    Template:

    Hey there,

{1 Sentence Strong Opinion opener}

{3-5 Sentences Elaborate On Problems and why they should listen to you}

{1 Sentence Transition To “What We’re Going To Talk About Today”}

{3 succinct bullets}

{Quick segue sentence}

    Note: You can use each template in this newsletter with your favorite AI tool (like Claude or ChatGPT). All you have to do is add your topic and run the prompt.

    Section #2: Curated Links, Tools, Or Resources

    Give your readers an easy way to access the best content on your topic without doing the research themselves.

    Gather up:

    • Links

    • Audience case studies

    • Your best posts from prior issues

    • Books, Podcasts, Tools, YouTube Videos

    Your readers come for the deep dive section, but this section is bonus value for reading.

    Keep it relevant and short.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ITNY!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa44ae7d1-3b0e-4550-a207-c4c2da44b7df_1627x1441.png)

    Template:

    If you’re {short synopsis about where the reader is on their journey, and what their goals are}, then here are the resources you need to dig into to {achieve that goal}:

# Weekly Resource List:

* {Resource Name + Link} (Reading Time) Description…
* {Resource Name + Link} (Reading Time) Description…
* {Resource Name + Link} (Reading Time) Description…
* {Resource Name + Link} (Reading Time) Description…
* {Resource Name + Link} (Reading Time) Description…

    Note: ChatGPT will generate content for this section, including suggested links. However, it may occasionally miss a link or two. If that happens, simply run a quick Google search to fill in any missing details during your editing process.

    Section #3: The Deep Dive

    In this section, share your insights, original thinking, and perspective.

    To structure it, use any proven long-form framework:

    • Step-By-Step Guide

    • The Biggest Mistakes

    • Reasons Why

    • Lessons Learned

    • Breakdowns

    • Etc.

    Choose the best fit format for your topic—and dive in.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!iAFm!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcaa30915-4191-437b-8f32-fc685ed56857_1671x1459.png)

    Template:

    
# {Number} {Things} To {Get Outcome} With {Benefit} Even if {Obstacle}

{In order to achieve X, you're going to need a handful of things.}

{Quick transition to the main points}

Repeat the below section for each point

# {Subhead #}

{Tell the reader the first thing they need in order to be successful.}

{Now, tell them why. Get specific. What is it about this "thing" that is going to make all the difference? More importantly, what do they need to do with it? What instructions do they need in order to be successful here?}

    Section #4: Promotions & Offers

    If you’re monetizing your newsletter, this is where your sponsor shoutout goes.

    A few tips:

    • Keep it short (3-5 sentences).

    • Align the benefit of the offer to your newsletter issue theme for a higher click-thru rate.

    • Try moving this section around inside your newsletter (e.g. before the intro, in the middle of the deep diver, or as a part of the P.S.).

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!N0Fx!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbf371d53-e982-4daf-a5d0-c29d9a7a8e95_1428x846.png)

    Template:

    # Sponsored By: [Sponsor Name]

[1 sentence hook]

[3 sentences highlight a key benefit or unique feature of the sponsor’s offering that aligns with the topic]

[Call to action (CTA) encouraging readers to check out the sponsor’s site, product, or offer]

    Section #5: The Conclusion

    Wrap-up each newsletter with a quick close.

    • Remind the reader of what they just learned

    • Leave them with a parting thought

    • Give them even more resources related to the topic

    • Or point them to your products and services.

    Tie a bow on everything you just told them.

    Template:

    That's it.

Here's what you learned today:

- [Key takeaway 1]
- [Key takeaway 2]
- [Key takeaway 3]

[Reinforce the importance of applying what was learned in a practical way]

[Provide a specific action step or tool for the reader to use]

    Section # 6: The Poll

    In this section, ask for quick feedback.

    Make it simple for readers to tell you what’s working or what’s not.

    • What topics do they want more of?

    • What would they like to hear next?

    Use easy-to-answer polls or short surveys (keep it to one click or a few taps).

    Make it as effortless as possible for your readers to respond.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!E6S_!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F261c070c-1cdf-42d6-b253-cfd544f1ed56_1331x861.png)

    Template:

    Tell us what you thought of today's email.

* Good?
* Ok?
* Bad?

Hit reply and let us know why.

    Note: Most modern newsletter tools like Substack and Beehiiv have built-in poll feature that you can insert into any newsletter issue. But if you want to keep it super simple, ask your reader for a reply.

    Section # 7: The P.S.

    The most valuable real-estate in you newsletter is the intro and outro.

    In the intro, your reader is at their peak level of interest. And at the end, they’re ready to take action. This is the perfect spot to nudge them to take a next step with you.

    You can also use the P.S. to ask for a referral or to take a quick survey.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YUoZ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7b0e5497-20cd-4545-b5e1-aa3d8c05b56a_1599x875.png)

    Template:

    
PS...If you're enjoying [NEWSLETTER], please consider referring this edition to a friend. {Benefit for making a referral}.

And whenever you are ready, there {Number} ways I can help you:

1. [Offer 1]
...

- Creates curiosity

- Uses writer's perspective
- Makes bold statement or asks strange question

- Uses numbers when appropriate
- Creates urgency

Provide a brief preview text that complements the subject line.

    Pro Tip: Mix & Match The 7 Sections

    Experiment with different newsletter formats to see what your readers like best.

    For example:

    1. Intro + Curation + Conclusion

    2. Intro + Deep Dive + Poll

    3. Curation + Promotion + PS

    4. Deep Dive + Poll + PS

    5. Intro + Promotion + Curation + Conclusion

    Is your audience more interested in quick insights or in-depth original content?

    Study your analytics, iterate on the format, then double-down on the winning combination.
