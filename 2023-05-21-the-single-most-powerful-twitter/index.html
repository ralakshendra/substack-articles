The “Single Most Powerful” Twitter Thread Hook: How To Grab Your Target Readers's Attention With A Bold Promise (And A Simple Solution)

This is how you write things millions of people read.

Author: Dickie Bush
Date: 2023-05-21

Ahoy, Digital Writers!

5 AM. My alarm goes off.

I roll out of bed, grab my journal, fire up a double espresso, and start writing.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!yxtq!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0a9f51a0-fdc8-448c-89c0-7ca574c21d45_1640x1834.png)

It’s a simple, daily routine for me.

But for someone else who's struggling to stick with a daily writing habit? It's a beacon of hope. My mundane routine is a goldmine of inspiration for someone else.

It's a secret weapon to connect, motivate, and make a difference.

Sharing the success YOU'VE had keeping a habit makes for a powerful hook.

Why? Because everyone always wants "a lot" for "a little.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!VIli!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F705b4d20-e355-4c79-b2b9-8c07595d9948_1640x1070.png)

And when you’ve kept a habit for a long time, you are an expert to someone else who is just starting out. You hold the keys to years of experience that the reader can use to skip the line. So instead of spending 5 years experimenting with journaling prompts and numerous tools to achieve “massive personal growth,” with very little effort, you can hop straight to it with just “these 5 prompts.”

Viral-worthy ideas promise a huge outcome for minimal cost.

Turn your everyday habits into an attention-grabbing hook with this prompt.

  • Grab your coffee (or double espresso)

  • Grab your habit (meditation, counting steps, always using a certain color pen, etc.)

  • Run the prompt

I am going to train you to write a "Single Most Powerful" short-form content (280 characters or less).

I am going to give you a habit and you will write a "Single Most Powerful" short-form piece of content following the "Single Most Powerful" TEMPLATE.

You MUST follow the format of the template.

You MUST fill in all the variables and follow the template precisely to effectively capture the reader's imagination, establish credibility, and create curiosity.

---
Here is the "Single Most Powerful" TEMPLATE:
---
### The single most powerful habit

The single most powerful habit for {Outcome}:

{Habit}

Over the last {Time Period}, I’ve {Habit} every single {Period}—and along the way, I’ve:

• {Valuable But Time Consuming Action 1}
• {Valuable But Time Consuming Action 2}

But I always return to {Essential Action 1} and {Essential Action 2}:
---
Here is an EXAMPLE of what the template looks like when it's completed so you know what I'm looking for:
---
The single most powerful habit for personal growth:

Journaling.

Over the past 5 years, I've journaled every single morning—and along the way, I've:

• Answered over 1,000 questions
• Tested every app, pen, & notebook

But I always return to a pen, paper, and these 5 prompts:
---

Remember: follow the template precisely to effectively capture the reader's imagination, establish credibility, and create curiosity.

Are you ready for me to give you a habit?
