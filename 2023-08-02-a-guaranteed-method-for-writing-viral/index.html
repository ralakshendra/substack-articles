    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Today I am going to walk you through the easiest way to write viral hooks using ChatGPT.

    If you are anything like me, then you are a student of viral hooks. Whenever I see a hook, a viral Twitter thread, or website with amazing copy that captures my attention, I don’t let it slip away. I take action!

    • I bookmark it and then dissect its structure

    • Then I create my own template by stealing the layout (not the content)

    • Then I write something new within that structure

    I am constantly saving things that “work” so that whenever I am writing that same sort of “thing” (either for myself, or for a client), I can pull it up, stare at it, and steal the same structure (or parts of it) so I have a proven container to write within.

    This is so much better than staring at a blank page.

    And after years of practicing, I have amassed a huge library of my own “proven templates” many of which are now a part of Typeshare.co. I have templates for Twitter Threads, LinkedIn posts, and even articles I’ve written that have gone massively viral.

    I never start from scratch.

    Templates, frameworks, and reliable processes are how you repeat success over and over again.

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YEbL!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7830b167-e422-4614-8e7a-4bca651617fd_1188x762.png)

    Here’s a viral Twitter Thread opener of mine (that got over 1M views):

    It took me 4 years to write my 1st book.

    And it took me 4 months to write my 2nd book.

    Now, I can write a 60,000 word book in 30 days.

    And it took us 6 months to ship our second product, an AI accounting tool for startup founders.

    Now, we can ideate, design, code, bug test, and publish new product upgrades in 60 days or less

    See how this works?

    Side by side, the average reader would never see these two Twitter Thread openers and think they are “the same.” But they are.

    Same structure, different content.

    It’s important to understand how these techniques work “manually” so that when you use technology (like ChatGPT) to speed up the process, you understand what’s happening—AND you understand how to work with the technology to create amazing outputs.

    It’s never been easier to Write With AI using “proven templates” to dramatically increase your chances of going viral.

    But first, what I’m about to show you (and give you) is so simple that you will be tempted to skip putting in the “manual” reps to build your Digital Writing skill.

    Don’t make this mistake! Your hooks will fall short, you will be left wondering why, and you will miss out on the valuable process of internalizing the flow of catchy writing.

    You’ve been warned.

    Let’s go!

    How to use ChatGPT to create hundreds of irresistible hooks & headlines using “proven templates.”

    Anytime you see or create a framework that works—turn it into a reusable prompt!

    • You’ll save hundreds of hours poking through your swipe files.

    • You’ll immediately have 3, 5, 10, even 100 hooks to choose from.

    • And you’ll never get stuck staring at a blank page.

    Here’s how:

    Step 1: Create a prompt frame (template)

    First, we are going to build a frame (or template) for executing hook structures.

    Think of building a frame like creating a blueprint or a structure to help you achieve your goal. In this case, our goal is to get ChatGPT to write hooks for us. The frame we create is like a basic prompt structure that we can use repeatedly for different types of templates.

    I want to write a hook(s) to capture my target audience's attention using a hook structure(s).

I will give you a topic, an audience (FOR WHO), and outcome that audience desires (SO THAT).  

You will write {Number} hook for each of the {Number} hook structures by replacing the variables between the brackets "{}" or any X placeholders.

******

{Insert Hook Structure}

******

Keep the structure (line spacing) of each hook.

Do you understand?

    I included placeholders to make it flexible, allowing you to customize the number of hooks you want to write and how many hook structures you want ChatGPT to follow. So, you can tell ChatGPT, "Hey, give me three different variations for my Twitter Thread opener and adapt it for a Fintech founder" etc.

    With this frame, you can change the prompt for any type of template you want to work on without starting from scratch each time.

    Meta, right?

    Step 2: Plug-in the “proven templates” you want ChatGPT to follow.

    Hand copy each template you want to use into the prompt frame.

    It’s a tedious task, but worth the investment. When you hand copy a hook, you get the added benefit of internalizing the structure with “manual” effort. And now, anytime you want to brainstorm hook ideas, you can just run your prompt.

    100X easier and faster than:

    • Searching through hundreds of saved posts in a swipe file

    • Finding a template that fits your situation

    • Copying the template you want

    • And then finally filling it in

    We’ve gone ahead and created a prompt with 15 proven hook structures for you, so you can see how this works!

    I want to write hooks to capture my target audience's attention using hook structures.

Here's what we are going to do:

I will give you a topic, an audience (FOR WHO), and outcome that audience desires (SO THAT).  

You will write 1 hook for each of the 15 hook structures by replacing the variables between the brackets "{}" or any X placeholders.

*******************************
Here are the 15 hook structures:

**Hook Structure 1:**
I'll let you in on a secret.

You're {doing something wrong}, and it's {causing adverse consequence}.

Here's {strategies to fix it}

**Hook Structure 2:**
X {strategy} I use to {action} instead of {no action}

**Hook Structure 3:**
{Platform/software} is the most popular {thing}

But it's not always {what you think it is}.

Here are X {things} you need to change immediately:

**Hook Structure 4:**
99% of people will never {get to do something rare}.

I did and {here's what happened}.

Here's {what I learned and you can too}:

**Hook Structure 5:**
{Skill} is a superpower.

Learn it and {desirable outcome}.

Use these X {resources} to {achieve outcome} in Y {amount of time}.

**Hook Structure 6:**
If you're not {doing something}, you're falling behind.

Here are {solutions} that will bring about {desirable outcome}.

**Hook Structure 7:**
The most important skill nobody ever taught you: {skill/concept}

These X powerful {strategies} will bring about {desirable outcome}. Use responsibly.

**Hook Structure 8:**
X sentences that'll make you more money than a {dollar amount}{skill} course/degree

**Hook Structure 9:**
{Platform} has {amount of users}.

But 99% of people don't know how to use it.

Here are X powerful features you didn't know existed

**Hook Structure 10:**
Why does {something strange happen}?

Because {data}

And {more surprising insight}.

Here's a breakdown

**Hook Structure 11:**
I've done 1,000 {things} over the past {period of time}.

Here are X tips {I derive from my first hand experience}

**Hook Structure 12:**
{Skill} is the most profitable skill {this year}.

But it's too hard to find resources to master it.

So here are X {strategies} to cut your learning time by 90%

**Hook Structure 13:**
X insanely useful {resources} for {target audience} nobody told you about. You won't believe they're free.

**Hook Structure 14:**
Master {skill} and you can print money at will.

Tragically most people don't know how or where to start.

So here's {X skill} frameworks to get you started

**Hook Structure 15:**
Most advice is garbage.

Here's some advice you shouldn't listen to on {topic}, {topic}, and {topic}.

*******************************

Keep the structure (line spacing) of each hook.

Do you understand? 

Let me know when you are ready for the first topic, audience (FOR WHO), and outcome (SO THAT).

    Step 3: Give ChatGPT a Topic, an audience (FOR WHO), and an outcome the audience desires (SO THAT) and run the prompt.

    Boom! Now you have 15 new hooks you can fold into your writing routine!

    Scan through the list of all the hooks. Find one you like. Then make any final edits for your voice and style and get to shipping!

    I told you it was simple!

    But before we wrap up, let’s see how AI writing stacks up against the Twitter Thread opener I wrote by hand for Fintech startups earlier in this post.

    How ChatGPT hooks compare to a “manually” written hook

    To compare ChatGPT and my writing, I took the original tweet, extracted the structure (turning it into a template), and then ran the hook prompt.

    Here’s the full tweet rewritten as a template:

    It took me {Long time period} to {Achieve huge outcome}.

    And it took me {Short time period} to {Achieve the same outcome again}.

    Now, I can {Achieve the outcome} in {Even shorter time period}.

    Here's the framework:

    Each variable in my template is a mini-instruction for AI to fill in. For example, “4 years” became “{Long time period}” and “write my 1st book” became “{Achieve huge outcome}.” Remember, you are telling AI what you want it to do!

    Once I had my template, I pasted it into the prompt frame we created in Step 1 above, modifying the prompt to create 3 different versions for me, so I had something to choose from.

    Not bad!

    The secret to great outputs using this prompt is the level of clarity you have on who you are writing for. The more time you spend thinking about your topic and the outcome you want to help your target audience achieve, the more powerful the hooks will be. If you need help getting clarity on your topic, try running the FOR WHO / SO THAT prompt.

    Never start from a blank slate.

    Either pull from “proven structures” you’ve discovered & created in your own content library (which is best, because practicing on yourself is the best way to internalize what works, what doesn’t, and why), and/or create swipe files and study other people’s highest-performing content—and then use those “structures” with AI whenever you’re creating a similar asset.
