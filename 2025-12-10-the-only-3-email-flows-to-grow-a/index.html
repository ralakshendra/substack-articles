Flow #1: Convert Free Subscribers To Paid

The goal of this campaign is to convert your new free subscribers into paid members before they lose interest.

It does this by:

  • Welcoming them with your best free posts

  • Overdelivering with a bonus free asset

  • Stacking all the paid benefits in one email

  • Showing social proof through case studies

  • Offering a limited discount to nudge fence-sitters

This is your highest-leverage automation because you’re constantly nurturing warm leads and converting them into revenue—without manually sending a single email.

Flow #2: Onboard Your Paid Subscribers

Substack actually offers 3 variations of this email flow.

Each one allows you to tailor onboarding for a specific type of paid subscriber (new, upgrade, or founding). The goal for all 3 is to ensure your new paid subscribers leverage what they bought. Because engaged customers stay subscribed, leave testimonials, and buy your future products.

Here’s how each variation works:

  • For brand new paid subscribers: In this series you’ll want to reinforce your new paid subscribers decision to upgrade. Make it easy for them to consume your best work and ask what they need help with so you can tailor future content.

  • For free subscribers who upgrade later: Use the same 3-points as above, but acknowledge the transition with context-aware messaging like “Thanks for upgrading...” and “You already know our free content, here’s what else you get...” The psychology is different because they’ve already experienced your free content.

  • For Founding Members: In this sequence, you want to make your most valuable supporters feel like VIPs. Founding Members pay more and commit early—so they deserve exclusive perks, special recognition, and premium treatment that regular paid subscribers don’t get. 3 emails works well here: immediately deliver exclusive bonuses and recap all their Founding Member benefits.

Each of these flows follows the same framework, but you can adapt your messaging to where each subscriber is coming from.

Flow #3: Winback Subscribers Who Cancel

The goal of this flow is to re-convert subscribers who downgraded from paid to free.

Most subscribers who cancel don’t hate your newsletter—they just have timing or budget issues.

This campaign gives them a reason to reconsider when circumstances change.

Which makes them a warm lead and not a lost cause.

And with the right sequence, you can recover 10-20% of canceled subscribers.

Here’s how:

Send 3-6 emails:

  • Ask why they canceled (so you can address concerns)

  • Remind them what they’re now missing

  • Handle common objections like “no time” or “too expensive”

  • Offer a discount to remove the final barrier

And that’s it!

These 3 campaigns (with 5 total variations) cover every critical conversion point in your Substack journey—and they’re now built directly into the platform, so you can set them up once and let them run on autopilot.
