I just created a 60-second YouTube Short using 100% AI.

It’s incredible. It’s weird. And…honestly a little terrifying.

Why?

Because it looks like me. Sounds like me. But it’s not me.

Here’s how it all went down:

I started with a screenshot from a podcast interview I did with Ali Abdaal:

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!BD3s!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa4ff1ec7-d986-414b-9808-9428e2f5e087_1254x1410.png)

The real Nicolas Cole.

Then:

  • I removed the text in Canva and zoomed in on my face.

  • I asked ChatGPT (using the new 4o image model) to generate a portrait of me. It’s not perfect—but it’s freakishly close. _(_Thoughts on the hair?)

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Vb9A!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0173018d-5b37-4d15-a999-8123c1ec014f_468x832.png)

ChatGPT Cole

Next:

  • I grabbed a tweet from my feed and asked ChatGPT to rewrite it as a 60-second Short script.

  • I cleaned up the script and dropped it into ElevenLabs.

  • ElevenLabs cloned my voice with just 10 seconds of audio and turned the script into a lifelike narration.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!q8Hl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F16787c4d-0192-464a-848c-0da27be9f84e_1260x900.png)

Tweet To Transcript With ElevenLabs

Then:

  • I uploaded the AI image + AI voice to Hedra, which stitched them into a video.

  • I brought that into CapCut to add captions, b-roll, music, and some light transitions.

Start to finish: less than an hour.

Check out the finished product.👇

Even though you can 100% tell it’s AI-generated, it’s still amazing.

And it’s only getting better—fast.
