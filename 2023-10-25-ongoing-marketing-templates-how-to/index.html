    Ongoing Marketing Templates: How To Monetize A Superniche Product With Your Free Content

    Author: Dickie Bush
    Date: 2023-10-25

    [

    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    The key to unlocking long-term value from your digital library is a strong “Call-To-Action (CTA).”

    If you have stayed consistent writing online, giving people tons of free value, you need to help your audience take the next step on their learning journey. Ideally, what you want is to have some sort of Superniche Product for each of the different Content Buckets you consistently write about online.

    For example:

    • Content Bucket = Digital Writing

    • Superniche Product = A Beginner's Guide To Writing On “X”

    • Content Bucket = Self-Publishing

    • Superniche Product = How To Self-Publish Your First Book

    • Content Bucket = Solopreneurship

    • Superniche Product = 7 Steps To Make Your First $1 As A Solopreneur

    And so on.

    This way, every time you write & create content in a given Content Bucket, you can always mention to readers, "Oh, and by the way, I also have this product here for you—if you'd like." This is how you continue to collect dividends from your online writing for years into the future on auto-pilot.

    So, today we are going to give you 4 CTA templates and a ChatGPT prompt to help you start monetizing your Superniche product with organic traffic.

    What makes a good Call-To-Action?

    A well-crafted CTA always includes 3 things:

    1. Clear Promise: A good CTA emphasizes the positive outcome and transformation your product promises. Make sure the reader understands the benefit they will receive. For example, instead of just saying "Download Now," highlight the benefit, "Get Your Clear Skin Routine Today!”

    2. Action Words: Your words should encourage visitors to take the next step. Think about what specific action you want the reader to take, and use strong, commanding verbs that clearly communicate that action. Choose language that motivates action, “Grab,” “Steal,” “Unlock,” etc.

    3. Simple Message: A concise and straightforward message is more effective. Aim for clarity and simplicity to ensure your message is understood by all. You don’t want the reader to guess what will happen when they click.

    For any CTA you write, check to make sure you are delivering on all 3 points.

    Let’s look at some templates.

    1. “X” CTA

    At the end of any relevant “X” Thread or even individual Tweet that's getting traction, you can reply to your post with a final Tweet that drives people to your Superniche Product.

    For example:

    If you enjoyed this Thread and want to keep going down the rabbit hole on X topic, I encourage you to grab a copy of [NAME OF SUPERNICHE PRODUCT].

    Inside you'll find X frameworks for solving Y.

    Enjoy!

    2. Organic Atomic Essay CTA

    If you are writing Atomic Essays and want to casually mention that you have your own relevant product related to the topic you're writing about, here's an easy way to let readers know.

    For example:

    I write often about the topic of X. This year, I even put together an [ULTIMATE GUIDE] on the subject where I explain [PROBLEM, SOLUTION, OUTCOME].

    (If you want to check it out, you can find the link in my bio.)

    3. Medium/Quora CTA

    If you are writing articles on Medium or answering Questions on Quora, you should be linking to your Superniche Product every chance you can—so long as it's relevant to the thing you're writing about. You can either do this organically inside the article, or you can include a CTA at the end.

    For example:

    Did you enjoy this article?

    I am passionate about explaining X topic, and have even written an eBook on the subject.

    Click here to grab your copy of [NAME OF SUPERNICHE PRODUCT]. And feel free to DM me with any questions you have!

    I'd love to hear from you.

    4. LinkedIn CTA

    LinkedIn is similar to “X” in that you want to link to your Superniche Products in the comments/reply to your post—not in the main post itself.

    So, make a post on LinkedIn aimed at driving attention, and then in the comments direct people to your Superniche Product.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!x96X!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F31dca2ce-5602-4ef8-89ff-2ab190b81159_2000x2007.png)

    For example:

    If you want to accelerate your learning on X topic, here's a resource I put together to get you started: [link to Superniche Product page]

    Now that you have the templates, let’s speed up the process and get ChatGPT to write them for you.

    Train ChatGPT On The 4 CTA Templates

    Here’s the prompt:

    I need help writing CTAs for my "Superniche Product" using the 4 CTA templates I provide.

I will give you the topic, target audience, and the name of my "Superniche Product."

You will write 1 CTA following each template.  

Here are the 4 CTA templates:

1. X CTA:
If you enjoyed this Thread and want to keep going down the rabbit hole on X topic, I encourage you to grab a copy of {NAME OF SUPERNICHE PRODUCT}.

Inside you'll find {NUMBER} frameworks for solving {PROBLEM}.

Enjoy!

2. Atomic Essay CTA:
I write often about the topic of X. This year, I even put together an {NAME OF SUPERNICHE PRODUCT} on the subject where I explain {PROBLEM, SOLUTION, OUTCOME}.

(If you want to check it out, you can find the link in my bio.)

3. Medium/Quora CTA:
Did you enjoy this article?

I am passionate about explaining X topic, and have even written an eBook on the subject.

Click here to grab your copy of {NAME OF SUPERNICHE PRODUCT}. And feel free to DM me with any questions you have!

I'd love to hear from you.

4. LinkedIn CTA:
If you want to accelerate your learning on X topic, here's a resource I put together to get you started: [link to Superniche Product page]

Do you understand?

    Give ChatGPT your Topic, Audience, and the name of your Superniche Product and it will generate each type of CTA for you.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!oez5!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc298f9c9-925d-4fc7-83e1-c654806d05d0_1302x1254.png)

    From here you can ask ChatGPT to write a few different variations of each type of CTA.

    Great, now I would like a variety of "{Insert Type of CTA}"  

Can you give me 3 more options to choose from?

Be simple and direct.

    Try a mix of CTAs to see what resonates best with your audience.

    Iterate until you find the 1-3 that work best. Then rinse and repeat.

    Ongoing Strategy

    The best thing you can do for yourself as a creator is build a library of relevant Superniche Products you can point to every time you write about your core 3-5 content areas.

    The more often you can direct readers to relevant resources, the more likely they are to buy. Which is how you start monetizing your free content online (by driving readers of your free content to your paid content).
