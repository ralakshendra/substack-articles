Ahoy, Digital Writers!

Being a Digital Creator, is all about maximizing your time, effort, and energy by leveraging things that “scale” online.

And when you:

  • Record a video

  • Hit the publish button

  • Then just leave it alone

You’re missing out on a huge opportunity to connect with more people.

Why?

50% Of Your Audience Will Never See Your Video

Which is why you want to squeeze all the juice you can out of what you’ve created.

The reason so many people don’t do this is because they think, “Well I can’t publish the same content in two places!” Yes, you can.

For example:

Let’s say 1,000 people subscribe to your channel, and 50% see a video. OK, that means 500 people DIDN’T watch. Are they going to be upset they saw the same content elsewhere? No—because they didn’t even see it the first time! And for the people who DID see your video, they won’t care either, because it reinforces what they already chose to consume.

The easiest way to maximize the reach of your video is to take something that worked on YouTube and repurpose it for another platform.

Create Once. Publish Everywhere.

Transform your videos into step-by-step instructions so more people can use your expertise in the way that suits them best.

With Claude.ai and a genius prompt from Design Hacker—it’s dead-simple.

  • Break down any video into actionable steps.

  • Avoid painstaking hours of tedious transcript tinkering.

  • Create something new from something you've already made—instantly.

The first step to turning any video content into an easy-to-work-with format?

Transcription.

Here we go.

Step 1: Find A YouTube Video

Pull up any YouTube video you like.

This could be something in your library. It could something you want to curate for your audience. Or it could be a video you want to learn more about by quickly extracting the lessons.

We like this video. 😉

Once you’ve found a video, click the menu icon underneath the title and expand it.

Scroll down until you see the “Show Transcript” button. Then click it.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Sqkt!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3ed03ca9-bd56-4973-aea6-392357246f0a_479x240.png)

The transcript will display next to the video.

Like this:

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!6RZE!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F98b59b76-c588-4fa1-9c86-c91ae212cabf_844x1112.png)

You can choose to show or hide the timestamps by toggling the ellipsis ( ... ) in the upper right hand corner. We recommend you remove the timestamps. This will prevent any AI from getting confused with a bunch of numbers.

Highlight the video transcript and copy it before moving to the next step.

STEP 2: Paste The YouTube Transcript Into Claude

Head over to https://claude.ai/ and sign up for a free account.

Claude is an AI chatbot just like ChatGPT. It can read and act on prompts that you provide. While it may lack the creativity of ChatGPT, it compensates with clear, simple writing.

Once you are up and running, paste the transcript into the chat.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!rEnc!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3f383652-49b9-4687-beab-03424331346a_500x238.png)

Unlike ChatGPT, Claude can read A LOT of text at once.

It has has no problem reading, sorting, and expanding on a 1-2 hour transcript.

  • You don’t have to paste one section at a time.

  • You don’t have to use any plug-ins.

  • And you’ll get to the writing faster.

Drop the text into Claude, and it will create a text file that you can use however you like.

Which brings us to the next step!

Step 3: Create A Step-by-Step Guide

Once the video is in text form, and you you’ve pasted it into Claude, it's simple to create a step-by-step guide with a prompt.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YJyw!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2219671e-e54b-4000-8ca5-0eeb1fb460ff_500x397.png)

Claude will analyze the transcript, pull out key points, and repackage them into an organized, easy-to-follow step-by-step guide.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!oo6_!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9abadd00-7134-475d-a30d-bfe06e3faf6b_500x433.png)

Review the output, edit as needed, and ship it!

And if you REALLY want to maximize the step-by-step guide, don’t just publish on your blog, you can literally copy/paste and:

  • Publish it on Medium

  • Publish it as an Answer on Quora

  • Include it as checklist in your course.

  • Share it as a downloadable PDF guide.

  • Etc.

In less than 10 minutes, you can turn a 60 minute video a brand new asset.

The point is: just because you used it once, doesn’t mean you can never use it again. You need to train your brain to think the opposite: “I spent all this time creating this amazing video—now what are all the different ways I can reuse this same asset over and over and over again?”

This is how you start to unlock real Digital Leverage.

Here’s the prompt:

Please analyze the transcript of this video and create a fresh new blog article.

Format of your results:

- Intro/overview of the content with a general summary and big idea. Mention who this article and content is for and why it matters.

- A section for each key insight or tip. Write a descriptive but interesting and engaging title for each section. Try to make this is a step-by-step guide with each section being a step in the process. In each section, include a list of important tips, insights, examples, or takeaways. Add sub-bullets with extensive details to teach the concept. Add as much detail, tips or insights as possible. Please also include any common mistakes or misconceptions that were mentioned and then provide the new better/faster approach that was taught.

- Conclusion with a reminder of the important big idea plus a recap and list of steps to take to implement or integrate these ideas successfully.

Important: Instead of just summarizing the content of the transcript, please write it as new and original content as if you are teaching it and sharing it for the first time. Use the content from the transcript as inspiration for this new content.

Additional instructions: Format your response using markdown. Use headings, subheadings, paragraphs, bullet points, and bold text to organize the information in a clean and concise way that is easy to read. Do NOT mention specific names of people, brands, or products mentioned in the training. Instead, write this as more general tips or insights. Write this as if you are teaching a new student this content who is watching your training video or online course. Writing style: thoughtful, educational, and conversational. Write at a 5th-grade reading level for easy comprehension and readability. The goal is to create an 'aha' moment in the reader's mind and raise desire and interest in the topic. However, don't tell them what the aha moment is. Instead, build up tension and then reveal a solution. Make this content engaging, insightful, and persuasive. Use an active voice and write in the 2nd person perspective. Connect with the reader by writing in a conversational and relatable tone.

For a free live training on how to use this prompt and others like it, check out this webinar hosted by Design Hacker on how to save hours creating content by repurposing video.

Bonus: Repurpose A Key Point Into A Social Post

Don’t stop with a guide!

Poke at the transcript to find other key points worth expanding or investigating.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!xavF!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F426b7f9d-b954-42ee-9c90-cff6722c4eba_500x433.png)

Drill down by asking Claude to expand on any key point.

Then keep going.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!8XBl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3d838b44-ede1-4c7f-b251-5eee85bb2f7b_500x433.png)
