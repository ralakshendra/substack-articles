    Want to know the #1 skill that separates six-figure writers from the rest?

    Hint: it's not writing.

    It's sales.

    "Oh no," you scream, "I got into writing so I wouldn't have to talk to people. I don’t want to be salesy.”

    You are not alone, but you are wrong.

    My name is Mitch and I’m an account manager inside PGA. Today I want to show you a prompt I use to turn introverted writers into professional business operators.

    Why You Need to Learn Sales

    Nicolas Cole's $1.8 million ghostwriting agency relied on a powerful sales system.

    Even learning basic sales skills can 5x or 10x your ability to make money writing. What's more, if you want to write persuasively, learning how to have a sales conversation can improve your WRITING ability too.

    No other skill has changed my life more than becoming a competent sales guy.

    If you want to get paid writing...

    You HAVE to learn how to sell.

    Never Get Nervous Before A Call Again

    To that end, I've got two blue-chip assets for you today to help you break through this barrier:

    1. An AI prompt that acts as your personal sales coach (who knows how to find the exact reasons people don't hire you.)

    2. A proven training plan for practicing sales calls (so you can sound LESS salesy and get rid of stage-fright nerves forever)

    All sales calls follow a process. Most conversations do as well. The trick is to be able to influence that process so you can help the person in front of you to the best of your ability.

    Notice I said “help”

    Not “push” or “manipulate.”

    Sales is simply getting someone emotionally, logically, and financially committed to a future result that’s good for them.

    A solid process also gives you MORE flexibility, not less. It allows you to explore rabbit holes and a prospect's curiosity, knowing that you can bring them out of that rabbit hole and back onto the yellow brick road.

    Every successful call follows five fundamental checkpoints:

    1. Opening and setting the agenda

    2. Problem discovery

    3. Process education (what we call Free Consulting)

    4. Product (or service) pitch

    5. Handling objections

    This level of framework alone can't create change. You need a systematic practice routine to move the principles from words on a page to words in your heart.

    How to Make The Invisible Visible

    Today's prompt is all about having an expert review your sales call.

    Think of it as having a 10-year veteran coach with millions of dollars in commissions earned in your back pocket.

    Here are the steps for how to use it:

    1. Record your calls This is not optional. I tend to be merciless to my students if they don't record them. It's like pro football players watching game-tape. Mandatory. Use fathom AI notetaker in your zoom calls. I don't have an affiliate link but at this point I should.

    2. Copy the transcript and paste it into Claude or ChatGPT Personally I prefer Claude. The responses seem to understand human conversational nuance better than ChatGPT.

    3. Paste today's prompt into the LLM The AI will give you a full breakdown of the call process, and give you a rating out of 10 for how you handled it.

      But that's not even the best part.

    4. Targeted practice When I was learning under Alex Hormozi, he gave me a "High Volume; Max Effort" training routine. Unlike going 100% in the gym all day every day, you can train sales as hard as you can and live to fight another day.

    Ghostwriters have about 7 business departments to run all by themselves, so drilling sales for an hour every day like I did back then might not be reasonable.

    But the prompt output will give you EXACTLY where you should practice first so you can close more sales with minimum time investment.

    Here's the prompt:
