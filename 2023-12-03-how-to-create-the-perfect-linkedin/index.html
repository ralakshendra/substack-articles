    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    I am obsessed with learning different AI writing techniques.

    And yesterday I got lost in a 3.5-hour rabbit hole. But there was a fruit at the bottom of the tunnel! Because I discovered Matthew Lakajev (The #1 fastest growing LinkedIn creator in Australia).

    I walked away with a dead-simple ChatGPT technique that he uses for writing a LinkedIn post like his favorite creators, in less than 60 seconds, without typing, and without editing a single word!

    Sounds too good to be true, right?

    Let’s check it out.

    Matthew Lakejev’s Method To Remix Any LinkedIn Post In Under A Minute

    The magic in Matthew's process is in how he reverse engineers a popular post into a set of questions that you, as the creator, can turn around and answer.

    • In your way

    • In your voice

    • For your context

    This makes it very easy for you to use ChatGPT to repurpose a proven post's structure into something unique!

    Nerding out here for a second: the "technical" description for what we are about to do is called "Rhetorical Structure." A rhetorical structure is a method for describing the logical organization of a text. It's how words and sentences fit together to make your writing clear, convincing, and flow in a neat package that your reader will enjoy, like LEGOs.

    Which means you can pick a piece of writing, leverage ChatGPT to unpack why it works (rhetorical structure), and then create something that follows the exact same pattern—remixed in your voice. Amazing! But we don’t stop there.

    To make the process “user friendly,” we turn the structure into a set of questions.

    Then all you have to is answer each question—like talking to a friend!

    The Setup

    You are going to need 3 things to follow along here:

    1. ChatGPT (The “Plus” version works best)

    2. A LinkedIn post (We will use this one as an example.)

    3. Speech-to-text enabled on your computer (or install your favorite plug-in)

    Voice dictation is only necessary if you want to test the promise “without typing.”

    Full transparency: talking is waaaaay easier and faster for giving ChatGPT a laundry list of instructions or answering questions like we are going to do here in a minute.

    With all that out of the way, let’s get into the steps.

    Step 1: Train ChatGPT To Analyze A Viral LinkedIn Post

    Here’s the prompt:

    Hey ChatGPT, I am going to give you a text and I want you to analyze the text and then return a list of questions that someone can answer to create their own post that follows the same rhetorical structure of the original text.  I will give you an example of what I want you to do first before we get started.

For example, here is a post that I might ask you to analyze:

"The most overused cold email phrase ever:

"I hope you're doing well."

Stop this for 3 reasons:

1 → It occupies your first lines (super important space)
2 → It sounds like the start of a selfish sales pitch
3 → It doesn’t add value to your pitch 

It’s said by everyone
→ so you sound like everyone 

It's safe to remove it and
→ get to the point "

And here is an example of how you would respond with questions:

"Question 1: What's an overused phrase or concept in your industry?

Guidance: Think about a term or phrase that is frequently used in your field to the point of becoming a cliché.

Question 2: Can you identify three reasons why this phrase should be avoided?

Guidance: List three reasons that explain why using this overused phrase could be detrimental or ineffective.

Question 3: What's a common characteristic of those who frequently use this phrase?

Guidance: Describe a characteristic that those who frequently use this overused phrase might share.

Question 4: What would be a more beneficial action to replace using this phrase?

Guidance: Propose a more effective or impactful action to replace the use of the overused phrase."

Do you understand what I want you to do?

If so, I will give you the text so you can create the questions.

    ChatGPT will “process” your request and kindly ask the post you would like to analyze.

    Step 2: Turn A LinkedIn Post Into An Interview Script

    Head over to LinkedIn, find a post you want to remix from your favorite creator or use the one we linked above.

    If you need some help, here are a few creators to start with:

    • Dickie Bush :-)

    • Justin Welsh

    • Charles Miller

    Copy/ Paste the original post into the chat box and hit enter!

    Wabam!

    Now you have an interview script.

    Time to do some talking!

    Step 3: Conduct A Personal Interview And Dictate Your Response To Each Question

    At this point, you are ready to have a conversation with ChatGPT.

    Fire up your speech-to-text software. Then tell ChatGPT knows what you’re going to do.

    Here’s a sample script to follow:

    Thanks ChatGPT! Here’s what we are going to do next. I'm going to answer the questions you provided and then you are going to rewrite the original post using my answers. You will follow the style and layout of the original post. Mirror the spacing, sentence length, number of characters, etc. as close as possible.

    After you have primed ChatGPT with your instructions, go right into answering the questions while you are still recording.

    You don’t have to repeat each question verbatim, or even answer all of the questions. Pretend you are talking on the phone with a friend and talk. The dictation software will misspell some things and miss words, but that’s ok because ChatGPT is really smart and can discern what you are trying to say.

    So, does Matthew’s method live up to his promise?

    • Like your favorite creator? Yeah!

    • No typing? Check. I don’t count hitting the enter keys.

    • < 60 seconds? Pretty dang close. The longest bit is answering the questions.

    • No editing? Iffy. It’s hard to skip polishing the last 10%.
