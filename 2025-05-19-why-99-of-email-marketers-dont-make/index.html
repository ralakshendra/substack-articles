    Today’s the final lesson in our 5-day crash course on how to master the most profitable marketing channel on the internet.

    And it’s probably the most important one.

    Because this is where 90% of marketers fumble the bag.

    You write a great hook, a killer story, and a perfectly agitated pain point...

    And then you close with something like:

    “Let me know if you’re interested.”

    “Thanks for reading :)”

    “Hope this helps.”

    😵‍💫

    That’s like going on a perfect date and never asking for a number.

    Here’s What Most People Get Wrong About CTAs:

    They make the reader think.

    Big mistake.

    If you want clicks, you need frictionless action.

    Here’s what I mean:

    ❌ Don’t use words like “Register,” “Sign up,” or “Submit”

    ✅ Do use words like “Get,” “Grab,” “Save,” “Click,” “Watch”

    Your reader doesn’t want to work.

    So make the next step feel like a reward.

    The Best Closing Line I’ve Ever Written

    This one works for free stuff, paid offers, and low-ticket products:

    “That’s just one of the tricks I show inside [Product Name]. If you want the rest, click the link below to grab it.”

    Go back and look at yesterday’s email for an example.

    I gave you a prompt to write an SLO email. Then at the end, I closed with: “Want more?” and dropped a link for even more email marketing prompts.

    Here’s why it works:

    1. Recaps the value you just gave

    2. Hints there’s more where that came from

    3. Gives the reader something to click, immediately

    Like I said above: the click is getting the number.

    So ask for it.

    A Few More Tricks To Get More Clicks

    • End with urgency: “The early bird bonus expires tomorrow.”

    • Add a dopamine tease: “There’s a trick in lesson 4 that made me $10K with one email.”

    • Use specifics: “You’ll walk away with 3 templates you can use forever.”

    And always, always include a link.

    No CTA = no clicks. No clicks = no sales.

    Build Your Library Of High-Converting CTAs

    Paste this into ChatGPT:

    You’re a world-class email marketer.

Write 10 call-to-action lines that match the tone of a helpful, smart friend — not a pushy marketer.

Each CTA should feel like a reward.

Here’s my product: [INSERT PRODUCT]

Here’s the result it helps people get: [INSERT RESULT]

Use verbs like get, grab, click, or watch. 

Make the CTA feel simple, obvious, and dopamine-packed.

For example:

“Click here to grab the Email 365 course and write one high-converting email every day for a year”

    Pick 3–5 and test them in the wild.

    Drop them into your emails and study the results. Watch for clicks, replies, and conversions. Add the higher-performing to a swipe file that you can use over and over .

    Final Thoughts

    If you’ve made it through all 5 lessons, you now have the skills to:

    • Find the right pain points

    • Use proven frameworks

    • Write punchy subject lines

    • Tell stories that sell

    • And close with CTAs that convert

    That’s it.
