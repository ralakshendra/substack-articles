    The Caption Condenser Prompt

    This prompt trims the excess from your writing—without stealing your style.

    LLMs, like ChatGPT, are trained to spot patterns in language, which means AI can quickly identify the core ideas in your content, focus on what matters most, and still write like you.

    There are 3 steps to this prompt.

    Step 1: Copy/Paste The Prompt Into ChatGPT

    Here’s the prompt:

    You are a skilled content editor specializing in social media optimization. 

Your task is to transform a lengthy, in-depth article into a concise, engaging Instagram post. 

Follow these guidelines:

1. Read the entire article carefully to grasp its main points and overall message.
2. Identify the 3-5 most crucial ideas or takeaways from the article.
3. Rewrite these key points in a conversational, Instagram-friendly tone, using no more than 2200 characters total.
4. Maintain the article's core value and insights while dramatically reducing its length.
5. Use short paragraphs and line breaks where appropriate to improve readability.
6. If applicable, suggest an attention-grabbing opening line or question to hook readers.
7. Ensure the condensed version retains the original article's voice and style.
8. If there's a call-to-action in the original piece, include a concise version in the Instagram post.
9. After creating the Instagram post, provide a character count to confirm it's within the 2200 character limit.

Remember, the goal is to create a shorter version that still delivers value and maintains the essence of the original article. Prioritize clarity and impact in your editing decisions.

Original article:
<ARTICLE>
**{Insert your long-form content here}**
</ARTICLE>

Now, transform this article into an Instagram-friendly post following the guidelines above.

    Here’s why repurposing your content across platforms is so powerful:

    Each platform—whether it's Instagram, Medium, LinkedIn, or Quora—has a unique audience.

    Readers on Instagram might not visit your Substack, and LinkedIn followers are unlikely to scroll through your Twitter. By repurposing your content, you introduce fresh value to new groups of people. Every time you share on a different platform, you're essentially launching a "new" idea to a whole new audience.

    With the "Caption Condenser" prompt, you can quickly adapt your long-form content and ensure it reaches as many people as possible without giving up your personality.
