In the last 10 years, I’ve made millions from my writing.

Most of that income comes from repurposing content I’ve already written in new and different ways. But before you can monetize, you need a library of free content.

For two reasons:

  1. Publishing shows you what resonates with your audience.

  2. Without content, there’s nothing to monetize.

So, start by writing on social platforms. Then build a free newsletter to deepen your connection with readers and practice long-form content. Now you’re ready to monetize the same piece of content 5 times.

Here’s how:

Take the same ideas and the same content that you've already proven people find helpful and want to consume and plug them into different business models.

For example:

  1. ($) Write a paid newsletter

  2. ($$) Publish snippets behind Medium's paywall

  3. ($$$) Group newsletters by topic & publish as a book on Amazon

  4. ($$$$) Turn all this content into a course

  5. ($$$$$) Advise people 1:1
