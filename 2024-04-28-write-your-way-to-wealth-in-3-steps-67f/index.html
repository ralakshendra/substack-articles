Write your way to wealth in 3 steps with AI

Author: Nicolas Cole
Date: 2024-04-28

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!68Kg!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa95e33d4-500a-4d02-9a7a-d6b6d9796c3a_1260x900.png)

Hey there!

I've got a serious question for you...

Would you say you'd like to learn the secrets of copywriting in the fastest way possible? If yes, then sharpen your pencil, because I’m going to show you the easy way to write money-making copy with ChatGPT.

Sound good?

Let’s dive in.

The Fast & Easy Method To Write Proven Copy With AI

We are going to do this in 3 simple steps.

Let’s go!

Step 1: Pick A Proven Sales Letter

If you don’t know where to find a sales letter, head over to swiped.co and browse through the ads until you find one you like.

Direct Mail and Advertorials are a good choice. They’re money makers! And they’re long enough that you can see the all the nuanced pieces of a meticulously crafted ad.

After you find an ad, copy / paste the text into a doc or type out the letter by hand. It’s time consuming, but typing the ad will help you internalize the flow and logic of the sales letter.

Once you have the sales letter text, you’re ready for the next step.

I’m going to use the below ad for our example.

This copy was the lead in that helped scale Breakthrough Guitar's company to over $15,000 - $20,000 PER DAY!!

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!HMEl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F51f9b220-0339-4ab9-8370-c111782accb7_1034x1398.png)

Step 2: Turn The Sales Letter Into A “Mad Lib”

Now, head over to ChatGPT, and use the latest model and start a conversation with a prompt like this:

“I am going to give you a text. Please turn it into a mad-lib style template with variables so it's easy to plug my own topic into it.”

Run the prompt and then give ChatGPT the text from Step 1. ChatGPT will turn your text into something that looks like the below image with placeholders that you can fill in.

Which is what we’re going to do next!

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!cM3j!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F64d6ad71-45ff-4a5e-a2d3-042239d6176d_752x1280.png)

Step 3: Train ChatGPT To Write Your Sales Letter

Like any template, you can take what ChatGPT creates and fill in the variables on your own.

For example, I could replace the {Learn Skill} variable with “copywriting” and the {Body Part} variable with “hand” and so on, until I finished writing the letter. Or you can teach ChatGPT about your product / service and then ask AI to fill it in for you. You’ll be done in < 2 seconds. Then you can move on to do any final edits.

The prompt is simple.

Just add the template from Step 2 and details for your product / service. Then run the prompt. ChatGPT will take care of the rest.

I used the following inputs:

Product = The Copywriting Leverage Formula, A Proven Framework To Reverse-Engineer The Greatest Sales Letters Of All Time Into ChatGPT Prompts (Even If You're Brand New To Marketing, AI, and Monetizing Online)

Audience = Writers, solopreneurs, and entrepreneurs who want to grow their business and audience online with words without spending years studying sales and copywriting

Benefits = Write high-quality, money-making copy that engages and persuades with ChatGPT, Break down proven ads with AI and learn what makes legendary copy work, Take the guesswork out of writing sales copy

Here’s the prompt:

Given the below INPUT and TEMPLATE, please write a new text by filling the variables in the TEMPLATE using the INPUT context. 

Your goal is to follow the TEMPLATE. But, you can make minor adaptations to the template for the context.

Avoid salesy words like "game-changer, unlock, discover, skyrocket, revolutionize, etc"

Avoid purple prose AT ALL COSTS

# INPUT = 

{Input the details for your product / service here.}

# TEMPLATE = 

{Input the sales letter you generated in Step 2 here}

Boom! You’ve got a well-written sales letter. How about them apples?
