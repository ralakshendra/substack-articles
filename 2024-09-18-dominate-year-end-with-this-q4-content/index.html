    The internet is back from vacation. The days are shorter, and people are spending more time online. Which means there’s about to be an influx of eyeballs, deal seekers, and end of year shoppers.

    Don’t let it go to a waste!

    It’s time to create a content plan so you’re ready to dominate Q4, finish the year strong, and grab the attention you deserve.

    Here’s what’s in store:

    • Why Q4 is your golden opportunity to steal the spotlight while everyone else hits snooze

    • The lightning fast method for creating a 90-day content plan (and how to drop it right into your Gcal or Outlook calendar in 2-steps)

    • 3-prompt strategies you can heist to avoid 2 of AI’s favorite things: cliches and colons

    Let’s dive in.

    Why You Need A Plan Now

    • Most people slow down during Q4. And you can take advantage of this. The less competition there is, the more visible and relevant you become to your target audience.

    • There’s a natural sense of urgency. At the end of every year, people are motivated to wrap up projects, meet goals, and prepare for the new year. If you position your offer well, you can tap into this mindset and give them exactly what they need at just the right moment.

    • Every piece of content you put out reinforces your expertise. Which keeps you top of mind when your audience is ready to take action. And that’s exactly how you turn interest into conversions.

    Put the right plan in place, and you’ll not only ride the Q4 wave—you’ll create momentum heading into next year.

    So, let’s make a Q4 content plan.

    To do this, we are going to use Josh Spector’s simple method to build a 90-day content library.

    This is the 2nd time we’ve turned one of Josh’s frameworks into a prompt. He’s a clear a thinker (and explainer) which makes for great prompt writing!

    There are several benefits to following his plan:

    Once you plan out 90 days of content, you don’t have to worry about what you’re going to create tomorrow. All you have to do is look at your list and create the piece your content for the day. And after 30 days, you’ll know what pieces of content resonated best with your audience. Which means you can either create more like it, or repurpose it in Q1. The more you learn, the less new content you have to create.

    Josh spelled out his method on both YouTube and X/Twitter.

    I’m repeating it here.

    1. Pick a specific audience you want to help accomplish a specific goal

    2. Write a list of 30 things they need to do to accomplish it.

    3. For each thing on your list, create one piece of content that shows them HOW to do it.

    4. Then, create one piece of content for each that explains WHY they need to do it.

    5. Then, create one piece of content for each thing on your list that shows how OTHERS have done it.

    When you’re done you’ll have 90 individual pieces of content based on 30 things a specific audience needs to do to accomplish a specific goal.

    You can do this manually or you can hire ChatGPT to speed up the process for you. Which is what we’re going to do next.

    Step 1: Prep The Prompt

    Before using ChatGPT, we need to get clear on exactly what we want from it.

    For this prompt, this means knowing your target audience and what they want. Be specific.

    For example:

    • “Burned out freelance writers who want to quit trading time for money so that they can earn more, work less, and stop chasing the next project.”

    • “Online course creators who are overwhelmed by low student completion rates and need a proven system to increase engagement and reduce refund requests.”

    • “Busy working parents who struggle to find time to cook, and want to cut their dinner prep time in half without relying on takeout or processed food.”

    Optionally, build a list of the 30 key things your audience needs to know or do to accomplish what they want to prime AI with your specific ideas. If you’re not sure, AI will create these for you.

    Now that you're prepped, let's proceed!

    Step 2: Run The 90-Day Content Prompt

    First, customize the following AI prompt with the information you prepped in Step 1. For example:

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!nHo2!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2303a53c-fde2-4702-a81f-e3510336e9c6_1111x901.png)

    I intentionally left out specific guidance on the key actions my ideal customer (ICP) needs to take to hit their goal. I wanted to see how ChatGPT would handle it on its own.

    The more specific your input, the more specific (and targeted) AI will be. But if you're looking for AI to help generate ideas, keep it broad.

    Here’s the prompt:

    You are an expert content strategist and digital marketer. 

Your task is to create a detailed 90-day content system for a specific target audience. 

The system should help this audience achieve a particular goal through a series of 30 key topics, each explored from three different angles.

Target Audience:

{Insert Audience}

Action List: {Optional: insert the topics/skills your ICP needs to achieve their goal}

Using the provided target audience information, generate the following:

1. A list of 30 specific topics or skills that the target audience needs to master to achieve their goal. These should be tailored to their current knowledge level and presented in a logical order if possible. Use imperative verbs.

2. For each of the 30 topics, create three distinct content ideas:
   a. A "How-to" piece that explains the process or method for mastering the topic."how to without / even if / when / in / that / so questions" about a very specific, tangible and relevant pain, objection, misconception, assumption, or belief in the target audience's significant phases or milestones in this cycle.
   b. A "Why it matters" piece that explains the importance and benefits of the topic. "why to / why not to / X reasons Y / Why X is Y"
   c. An "Examples and case studies" piece that showcases real-world applications or success stories related to the topic. "What [Real-Life Known Figure/Company] / How [Real-Life Known Figure/Company] / Why [Real-Life Known Figure/Company]" 

3. Break the content into 6 logical phases (e.g. Phase 1: {Objective} (Days X to Y)}

Please ensure that the entire content system is cohesive, aligned with the target audience's needs and preferences, and designed to effectively guide them towards achieving their specific goal.

    After entering your input, ChatGPT will generate a full 90-day content plan in just a few seconds.

    Each phase of the plan includes five key actions, along with content ideas to support each one. For every action, you'll get:

    • A “How To/I” post

    • A “Why To” post

    • A “Who Else Did It” post

    This approach not only showcases your expertise but also builds credibility with your target audience.

    As a result, you'll naturally attract people to YOU.

    Plus, you’ll save time, eliminate the stress of wondering what to post next, and stay focused on writing rather than racking your brain every day to come up with new ideas.

    Why This Prompt Works

    I want to point out 3 things working in this prompt, so you can put them to use in your own prompt writing.

    • Imperative Verbs: Asking AI to use imperative verbs like "Create," "Write," or "Develop" makes it clear what you're helping your target audience achieve. Without this, AI tends to default to clever or cliché copywriting that feels outdated and robotic. Imperatives provide clarity. They make the output direct and useful rather than vague or overly fancy.

    • Prompt Stems: Specific stems like, "How to without / even if," "Why X is Y," and "What [Real-Life Example]" guide ChatGPT to create more interesting topics. These phrases focus the AI on curiosity-driven formats that are relevant to readers. Without them, ChatGPT tends to revert to overused, colon-heavy titles that lack originality.

    • Goal-Oriented Thinking: While not directly about prompting, Josh’s framework complements AI's ability to think logically. When AI has a clear end goal, it can assemble the building blocks to get there.

    The quality of your prompt directly affects the quality of your output.

    Bonus Step: Build Your Schedule And Put It On Your Calendar

    The 90-Day plan isn’t a plan unless it’s on the calendar.

    Scheduling your content makes consistency easy. Because as soon as you open your calendar in the morning, you’ll see exactly what you need to write.

    Turn your 5 phases and 90 content pieces into a “csv” to import into your calendar with this prompt:

    Create a detailed 90-day content calendar based on the 30 key topics from the provided 90-day plan. Mix up the phases so you are not posting about the same thing in a single week.

For each day, use the specific content ideas from the original plan (not placeholders). Format the Subject like this:

{Topic}: {Specific Content Idea}

Export the file as a .csv with the following columns:

Start Date
End Date
Start Time
End Time
Subject

Start time: 8AM every day for 1 hour.

The first day is 10/1/2024.

Name the file: My 90-Content Plan

    Copy/paste the prompt into the same ChatGPT-4 chat you used to create your 90-day plan, then run it. ChatGPT will create a downloadable csv file that you can import directly into your calendar.
