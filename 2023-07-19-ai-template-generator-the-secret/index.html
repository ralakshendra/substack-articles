    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Today we are going to help you build a Bridge to move your niche audience from a social platform to an email list.

    Digital marketers and “gurus” like to make this so much more complicated than it needs to be. In order to make money online (or monetize your writing, period) you do not need to become a master in funnels, ads, affiliates, or anything else.

    Literally all you need to do is:

    • Write about a topic

    • Give something away for free (related to that topic)

    • Sell the more advanced version (on that same topic)

    It's that simple.

    This is exactly how Ship 30 for 30 runs: we write about Digital Writing advice on Twitter → we drive people to our free guide → we encourage the people who download our free guide to join the next cohort of Ship 30 for 30. It's not rocket science!

    Here’s the thing:

    Customers want to “try” before they “buy.”

    Which is why… you need to build a Bridge!

    A Bridge is literally just you giving away something valuable, for free, in exchange for the person's email (aka a lead maagnet).

    A Bridge could be anything:

    • An eBook

    • A Template

    • A Swipe File

    • An Ultimate Guide

    • An Educational Email Course

    • Etc.

    For example, our Bridge for Ship 30 for 30 is our Start Writing Online Ultimate Guide.

    However…

    Your Bridge does NOT need to be some big, fancy eBook or Ultimate Guide.

    Why?

    Because most people end up spending 3+ months writing an eBook, getting in their own head, and ultimately convincing themselves it's “not right” or “not perfect” and then never publishing it. And we don't want that to happen to you!

    Instead, we would recommend starting with something much, much easier (but just as effective).

    Giving Away Templates As Your Bridge

    What is a “template?”

    It's a Tangible asset that makes it very easy for someone to solve a problem.

    For example, let's say you don't know how to calculate your taxes each month—because you have a full-time job, but you also have a handful of side hustles.

    Which “solution” do you value more?

    • Someone explaining to you how to calculate your taxes?

    • A Monthly Tax Calculator

    The second one—obviously.

    The first one sounds long and boring. The second one sounds short and easy.

    Every single reader, listener, viewer, and customer in every single niche on planet earth wants a template, checklist, or step-by-step guide for something

    • Digital Writing: “A 5-Step Checklist To Go Viral On Twitter”

    • Hiring: “A Freelance Contractor NDA Template”

    • Knitting: “A 13-Step Guide To Knitting Your First Wool Sweater”

    • Gluten-Free Cooking: “11 Plant-Based Recipes”

    The list goes on and on.

    Which means, in order to start moving your rented audience over to an owned platform (email), you DON'T have to write a 300-page eBook and you DON'T have to sign yourself up to create a weekly newsletter.

    A much easier, and more effective place to start is to simply create a template that helps solve a problem for your target reader.

    And then give that template (or templates) away for free—in exchange for their email. THEN…once you have people on your email list, you can consider (later) sending them evergreen emails and moving them onto your newsletter.

    Not sure what sort of template you could create?

    Here you go…

    Training ChatGPT To Give You Template Ideas

    When in doubt, ask ChatGPT.

    Step 1: Tell ChatGPT what you need help with.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_KJ3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F61d8f63e-1f30-46f1-b284-c20ca17a274b_2000x678.png)

    Here's the prompt:

    I want to create a free template I can give away to people who follow my content on Twitter.

    I want to create a free template I can give away to people who follow my content on Twitter.

I will give you my niche, and you will give me 10 ideas for templates I can give away for free in the form of a document or digital download.

Are you ready for my niche?

    Step 2: Provide your niche.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!tqDq!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff9f91fb9-e4e1-44da-a518-62dd6d892810_2000x1812.png)

    Step 3: Build one of the options you think your target readers would find most valuable!

    For example, if I'm targeting “ghostwriters,” what templates would ghostwrites find most valuable?

    • They probably would value a Ghostwriting Client Agreement Template.

    • They probably would value a Ghostwriting Proposal Template.

    • They probably would value a Ghostwriting Invoice Template.

    Simple, right?

    So, what “Templates” would your target readers value?

    “But I've never created a template before! I don't know how!”

    Totally valid.

    Which means, in order for us to help you REMOVE that friction, what might be a solution we could provide you?

    “Here's a template you can use… to create a template of your own.”

    See how this works?

    And chances are, you value us giving you a template (making your life easier). Which should show you how much your target readers would value being given a template (to make their lives easier).

    Train ChatGPT to Create The Template For You!

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ymZE!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F11d0b34c-83c7-44e7-a726-99278576184c_1290x908.png)

    Choose a template from the list of ideas ChatGPT provided for your niche.

    Run the prompt:

    Here’s my niche and a description of my template. Create the template for me.

    I want to create a free template I can give away to people who follow my content on Twitter.

I will give you my niche and a description of a template, and you will create the template for me.

Here are the sections for the template:

- A quick blurb to explain WHO it's for, SO THAT the target reader can solve a specific problem/unlock a specific outcome. For example, "Welcome to {Named Template FOR WHO, SO THAT}! This will help you {DO WHAT?}."

- A brief overview of how the template works.

- A list of the steps, tips, mistakes, etc. the person should follow to get the outcome you promise. It must be something the niche can clearly follow and/or “fill in” themselves—in order to solve a specific problem.

FORMAT: Use Markdown formatting to structure the landing page. Utilize #H1, ##H3 for headings, bold for emphasis, and bullet points for lists.

Are you ready for my Niche & Template Description?

    Of course, this is just a starting point.

    • You can create your own templates in Canva.

    • You can create a template in Excel and export the file.

    • You can create a template and design it yourself in Notion.

    A template can literally be anything.

    All that matters is that it is something your target reader can clearly follow and/or “fill in” themselves—in order to solve a specific problem.

    Create your template, give it away for free, and start building the Bridge to your newsletter!
