    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    If you are a content creator, or if you are simply someone who would like to attract attention, stoke emotions, and get in your readers head quickly, this may be the best message you read all week.

    Here is why:

    When I graduated college with a degree in creative writing from Columbia College Chicago in my early 20s, I knew that in order to become a professional (and successful) writer, I was going to have to figure things out on my own. I searched for successful writers I could emulate. People I could look up to. Methods I could study.

    Do you want to know what I found?

    Nothing.

    None of my favorite writers had an online presence. None of them had active social media profiles. It was very frustrating.

    Then, one day, I stumbled upon a "crazy" idea to promote myself as a writer using personal branding, just like musicians and rappers were already doing online at the time—sharing photos and offering a peek behind the artist's curtain.

    My friends laughed.

    They questioned why I was doing what I was doing.

    Some said what most insecure people say, and called me narcissistic. They didn’t get it. Other people said, “What does that picture have to do with writing?” They couldn’t see the bigger picture.

    Well, I didn’t mind. I believed it was a solid strategy and I collaborated with photographers, added my photos to my articles, and actively shared them online. And guess what? People started to recognize me! And I leveraged that recognition to create more content, which attracted more followers. And I used that growing audience to share even more of my journey, which brought in even greater recognition. And so on.

    What’s the bottom line?

    This one simple strategy helped me drive…

    More Than 38 Million Views On Quora Alone!

    Think about that.

    Thirty plus million views—and a lot of people who have seen my picture. That’s crazy! Do you have writing on the internet? If so, you can use the same idea I used in my 20’s to create your own personal brand / image online.

    But what if you don’t want to get recognized on the street or when you walk into a restaurant?

    How To Create Your AI Generated Personal Brand

    Every picture sells a story.

    You can take this idea and use an AI art generator like DALL-E to create your own brand with images that nobody else in the world is creating to inspire your fans, readers, and loyal followers. I’ve been experimenting with AI generated images on X and in our Million Dollar Questions newsletter for the last few months. It’s easy way to stand-out from the stock photos everyone else uses from Unsplash, Pexels, and Pixabay.

    It’s a massive opportunity to differentiate yourself online.

    So…

    • Are you writer who needs a simple and free way to create images?

    • Are you a Solopreneur who wants to create a personal brand (without hiring a photographer)?

    • Do you have a blog, a LinkedIn account, a weekly newsletter, or some other kind of content you create on a regular basis that you’d like to build your image around?

    If you answered yes to any of the above questions, then you’ll love today’s prompts!

    We’re going to break this down into 3 different techniques using ChatGPT Plus.

    1. Generate AI Images that “resemble” you and create a brand image for yourself without revealing your true identity

    2. Create illustrations in the same style as your favorite brand (and avoid paying $5K per piece)

    3. Give you a simple prompting strategy to create any kind of image you want using DALL-E with ChatGPT

    Here we go!

    Technique #1: Create Your Avatar

    Let’s say you want to create an image that resembles you. The first thing you want to do is find a headshot or turn your camera around and take a photo of yourself.

    Then…

    1. Upload A Personal Photo And Generate A New Headshot

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!VKZW!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F865831e7-7a12-4d00-8eb7-69d0f9fae7b5_994x1182.png)

    Say hello to DALL-E Cole!

    Here’s the prompt:

    {COPY/PASTE YOUR HEADSHOT}

I would like to create an illustration in the style of the attached image. Please keep the same colors, hairstyle, clothing style, pose, facial expression, etc.

    2. Place Your Avatar Into A Scene

    A headshot is great for a profile image, but you want to create emotion with your images. And the way you do that is by placing your avatar into a relatable scene. Like sitting on a couch!

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!dCb_!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fda46c1c5-1c8d-41aa-9d65-e2a22d578aa9_1018x870.png)

    A little different, right? Unfortunately ChatGPT image generation is not as good at remembering image detail as it is with text. But it’s close enough that your readers and followers will know it’s “you.”

    Here’s the prompt:

    Perfect! Now, in the same style, using the same illustrated person you just rendered. Please create the exact same person sitting on a couch, arms crossed, with a serious and confident look on his face.

    3. Change The Illustration Style

    Like all prompting with AI, specificity wins. But if you aren’t an artist (like me) and you lack design skills it’s hard to know what and how to ask AI to make modifications to your images. Whenever I find myself in this situation, I do what I would do in real life—ask!

    The point here is to experiment with different styles until you find a mix you like. You can start with any image. But beginning with a headshot is a good way to learn how to dial in your prompt because there are fewer variables you will need to define with a basic image.

    4. Save Your Image Generation Prompt!

    Before we move to the next section, here’s are another quick tip so you don’t have to start from scratch every time.

    Copy DALL-E’s prompt, save it, and use it for later. DALL-E translates your prompt to a script it understands. You can see it by clicking on your image and then click on the “i” in the upper right hand corner.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!odGJ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5873e5bc-5455-4ba2-b94f-c73dcb3c4acd_700x625.png)

    The benefit of doing this is you will be able to see what assumptions DALL-E is making about what you want it to draw. For example, the illustration style, or even the clothes your avatar is wearing. These are variables you can change to suite your style.

    Alright, let’s move on to the next technique.

    Technique #2: Mimic Your Favorite Art / Images

    Using the same strategy we followed in Technique #1, let’s see if we can create imagery like the big brands with a massive budget that can afford 6-figure photographers and illustrators.

    Here are the steps again:

    • Search for an image

    • Copy any picture you like

    • Past the illustration into ChatGPT

    • Train ChatGPT to create a “Like Kind” prompt for you

    • Generate your own image

Return an example prompt that I can prompt you (ChatGPT) with to create something as close as possible to the original image—{INSPIRATTION / WELL KNOWN STYLE} style.

    Technique #3: Create Any Image You Can Write

    The last technique is for designers. This is a simple prompt template you can follow to create any image you can imagine. Replace the variables with your ideas and see what you come up with!

    • A book cover for your first eBook

    • A product box to sell your course

    • An illustration of your framework

    Let your imagination run wild.

    And if you get stuck, ask ChatGPT for ideas like we did in Technique #1.

    Here’s the prompt:

    Design a {A Type of Product, A Type Of Image, A Style Of Image, A Type Of Illustration} 

The design has {Number} colors: {Describe your colors or insert color hex codes}

The images size is {Desired Image Size} 

{Describe Your Image In As Much Detail As You Want!}

    Final Thoughts

    Play with these 3 different image-prompting techniques until you find a style you like.

    Then, use your AI brand style over and over in your social media posts, long-form articles, and newsletters—wherever you show up online. Like a good hook, an intriguing portfolio of images will capture your audience's attention—to the point of recognizing your digital fingerprints.

    Who knows, maybe you'll become the Picasso of AI!
