    How To Write Your Own “Best of 2024” Recap

    Think of an annual review as your highlight reel.

    Here’s what to include:

    • Key milestones: What did you achieve this year? (Big or small, it all counts!)

    • Favorite moments: What stood out? Share stories or numbers.

    • Future plans: What’s next? Give people something to look forward to.

    Why bother?

    It’s a great way to reflect, connect with your audience, and show progress (even to yourself).

    Here’s an AI prompt to get started:

    I want you to write an annual review post for [YEAR] following this structure:

Title: "The Best of [BRAND/NAME]'s [CONTENT TYPE]—[YEAR] Edition" Subtitle: Highlight the categorization approach

1. Year Overview Section:
- Open with "This was a big year for..."
- List 4-6 major achievements/milestones, including:
- Key events
- Metrics (subscribers, downloads, ratings)
- New product/feature launches

2. Future Plans Section:
- Introduce with "Looking ahead, a few things I'm planning to explore in [NEXT YEAR] (no promises!):"
- List 4-5 potential initiatives, including:
- New product launches
- Content expansion plans
- Membership/subscription improvements
- Core commitment to quality

3. Main Content Categories:
- Structure each category as:
- Bold header stating the user need: "I want to [GOAL]"
- Numbered list of relevant resources
- Mix of content types (articles, podcasts, case studies)
- Include cross-references and related content

Instructions:
- Maintain a conversational, personal tone
- Include specific metrics and achievements
- Link related content where relevant
- Use bold formatting for section headers
- Number lists consistently
- Include parenthetical notes for additional context
