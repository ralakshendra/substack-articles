Hey there, Digital Writers!

Great AI content starts with a great AI prompt.

Inputs determine outputs.

So, before I write a new prompt, I always start with the W.R.I.T.E.R.S Compass.

It’s a simple prompt template for guiding AI to create the content you want in less time.

Let’s dive in.

3 Mistakes Prompt Writers Make That Derail AI

Before we get to the framework, let me tell you about the 3 biggest mistakes I made when I was first getting started.

  • Mistake #1: Not giving clear instructions When I first started using AI, I didn't give it clear and detailed instructions. This led to the AI going off-topic. Now, I always include step-by-step instructions in my prompts to guide the AI in the right direction.

  • Mistake #2: Not providing examples I used to think that AI could understand what I wanted without any examples. But then I realized that AI learns better when it has examples to follow, just like humans do. Providing relevant examples helps the AI understand how to apply the instructions effectively.

  • Mistake #3: Not being specific about the target audience While it's obvious to target your audience, AI produces 10X better content when you give highly detailed information about your reader. Instead of just saying "I write for beginning writers," it's important to include specifics about their challenges, goals, concerns, and level of knowledge on the topic.

These mistakes are common—regardless of what you’re writing.

It took me a bit to see that writing prompts for AI is the same as any other kind of writing. The only difference is the fact that AI, not humans, will read it. Just like with any writing, you must give clear steps, useful examples, and think about what your reader (in this case, the AI) needs to get the best results.

The W.R.I.T.E.R.S Compass framework will help you sidestep these mistakes.

The W.R.I.T.E.R.S Compass

When you use this framework, you'll spend less time fiddling with prompts and more time polishing and publishing.

Let's take a look at the 7 key components of the framework:

(W)hat and Why

This component is the foundation of your prompt.

  • What do you want to create? An article, a YouTube script, a sales email?

  • What’s the promise you are making to the reader?

  • State the content type and aim to give AI clear direction.

For example, "Write a blog post about meditation benefits to help my reader cut stress"

(R)equirements

The second critical component is providing instructions and the desired output.

This includes the content's length, format, structure, and any specific actions or elements to include or avoid. For example, "The blog post should be between 800-1000 words, follow a listicle format with an introduction, 5 main points, and a conclusion, and include 3 stats and tips, while avoiding medical advice."

Note the length, format, must-haves, and must-nots so AI meets your needs.

(I)nspiration

Next is inspiration.

Specify the main points you want the AI to cover.

  • *Add 7 recipes that use only plant-based ingredients. *****

  • Use 2-3 real examples of how meditation eases stress.

  • Incorporate 6 podcast episodes that offer insights on AI writing.

Think about what inspired you to write and what the reader will get.

(T)arget Audience

Now, the most important part—who your content is for.

Include demographic or persona descriptions. Add any relevant characteristics, like pains, fears, problems, or desires. Again the more specific you can get, the better results.

For example:

Single moms aged 30-45 who are struggling to balance work, parenting, and self-care. They are always short on time and energy, and feel guilty when they prioritize their own needs. They are looking for quick, practical tips to simplify their lives, reduce stress, and find small pockets of “me time” in their busy schedules.

(E)laboration

At this point you’ve covered the critical elements in your prompt.

The next few components add context and rules to help AI grasp the scope.

For elaboration, focus on information that is directly relevant to your topic and purpose. Avoid overwhelming the AI with too much background information. It dilutes the focus of your content. Instead, aim for a balance that provides “just enough” context to inform the AI.

If you're writing post about the benefits of meditation, then you might include context about your business, your credibility, or the reason you want to talk about the topic.

(R)efinements

In this component, you want to consider your writing from a macro-perspective.

What do you want to make sure the AI does and does not do?

For example:

  • Avoid jargon

  • Always include a real-life example.

  • Avoid words like "deep five, realm, game-changer”

Refinements are like guardrails.

They keep the AI on track.

(S)tyle and Tone

The last component is style and tone.

The key here is to provide examples of what you're looking for. So, choose writing samples that accurately represent your content. Target around 3 samples to give AI a well-rounded understanding of your writing.

Tip: Choose samples that are “similar” to what you want to create with AI. For example, if you’re writing a mistakes posts, don’t give AI a “how to” style post for your example. Instead, use another sample mistakes post.

The W.R.I.T.E.R.S Compass Cheat Sheet To Speed Up Your Prompt Writing

Here’s a handy cheat sheet to reinforce your understanding of the the W.R.I.T.E.R.S Compass framework.

All you have to do is fill in the blanks.

And If you want to speed up the process…

…you can use the below prompt and let AI create a first draft for you.

Just plug in your target audience and go!

I need help filling out the WRITERS COMPASS PROMPT framework for my target audience.

Target Audience = {TARGET AUDIENCE}

Please create 3 prompts using the WRITERS COMPASS PROMPT for my audience.

Vary the type, topic, outcome.

# WRITERS COMPASS PROMPT

Write a [content type] about [topic] with the goal of [desired outcome]. 

The [content type] should be [length] words, follow a [format] with [sections], and include [must-haves], while avoiding [off-limits elements]. 

Incorporate [number] [example type] that [example purpose]. The target audience is [audience description] with [audience characteristics]. 

[Add relevant background information, setting, or details that give the AI a clearer understanding of the task's context, purpose, or intended audience.] 

[Additional refinements. The do and do not list]. 

Use a [tone] and [writing style] similar to this [example length] example: 

[Insert an example text]

As an example, I used the prompt for a target audience of first-time homebuyers who require $10K to cover closing costs.

Refine the output and then copy/paste the prompt into a new AI chat to create a new piece of content.

Try different variations of your prompts and refine them based on the AI's output. By working through the W.R.I.T.E.R.S Compass framework for every prompt, you'll set the AI up for success.

The result?

On-target content generated in a fraction of the time.
