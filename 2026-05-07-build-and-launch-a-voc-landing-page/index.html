    The world’s best conversion copywriters don’t write copy.

    They steal it.

    Specifically, they steal it from the mouths of their customers.

    But most writers and marketers who use AI, when they hear “research,” reach for ChatGPT Deep Research or Claude Research—and get back a polished 12-page report with citations.

    It’s actually the OPPOSITE of what you want.

    Because AI deep research synthesizes secondary sources. What you actually need is the primary source (the unfiltered voice of the customer themselves).

    Here’s how it works:

    • You mine language from places where your customers are already talking about their pain like online reviews, forums, and Reddit threads.

    • You swipe the stickiest phrases and paste them into your marketing copy.

    • Then you sit back and smile when your brand new customers start telling you “I just felt like you were speaking directly to me.”

    This is called Voice Of Customer Research (VOC).

    And through the magic of Claude, you can find and turn the golden nuggets buried in Reddit threads into mouth-watering copy (without wading through hundreds of posts yourself).

    Even better?

    You can instantly build and launch a landing page right from Claude, using your customers’ exact language, and start attracting your audience in under 30 minutes.

    And today I’m going to show you how to do it 5 steps.👇

    Step 1: Install The Reddit PullPush MCP Connector

    MCP (Model Context Protocol) is what lets Claude actually do things outside the chat window:

    • Hit the web

    • Query APIs

    • Search databases

    Without MCP, Claude is a brilliant assistant locked in a room. With MCP, it has hands.

    And we’re going to give it one specific hand: the ability to search Reddit’s archive via PullPush—a free, unofficial archive that has the data Reddit’s official API charges thousands of dollars for.

    Installing an MCP is simple. You just point Claude at what you want and ask it to install it for you. That’s the whole point of having Claude do work for you.

    How to install the PullPush MCP:

    PullPush lives on GitHub.

    This is a website where developers share their code (think: a public shelf for software). You don’t need to read or understand anything on the page. So don’t worry if you’re not technical.

    Here’s what to do:

    1. Grab the Github URL. Just copy the link below:

      https://github.com/jacklenzotti/pullpush-mcp

    2. **Open Claude Code on the Claude desktop app and paste this prompt:

      **“Install this MCP for me and the skill that comes with it: https://github.com/jacklenzotti/pullpush-mcp”

      Claude asks permission to visit the page (click Allow), reads the install instructions, asks permission to update your Claude config (click Allow), saves the skill to your skills folder, and tells you when it’s ready.

    3. Quit Claude completely and reopen it. Cmd+Q on Mac, fully quit on Windows — not just close the window. The new tools only load when the app starts back up.

    4. Verify it worked. Start a fresh chat and ask:

      _“What PullPush tools do you have available?”

      Claude should respond with two: search_comments and search_submissions

      _If it does, you’re set.

    If something went sideways, paste: “Something went wrong with the PullPush install — can you check what happened and fix it?”

    Claude will figure out what broke and fix it.

    Let’s keep going.

    Step 2: Create A Pains & Gains Modifier List To Prep Your Reddit Search

    The biggest mistake writers make when researching with AI tools is trying to research too many things at one time.

    When you do this you get a pile of off-topic results and one or two okay quotes.

    Instead, pick ONE research goal per query, before you type anything.

    There are 6 to choose from:

    • Find pain (people complaining)

    • Find buying intent (people asking what to buy)

    • Find comparisons (X vs Y)

    • Find workflows (how do you actually do this)

    • Find objections (why people don’t buy)

    • Find switching signals (people leaving a tool)

    Constrain your research and you’ll get better results.

    Now, the easiest way to find marketing copy ideas is by using modifier words.

    For example:

    Say you want to find pain around lead magnet creation. You want phrases that signal the emotional state of the creator. People in pain say things like “takes forever,” “no one downloads,” “doesn’t convert,” “wasted my time,” “no signups.”

    Those become your modifier phrases.

    You feed them to Claude Code along with the anchor topic (”lead magnet”), and PullPush returns the comments where real people are venting about your topic in their own words.

    But how do you come up with the modifier phrases in the first place?

    Don’t guess.

    You can generate them with a prompt:

    “I’m doing VOC research on [topic].

    My research goal is to find [pain / buying intent / comparisons / workflows / objections / switching signals].

    Give me 10 phrase snippets (2 to 4 words each) that people would actually type into a Reddit comment when they’re in this state.

    _For example, someone in a desire state might use phrases like “I want” or “I wish” or “where can I find.”

    And someone in a pain state might use phrases like “sucks” or “hate it when.”_

    Run that prompt before you run any Reddit queries.

    The phrases it gives you back become the modifier list you hand to PullPush.

    Let’s build your swipe file.

    Step 3: Build A Copywriting Swipe File From Reddit For Your Marketing Assets

    You could just go to reddit.com and search with the terms from Step 2.

    But you’d be scrolling through dozens of threads, copying lines into a doc by hand, losing your place, and probably giving up after 20 minutes.

    This is where your new MCP pays off.

    Here’s what doing it through Claude gets you instead:

    • Searches comment bodies, not just titles (where people are actually chatting)

    • Pulls 15 comments at a time, sorted by score, so you don’t have to scroll

    • Saves verbatim quotes to a file automatically — no copy-paste tax

    • Synthesizes patterns at the end, instead of you reading every thread

    Once you have all this data you can plug it into all any marketing material you want—landing pages, ads, LinkedIn carousels, Instagram posts, etc.

    Let’s look at an example.

    We’ll use “the pain of building landing pages” for our research topic.

    Try these 3 prompts take you from blank page to usable swipe file.

    Post these into Claude Code one at a time.

    Prompt 1 — Find the hot threads.

    “Use PullPush to find the 10 most-discussed threads in r/Entrepreneur about landing pages, with at least 20 upvotes. Show me titles and links.”

    Claude figures out the search parameters under the hood. The output is a clean list of threads where real conversation actually happened. You skim, pick the 2–3 that look most relevant, and move on.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!rZZr!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0a8e592b-74b0-45e7-bbeb-38ce891ffba0_1704x872.png)

    Prompt 2 — Mine the comments for pain language.

    “Now search comments in r/Entrepreneur for ‘low conversion’ and ‘landing page’. Give me 15 results sorted by score. Save the verbatim comment text to a file called landing-page-voc.md.”

    This pulls the highest-engagement comments containing those phrases. Claude saves them as raw quotes to a file without sanitizing the data.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!AgjE!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F853b78fa-5a04-4daf-bf1d-b243ec12961f_2492x1466.png)

    Repeat with 2–3 more phrase variations to round out the file: “my landing page doesn’t convert,” “wasted time writing copy,” “struggled to write a headline.”

    Each prompt adds fresh quotes. After 4–5 prompts, you’ve got a swipe file with 50–100 verbatim phrases pulled straight from your target customer.

    Prompt 3 — Synthesize at the end.

    Every time Claude has to “remember” what you’ve already searched, it gets slower and more expensive.

    Saving raw quotes to a file lets you run 5 quick searches without that build-up. And then you can synthesize from the file in one final prompt instead of asking Claude to summarize after every query.

    This is the prompt that turns a wall of quotes into actual copy gold:

    “Read landing-page-voc.md.

    Pull the top 10 verbatim quotes that capture pain about building landing pages.

    Group them by recurring pattern (time waste, conversion struggles, copy problems, etc.).

    Then tell me which 3 phrases would work as landing page headlines.”

    Claude reads the file, finds the patterns, ranks the strongest quotes, and hands you headline candidates pulled directly from your customer’s mouth.

    Step 4: Turn Your Swipe File Into Landing Page Copy

    The swipe file you created in Step 3 is the raw material for your landing page.

    And the cool thing is Claude can build the whole page for you.

    I liked this phrase from my research:

    “What’s the value of traffic that doesn’t convert? $0”

    You feed it the swipe file copy and tell it what you’re selling. It does the rest. Which is exactly what I did with the above phrase.

    Step 5: Publish Your Landing Page On The Internet With Vercel

    A beautiful landing page sitting on your hard drive won’t help you make sales.

    So, the last step is to put your landing page on the web.

    To do this, connect Vercel to your Claude account. Vercel is a free web hosting platform. It’s less expensive and more scalable than buying your own physical machine and it can replace your GoDaddy account.

    Here’s how to get started:

    From the Claude Desktop app click: Customize → Connectors → Browse Connectors. Then search for “Vercel.”
