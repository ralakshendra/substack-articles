Fill Your Content Calendar With This 5 Step Prompt Sequence

Author: Nicolas Cole
Date: 2024-05-15

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!zUIs!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0c7d9ea8-2838-44f4-8b64-38f86ef9f6b7_1260x900.png)

Hey there, Digital Writers!

Today, we're using AI to help fill your content calendar in 5 simple steps so you can consistently produce valuable posts, discover new content angles, and maintain a steady flow of content.

Let's dive in.

Step 1: Break Your Topic Into Sub-Topics

The first step in generating a month of content ideas is to break down your main topic into smaller, more manageable sub-topics.

For example, if your main topic is yoga, you might break it down into sub-topics like yoga for athletes, yoga breathing techniques, yoga for stress relief, and so on. Covering different angles in your niche keeps your audience engaged. And it helps you understand what they want to hear from you.

To do this with AI, start by running the below prompt:

I am going to provide you with a topic.

I want you to help me brainstorm a list of sub-topics.

For example, if I give you the topic of gardening, then the sub-topics might be:

- Vegetable gardening
- Flower gardening
- Commercial gardening
- Home gardening
- Gardening with kids
- Gardening tools
- Gardening methods
- etc.

Do you understand?

After you run the prompt, give AI your topic.

AI will generate a laundry list of related ideas for you.

Pick one sub-topic and move to the next step.

Step 2: Brainstorm FAQs

After you’ve selected a sub-topic, the next step is to brainstorm a list of questions that your readers might have about that topic.

Readers read because they want answers to their questions. By addressing these questions, you show you understand their needs. Which ultimately builds their trust in you.

To generate a list of questions using AI, try this prompt:

List 5 FAQs about {SUB-TOPIC}.

Only give me the list of questions.

For example, if your sub-topic is Yoga for Athletes, you might generate questions like:

  • What are the best yoga poses for athletes to increase flexibility?

  • How often should athletes incorporate yoga into their training routines?

  • Can yoga assist with mental focus for athletes?

  • How can yoga help athletes prevent injuries?

  • What are the best yoga poses for relieving muscle soreness and tightness?

Step 3: Expand A Sub-Topic With The 10 Magical Ways

Now that you have a list of questions, it's time to expand on those ideas using the 10 Magical Ways.

Readers love content that provides tips, steps, stories, etc. For example, if one of your questions is "What lessons about body mechanics can athletes learn from yoga?", you might generate sub-questions like:

  • Tips: What are some tips for athletes to maintain proper body mechanics during yoga?

  • Reasons: What are the top reasons athletes should learn about body mechanics through yoga?

  • Mistakes: What are common mistakes athletes make when it comes to body mechanics in yoga?

  • And so on...

You can generate 50 unique content ideas in less than 10 seconds by breaking your questions down into these categories, like magic.

Here's the prompt:

Now, break each question into a list of sub-questions using the 10 Magical Ways to write about a topic.

- Tips
- Reasons
- Mistakes
- Stories
- Examples
- Steps
- Lessons
- Benefits
- Resources
- Stats

Only give me the list of sub-questions.

Step 4: Generate Post Openers

Even with a great question or idea, it can be hard to start writing.

That's where post-openers come in. Flipping each question into a sentence stem gives you a starting point for your writing. For example, if one of your sub-questions is "What lessons about body mechanics can athletes learn from yoga?", you might generate a post opener like: "3 lessons I learned about body mechanics from yoga."

Here's the prompt to use:

Now, write each sub-question as a 1st person sentence stem.

Below is a list of example sentence stem templates. 

- {Number} of lesson(s) I learned about {problem/topic}
- {Number} of mistake(s) I made when {problem/topic}
- {Number} of tip(s) that helped me {problem/topic}
- {Number} way(s) to tackle {problem/topic}
- {Number} benefit(s) of {problem/topic}
- {Number} example(s) of {problem/topic}
- {Number} stat(s) that {problem/topic}
- My {Number} step system to {problem/topic}
- How I helped X with {problem/topic}
- The best way to {problem/topic} is X
- How I got myself to do X for X days in a row.
- I did X for X days. This is what I learned.

Step 5: Write 1 Post A Day For 30 Days

Now that you have a list of post openers, it's time to start writing!

Over the next 30 days, select one post-opener from the pool of 50 ideas you brainstormed in Step 4 and write.

  1. An X post/tweet or thread

  2. An Atomic Essay

  3. A LinkedIn Post

  4. A YouTube short

  5. Or even a long-form newsletter

And on and on your Digital Writing flywheel spins…

That’s it!
