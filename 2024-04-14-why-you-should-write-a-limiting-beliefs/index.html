Why You Should Write a "Limiting Beliefs" Post for Your Niche (And How AI Can Help)

Date: 2024-04-14

Ahoy, Digital Writers!

As someone who's been writing online for years, I've learned that one of the most powerful ways to connect with your audience is by addressing the limiting beliefs holding them back.

By tackling these head-on, you demonstrate you get their challenges. And by providing a new perspective, you help them break through mental barriers and build a bridge that helps them achieve their goals.

Beliefs like:

  • "I need to be an expert to start writing online."

  • "The market is too saturated, there's no room for my voice."

  • "I don't have an interesting story or unique insights to share."

Sound familiar?

These myths are not only false, they're dream-killers. Buying into them means leaving impact and income on the table. But here's some great news—you can use AI to help debunk these beliefs faster and more effectively than ever before. In this post, I'll show you exactly how to do that, using AI writing as an example.

Then I'll give you a prompt to create your own belief-shifting post, no matter what niche you're in.

Let's dive in.

False Beliefs Beginner AI Writers Tell Themselves

When writers first discover tools like ChatGPT, common reactions include:

  1. "Using AI is cheating, it's not real writing."

  2. "Leaning on AI will make me a worse writer in the long run."

  3. "AI is going to replace human writers entirely."

I believed these myself, at first.

But after using AI to write dozens of tweets, threads, and newsletters, I can confidently call BS.

The truth is:

  • Using AI is just using a tool, like spellcheck or Grammarly. It enhances the writing process.

  • AI doesn't replace the need for human insight, creativity and personality. It complements and amplifies those.

  • AI isn't replacing writers, it's making them faster and more productive so they can create more, better work.

See how a little perspective shifts everything?

By directly addressing these myths, we empower writers to embrace AI and level up their craft.

Your Turn: Debunk The Limiting Beliefs In YOUR Niche

Now it's your turn.

Here’s how to write your own belief-shifting post:

  1. List the 3 most prevalent limiting beliefs in your niche.

  2. For each one, share the flawed assumption behind it and why it holds people back.

  3. Provide a new perspective that dissolves the belief and empowers your reader.

  4. Illustrate with examples to make it concrete and relatable.

  5. End with a strong call-to-action to embrace a more expansive mindset.

To make it even easier, we chef’d up this prompt to help you generate the first draft for you.

It’s a long one, but it’ll help you go from zero to first-draft in Ferrari time.

All you need for this prompt is your target audience and the outcome they want. AI will fill in the “limiting beliefs” for you. But…to get a better result, add your perspective on the beliefs holding your audience back.

Here’s the prompt:


# INPUT

Audience = {Your Target Audience}

Outcome = {What Your Target Audience Wants}

Beliefs = 

{List 3 Beliefs Holding Your Audience Back From Getting What They Want}

# Writing Style

Plain and simple. Keep the language straightforward and concise.

# TASK

Please create a new 1000 word text using the INPUT and TEMPLATE I've provided.

TEMPLATE = 

# {Number} Limiting Beliefs Preventing You From Finally Becoming A {Desired Identity}

Hey there!

State the conventional thinking or dominant belief about a topic.

Explain the negative implications for those who don't follow to it.

Give an example, quote, or illustration of the conventional thinking.

Then call it out as foolish.

Explain what you should do instead.

Segue into the 3 beliefs.

# Limiting Belief 1: {BELIEF 1}

The flawed reasoning or assumption behind the belief. Use 1-2 sentences to explain what people mistakenly believe.

Pose a rhetorical question that challenges the myth's validity.

Provide a list of 3 specific examples or scenarios that people might mistakenly use to justify the belief.

Directly state that the belief is not based on a concrete, measurable reality. Use 1 sentence.

Provide a specific, actionable step the reader can take to disprove the belief for themselves. Use 2-3 sentences.

Give a real-world example that clearly illustrates the flaw in the belief's reasoning.

Ask a direct question that prompts the reader to consider the belief's validity. Use 1 sentence.

Provide a definitive answer to the question, emphasizing the importance of taking action. Use 2-3 sentences.

Reassure the reader. Use 1-2 sentences.

# Limiting Belief 2:{BELIEF 2}

Directly refute the belief in a concise, impactful statement. Use 1 sentence.

Identify the flawed reasoning or assumption behind the belief. Use 1-2 sentences to explain what people mistakenly believe about achieving "expert" status.

Emphasize that expertise is a continuous process rather than a one-time achievement. Use 1-2 sentences to highlight the ongoing nature of growth and learning.

Provide a list of 3-4 specific time periods to illustrate the continuous nature of personal growth and development.

Conclude the list with a bold statement that reframes the reader's self-perception.

Provide a specific, actionable step the reader can take. Use 1-2 sentences to guide them in reflecting on their own growth.

Give a real-world example of a successful individual who has overcome the belief. Use 2-3 sentences.

Highlight the key actions the example individual took to turn their experiences into a value. Use a list of 3 short, impactful statements.

Prompt the reader to reflect on their own journey and the challenges they've overcome. Use 1-2 sentences.

Conclude with a bold statement that encourages the reader.

# Limiting Belief 3:{BELIEF 3}

Identify the flawed reasoning or assumption behind the belief. Use 1-2 sentences to explain the common misconception.

Provide a more accurate and empowering analogy for understanding. Use 1 sentence to introduce the new perspective.

Expand on the analogy by listing 3 specific ways in which the process is similar to the new analogy.

Emphasize the reader's agency and control in the process. Use 1-2 sentences to reinforce their power to make decisions and changes as needed.

Provide a specific recommendation for when and how to commit more deeply. Use 1-2 sentences to guide the reader in making an informed decision based on their experiences.

Give a real-world example of a successful individual's journey in exploring and ultimately committing to change. Use 2-3 sentences to illustrate the process of experimentation, learning, and decision-making.

Highlight the key factors that influenced the example individual's decision to commit. Use 1-2 sentences to emphasize the role of data, audience resonance, and personal enjoyment in the decision-making process.

Reinforce the reader's agency and control in the process. Use 1-2 sentences to emphasize their power to make decisions based on their own experiences and goals.

Now, remind the reader what they just learned.

Leave them with a final poignant takeaway.

# Constraints
Avoid metaphors.
Avoid antithesis phrases.
Avoid purple prose AT ALL COSTS
Avoid words like "imagine, streamline, realm, game-changer, unlock, discover, skyrocket, abyss, you're not alone, in a world where, revolutionize, etc"

# Now, take a deep breath, relax, and try your hardest.
