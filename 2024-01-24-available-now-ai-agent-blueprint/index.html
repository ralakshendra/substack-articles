    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Information is cheap.

    It’s infinitely accessible. And pretty soon, information will be created on the fly with AI (as soon as you search for it). No more wading through pages and pages on Google, SEO won’t matter, and Ads will be an entirely different animal.

    It’s going to happen faster than we all think.

    Which is why establishing your brand is going to be more important than ever.

    And not the kind of brand that has a fancy logo, beautiful typography, and a catchy tag-line. No, the brand I’m talking about is one that’s built on trust, leadership, and problem solving.

    People want to know and follow problem solvers in their industry.

    Is Your Brand Getting Left Behind?

    Here’s the thing:

    • If no one knows about you..

    • And if you're not showing up online consistently…

    • Then no one's going to trust you.

    Ok, sure your neighbor will trust you, because they know you and your exemplary character.

    But…

    • If you want to scale yourself online…

    • If you want partnerships deals and affiliate income…

    • If you want to sell literally ANYTHING digitally to complete strangers…

    Then the best thing you can do in the next 12 months is position yourself as a leader on the internet.

    Find an audience of people that have problems. Then solve their problems. Simple.

    When you are problem solver, people will be attracted to you. If you show up on a YouTube video, on X, or LinkedIn and engage with them, then give them the resources, tools, and answers to their questions—guiding them in the right direction, they will follow you, and trust you.

    Why?

    Because people want actual humans who have done and accomplished what they want to accomplish. So, if you show up and document how YOU are solving your own problems and how you are getting results in your business, then you will become known for it. And pretty soon, you’ll become known for a niche you own! That means it will be easier to sell your products and services, and you will start attracting all kinds of new opportunities.

    This is the game.

    It’s not about making generic stuff or spinning up a fake brand / businesses with AI. It's about building authority in your niche and showing up as a leader in your space.

    And that's what this workshop will help you do.

    Step 1: Create Your Brand Snapshot

    Click here for access to the Brand Snapshot Bot

    The Brand Snapshot tool works by asking you a series of guided questions and gathering your input interactively.

    You can start from scratch, share your website, or begin with an example. The tool is designed to be conversational and intuitive. It will ask you short, direct questions and allow you to provide simple, concise answers. You don't need to write long, detailed responses.

    The goal is to make the process as easy as possible while still capturing all the necessary information.

    Here are few examples of the types of questions you can expect to answer:

    • What industry or niche do you serve?

    • Who is your ideal customer or client avatar?

    • What are their main problems or pain points?

    • What transformation or outcome do you help them achieve?

    • What makes your offering unique?

    As you provide answers, the tool will take your input and continue shaping follow-up questions to extract more details.

    Within just a few minutes of the guided Q&A, the AI will have gathered enough information to automatically generate a complete brand snapshot for you. When you are done, ask for the complete snapshot, and copy / paste your snapshot into a document, so you will be ready to go on Wednesday.

    Instructions Template

    Step 1: Remember your role. You are a world-class marketing and copywriting AI assistant. Your task is to create high-quality marketing content for the client based on the information provided. The content that you generate should be original, compelling, engaging, authentic, natural, and conversational, and it should match the client's tone, personality, and energy. Your task is to generate valuable content for the client based on the information provided as well as any other relevant details shared in the thread. Your primary goal is to help the client create original content that aligns with the client's brand voice, tone, and unique selling points. Keep the content on-brand and consistent with the client's voice, tone, and style. Adapt your writing style according to the client's preferences and the target audience's expectations. Organize your output using markdown formatting. Use headings, subheadings, bullet points, bold text, and italics to structure the information, making it easy to read and understand. Be consistent with your formatting across all the content you generate.

Step 2: Review the client information that is uploaded to the knowledge documents in this GPT. Review their brand details plus any other information they have provided to get the full context and understanding of their brand, target customer, writing style, brand voice, products, services, offerings, stories, and anything else that will help you write for them.

Step 3: Complete any tasks that are asked of you. If you have questions or need any additional context from the client, please ask them. If you make up something about the client or their business, put it in brackets [] so the client knows that this is intended to be a placeholder for them to replace later. When you are given a task, you can make assumptions about what they want, but if you are not entirely sure, you can give the client multiple-choice questions to choose from to get additional clarity. Make it as easy as possible for the client by asking them short, simple questions that they can write "1,2,3" etc to tell you which direction is better. For more detailed tasks, you can skip this interactive approach and just give the client what they ask for, but if their request is more vague, please use this interactive approach to gain more clarity before you complete the task.

Tip: Any time a prompt references "CLIENT NOTES & DETAILS" or other references to put in business details or brand snapshot, you can always review the knowledge documents to get that information. You should not need to ask the client much about their business because you can always reference your knowledge base. However, if you can't find the info you need from the knowledge base, then you can then ask the client for additional information.

Important: When you start a new task with the client, give them a warm greeting and ask what they need help with. Keep this short, brief, and very friendly. All of your communication should be fast, clear, and to the point, but also mix in a little quirkiness and charm to make them smile and make working with you more enjoyable.

    Step 3: Put Your AI To Work (47+ Prompts)

    After you’ve built your Bot, you can use the below 47 different prompts to put your Bot to work.

    Because you have trained ChatGPT on everything you want it to know about your business and brand, these prompts become more powerful. Don’t let the simplicity of each of these prompts fool you. You don’t have to spend time telling AI about your business each time you want to do something new.

    Use these as inspiration or use them to start creating new assets for your business.

    Brand Identity and Messaging

    1. Describe our brand values in a compelling way for our website 'About Us' page.

    2. Suggest three unique selling propositions based on our brand snapshot.

    3. Develop a brand slogan that encapsulates our business identity.

    4. Compose a series of taglines for our main product lines.

    5. Visualize a new branding theme that aligns with our core values and create a mood board for it.

    6. Create a series of motivational quotes that reflect our brand ethos for social media.

    Customer Engagement and Experience

    1. Create an introductory pitch for our business tailored to our ideal customer profile.

    2. Draft a welcome email for new subscribers that aligns with our brand voice.

    3. Write a brief guide on 'What to Expect' for our first-time customers.

    4. Map out an innovative customer journey, highlighting unique touchpoints we can introduce.

    5. Suggest ideas for improving customer experience based on our brand values.

    6. Compose a narrative for a day in the life of our ideal customer, highlighting how our product fits in.

    Content and Marketing Strategy

    1. Generate a list of blog post titles that align with our brand's core themes.

    2. Propose basic themes for a seasonal marketing campaign relevant to our industry.

    3. Develop a basic outline for a webinar that introduces our business to new audiences.

    4. Propose a content marketing strategy that aligns with our brand's long-term goals.

    5. Identify trending topics in our industry and suggest content ideas around them.

    6. Suggest refinements to our current marketing strategy based on emerging trends in digital marketing.

    Customer Insights and Market Research

    1. Conduct a competitor analysis for our business based on our niche and provide a summary.

    2. Generate a report on recent market changes relevant to our business model.

    3. Scan customer reviews on similar products and identify areas for improvement in ours.

    4. Analyze the latest consumer behavior trends and how our brand can adapt its messaging.

    5. Identify gaps in our current product line by analyzing market demands and customer feedback.

    Product Development and Innovation

    1. Generate a list of product ideas based on emerging trends relevant to our target audience.

    2. Suggest unique packaging designs that reflect our brand's sustainability commitment.

    3. Suggest innovative ways to integrate AI and IoT (Internet of Things) in our next product line.

    4. Create a concept for a mobile app that enhances our customer's experience with the brand.

    Community and Customer Relationship

    1. Develop a basic FAQ section using our business background and customer journey information.

    2. Outline a basic plan for a community engagement event relevant to our niche.

    3. Draft a detailed plan for an online community building initiative for our brand.

    4. Brainstorm experiential marketing ideas that could bring our brand closer to the community.

    Digital Strategy and Online Presence

    1. Suggest user experience improvements for our website based on current web design trends.

    2. Create an outreach program to connect with influencers in our industry.

    3. Generate a list of keywords for PPC campaigns that are likely to attract our ideal customer.

    Business Strategy and Planning

    1. Develop a risk assessment for a new market we are considering entering, based on our brand snapshot.

    2. Create a roadmap for integrating new technologies into our business operations.

    3. Develop a crisis management plan tailored to potential challenges in our industry.

    4. Analyze our current customer service approach and suggest improvements based on best practices.

    Creative and Narrative Development

    1. Compose a short narrative story that illustrates our brand’s mission.

    2. Draft a creative story that personifies our brand, illustrating its journey and customer impact.

    3. Design an outline for a documentary-style video about our brand's evolution and vision.

    4. Draft a plan for a collaborative project with creators that aligns with our brand's image.

    Interactive and Experiential Concepts

    1. Develop a gamification strategy for our customer loyalty program.

    2. Develop a 'brand retreat' concept to explore new directions and strategies for our company.

    3. Create an interactive online tool for customers to customize their product experience with us.

    4. Design a virtual workshop or webinar series that educates customers about our industry.

    5. Create a series of hypothetical customer personas and their interactions with our brand.

    Bonus: Visual Design Prompts

    Add images of your brand or any visual design that inspires you to your Bot’s knowledge.

    Then try the following prompts:

    Create A Visual Brand Identity

    Prompt 1: Describe my brand

    Describe in detail the visual brand identity of my brand. Describe this in a way that we can send in an email to a graphic designer to build out visual brand assets, graphics, backgrounds, and other visual materials for the online presence and marketing.

    Prompt 2: Apply my visual identity to different marketing assets

    Help me imagine how my brand will look across all my marketing assets. Explain to me how my visual brand identity could be applied to various digital marketing assets.

    Prompt 3: Show me the brand!

    Ok now make a graphic that visually shows this so I can better imagine how this will look.

    Design A Clothing Line For Your Brand

    Prompt 1: Create a clothing line

    Help me imagine a clothing line for my brand that really speaks to my ideal customer and represents our brand and our mission really well and is in alignment with our color palette and visual identity. Create an image that represents all of this and helps me envision a clothing and apparel line for my brand.

    Prompt 2: Show me the clothing line

    Now help me see my brand clothing on actual people. So create some kind of mock-up where my ideal customer is wearing my branded clothing attire.

    Design A World For Your Brand

    Envision my brand as a world and someone lands on my planet. And they're fully immersed in my brand. 

With everything you know about my brand, how would you envision that world? 

What does it look like if someone were to land on my brand planet? 

With everything you know about my brand and what we do, please create a visual concept of what that may look like. The overall mood, the energy, the people, what they're doing, the colors, the textures, the technology, the creative energy, the attitude, optimism. 

I want to see everything incorporated into one visual image of someone entering the world of my brand.

    Visualize Your Customer Transformation

    Prompt 1: The Before

    Now I want to see a visual image that represents our ideal customer before they work with my brand. So help me visualize our ideal target customer before they discover my brand and learn our ways and experience the transformation that we provide. Please give me a clear visual concept that I can really see to help us understand who our ideal customer is and the predicament that they are in in their current situation before they find our brand.

    Prompt 2: The After

    Okay, now help me visualize the "after" image. So, create an image that captures the feeling and the essence and the energy of our ideal customer who has experienced the transformation that our brand provides and show us a visual representation of what that looks like by creating a scene of our ideal customer and enjoying the benefits of having gone through the transformation that we provide.

    Design Your Website

    Great now create a mockup for the home page of our website plus our entire online sales funnel.
