    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Today we want to have a little tête-à-tête about two platforms that have stirred up the Digital Writing community:

    Open AI’s ChatGPT/GPT-4 vs Google’s Bard

    These tools have been making waves for a while now, so, we decided to put them under the microscope to find out which one will make the best Digital Writing intern. After all, you would never hire an intern without interviewing them first!

    Let’s skip to the end.

    If you are wondering which AI platform delivers the perfect prose without having to edit after you run a prompt? Here’s your answer: neither of them. Which, frankly, is true for any AI writing tool. Remember the 10/80/10 Rule.

    Since there's no one-size-fits-all solution (at least for now), you can mix and match the top AI writing tools that align with your specific writing objectives.

    How Digital Writer’s should be thinking about the future of AI tools like ChatGPT and Bard.

    We are still in the very early stages of AI.

    AI is not the first technological advancement to change the world. So, it’s worth peering into the past to see what we can learn and what patterns to look for as AI progresses. Just like the industrial revolution, the PC, the internet, and the smartphone, AI will follow the "Technology Adoption Life Cycle.”

    Here’s what you can expect:

    • The Goldrush: AI is here. Every week a new AI start-up is launched. Entrepreneurs are rushing to carve out a niche for themselves. It’s full of excitement, optimism, and a high degree of uncertainty/fear, as it's unclear which technologies or businesses will prevail.

    • Scramble For Dominance: As AI matures, a Category King will emerge and competition will grow fierce. We are already seeing this. And if you listen carefully, everything is being compared to ChatGPT (a sign of a Category Leader). AI platforms will fight for market share. We will start to see broader marketing efforts, partnerships, acquisitions, and once innovative providers will go out of business.

    • The Category King: Eventually, AI and the market will mature. Innovation will slow, and industry standards/regulation will be enforced. The Category King will set the standard and control the market. But, new entrants will find ways to disrupt the established order.

    • The Rise Of The Underdog: The cycle starts all over again when the status quo is challenged, potentially rendering the previous technology obsolete—think about how “AI chat” has challenged Google search.

    “There’s gold up in them their hills! Grab yer shovels.”

    You’re an early adopter.

    Which means you have a strategic advantage as a Digital Writer over the masses. Leverage it. Write about it. It’s an amazing opportunity for you to stake out your expertise and help others who are coming behind you.

    Take risks and keep experimenting with different platforms.

    Decide for yourself what’s best and share your opinion. Get a deep understanding of how these tools work and how you can leverage them for your business. As the market matures, and a Category King emerges, double-down on your experience.

    You’ll be set.

    Let’s dive in!

    What separates ChatGPT and Bard?

    Just like any tool, each of these AI writing tools comes with their own set of strengths, weaknesses, and unique functions.

    Both ChatGPT and Bard are backed by powerful machine learning algorithms that generate human-like text. They can be used to draft articles, brainstorm ideas, write emails, tweets—literally anything you ask them to. They each have free access. But you’ll have to shell out $20 / month to get access GPT-4 and internet browsing on ChatGTP. AI Superconsumers would gladly pay 10X the price of ChatGPT (an argument for another day).

    What does ChatGPT offer Digital Writers that Bard doesn’t?

    Creativity.

    What ChatGPT lacks in user experience, it makes up for in creativity.

    It's like a multi-tool: it can simulate a conversation, answer questions, write like Jerry Seinfeld and Barbara Walters had a baby, etc. It’s particularly good at generating narrative and descriptive text, making it a better choice for creative writing and storytelling.

    To illustrate, let's consider the disparity between the two platforms when it comes to a basic task like brainstorming headlines.

    ChatGPT gives you what you ask for—a brainstormed list with plenty of creative choices. In contrast, Bard only gives you a portion of what you really want. Then it tries to guess what else you may be interested in.

    This very basic prompt draws out a significant difference between these two platforms.

    Tell ChatGPT what to do and it will do it. Tell Bard what to do—and it will make assumptions about what you want.

    Using Bard is like having an intern who does things you don't really want or need.

    Plus, Bard is horrible at handling in-depth training command prompts. ChatGPT, on the other hand, can handle complex instructions. This makes ChatGPT great for writing with a framework that you can use over and over again.

    What you can do in a single prompt with ChapGPT, will take multiple prompts with Bard.

    Bard prefers conversational questions and tasks to get better results. It’s an intern who needs directions, but needs them one at a time. Your job with Bard is to coach it—not command it. It takes patience, but with a few iterations you will get the hang of it.

    For example, here’s our Endless Idea Generation prompt rewritten for Bard.

    I need some help generating ideas for beginning writers who want to build a daily writing habit.

Can you help me create 5 ideas for each of these 4 different categories of ideas in the form of a headline?

Here are the categories:

1. Aspirational Ideas (Here's how to do something). For example, step-by-step guides, or practical tips and techniques etc.

2. Analytical Ideas (Here's a breakdown of something works). For example, key reasons behind phenomena, or compelling examples and case studies.

3. Aspirational Ideas (I'm going to show you. You can do it too!): For example, valuable lessons learned from experience, or mistakes to avoid for better outcomes.

4. Anthropological Ideas (Here’s the reason things happen): For example, common fears and strategies to overcome them, or failures and the lessons they teach.

    3 things to keep in mind when prompting Bard:

    • Specificity still wins.

    • Use examples to improve directions.

    • Prompt Bard as if you were writing an email.

    There is a reason the creators describe the tool with the word “chat.”

    Bard is a capable intern, but ChatGPT will get you better results faster.

    What does Bard offer Digital Writers that ChatGPT doesn’t?

    It includes internet browsing capability for free—that’s about it.

    But even internet access isn’t “powerful” yet.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!aFu7!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcc704081-6757-4f6c-b65f-47f50b18985a_1748x306.png)

    You can, however, perform other “Google’y” things which makes for a nice user experience.

    • It’s fast. You get an instantaneous response without having to wait or watch the AI write.

    • You can export text directly into a Google Doc.

    • You can export text directly an email (perfect for email marketers).

    • You can jump straight into a pre-populated Google search if you aren’t happy with your “chat,” which is admittedly a nice feature when researching.

    • And you can see a few different chat results with immediately after you prompt Bard, without having go click “Regenerate” like you do using ChatGPT.

    Bard also does a nice job of formatting. It’s breaks up long paragraphs of text, and uses bullets—making it easier to read than ChatGPT. And the tone sounds less like a robot.

    So, where does that leave us?

    Where should you Write With AI?

    ChatGPT / GPT-4 is the winner for Digital Writers.

    ChatGPT is the Category King.

    ChatGPT is a jack-of-all-trades, while Bard provides more specialized assistance. And no matter what Bard does, it will be compared to ChatGPT. Don’t get us wrong. Both platforms are incredibly helpful, the best one really depends on your personal preference and style.

    • Are you a “frameworker” with a prompt engineering mindset? Lean into ChatGPT.

    • Or are you “chatter” who prefers to iterate into what you want? Lean into Bard.

    But don’t take our word for it, decide for yourself.
