    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Many writers struggle to know who they’re writing for (and why).

    And when it comes to idea generation, most people start with big, broad topics.

    • Meditation

    • Real Estate

    • Productivity

    • etc.

    These are giant categories.

    It’s one of the biggest mistakes writers make when it comes to deciding what to write. Saying you want to write about “productivity” is like saying you want to write about “life.” There are a million things you could write about!

    So, how do you narrow in your thinking?

    You need to get more specific.

    Whether you are Writing With AI or with a pen, start with FOR WHO / SO THAT.

    FOR WHO/SO THAT is a simple foundational framework that we teach in our flagship cohort-based course, Ship 30 for 30.

    Every time you sit down to write, the very first thing you should ask yourself is:

    • WHO am I writing for?

    • SO THAT they can do… what?

    This is how you get specific.

    And with ChatGPT, this is how you can write anything you can imagine:

    • Long-form blog posts

    • Tweets and threads

    • Atomic Essays

    • Email subject lines

    • And even an entire landing page.

    This framework will literally save you hundreds hours of writing in circles.

    Let’s dive in!

    First, WHO are you writing for? List out all the potential audiences.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!DOeF!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F654dbd8f-6eb9-4fa0-ac4c-47f12dda9df5_1084x108.png)

    For example, let’s say you want to write about “investing.”

    • Investing Tips For Ambitious Middle Schoolers

    • Investing Tips For Startup Founders

    • Investing Tips For Retired Moms

    Every time you change the WHO, the topic itself doesn’t change but the way you explain that topic most likely does.

    Different audiences require different context, different action steps, and maybe even different language. For example, you wouldn’t explain “investing tips” to ambitious middle schoolers the same way you would explain “investing tips” to startup founders.

    So, whenever you sit down to write, at the top of the page write your WHO.

    This will give you clarity as to WHO exactly you are writing for.

    Second, WHY are you writing for them? SO THAT they can do what?

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!VrCw!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F61a3accd-75bd-41c6-9ffb-da6af5177be3_1062x106.png)

    • Investing Tips For College Students To Graduate Without Any Debt

    • Investing Tips For College Students To Save Enough Money For Your First Car

    • Investing Tips For College Students To Set You Up For Retirement By Age 65

    Again, notice how even if you keep the audience the same, the SO THAT outcome drastically changes what you are planning to write about. “Investing to graduate without any debt” is very different than “investing to set you up for retirement by age 65.” These are different pieces.

    So, whenever you sit down to write, at the top of the page after you write your WHO, write your SO THAT as well.

    You are writing for this specific reader SO THAT they can do… what?

    Now it’s your turn—pick a topic you want to write about!

    Ask ChatGPT for ideas on WHO, specifically, you could write for.

    I am going to train you on the "FOR WHO / SO THAT" framework.

First, I will give you a topic. 

Afer you have the topic, you will write 7 FOR WHOs (audiences) who would be most likely to be interested in the topic.

For example if I give you the topic "Investing" you would return something like this:

Topic = Investing
FOR WHO (Audience) = 
- For Single Moms
- For Retired Men
- For College Students
- For Startup Founders
- For Crypto Enthusiasts
- For Ambitious Middle Schoolers

Then you will ask me what FOR WHO I would like to continue with.

I will pick a FOR WHO and you will give me 7 SO THATs (outcomes) for the audience.

For example, if I gave you the topic "Investing" and then chose "For College Students" you would return something like this:

Topic = Investing 
FOR WHO (Audience) = For College Students
SO THAT (Outcome) = 
- To Graduate Without Any Debt
- To Save Enough Money For Your First Car
- To Set You Up For Retirement By Age 65
- etc...

Finally, you will ask me what SO THAT I would like to choose.

I will pick a SO THAT and you will return the Topic,  FOR WHO, and SO THAT.

For example if I give you the topic "Investing," the FOR WHO "For College Students," and SO THAT "To Graduate Without Any Debt" you would return something like this:

Topic = Investing 
FOR WHO (Audience) = For College Students
SO THAT (Outcome) = To Graduate Without Any Debt

A few rules for the Outcomes:
- Rule #1: Use visceral TANGIBLE language
- Rule #2: Be super specific (specificity clarifies what kind, what happens when/if, what does it mean, so that you can what)
      VAGUE: Gain financial freedom
        SPECIFIC: Overcome bad spending habits and build positive ones so you can start investing in stocks, real estate, and digital property this month.

Do you understand?

    Pick an audience (WHO) and ChatGPT will help you come up with what you’re going to help them do (SO THAT).

    After you run the run the prompt, ChatGPT will return a list of 7 potential audiences (WHO) for you to choose from.

    Choose an outcome (SO THAT) and use ChatGPT to turn your FOR WHO / SO THAT into any Digital Asset you want!

    Now that you have clarity on WHO you are writing for and WHY, you can write anything you can imagine using ChatGPT.

    • Generate endless ideas on auto-pilot

    • Write click-worthy email subject lines

    • Or, build a high-converting, publish ready landing page from scratch

    This framework will save you hours of writing in circles, trying to figure out who you’re writing for and why.

    It doesn’t get any easier than this folks. Give it a try and let us know how it goes!
