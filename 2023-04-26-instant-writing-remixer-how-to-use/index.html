Ahoy, Digital Writers!

Today I am going to walk you through a framework for using ChatGPT as your personal “writing remixer.”

If you’re anything like me, it’s easy to feel “stuck” in a certain pattern of writing. So sometimes it’s helpful to switch things up and try out different tones, styles, and voices. But rather than try and “create” these yourself, we’re going to employ our digital intern ChatGPT who can instantly do this for us.

To start, here’s a prompt to help do this that will “blow your mind.”

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!RH7c!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F11917d67-8354-4f13-88fb-ac649408c2b2_1224x682.png)

Just kidding.

This isn’t your typical garbage ChatGPT Twitter Thread that generates elementary school output.

This is Write With AI—and that means we craft detailed, step-by-step instructional prompts to generate high-quality output.

So with that in mind, let’s dive into the real prompt.

_Two important notes before we dive in here:

First, you can have ChatGPT rewrite entire paragraphs, newsletters, and even entire books. But for the purpose of this example, I am going to rewrite just one sentence so you can see all the different voices at once.

Second, you can choose more than just the options I lay out in the below examples. And you can use ChatGPT to generate those as well. Simply ask it for more ways to rewrite it with different tones/authors/goals/demographics, and use the ways it gives you._

1. Rewrite your writing with different “tones”

First, we’re going to take a sentence and rewrite it in 6 different “tones.”

I tend to write with a matter-of-fact, straightforward writing tone. So using these rewrites helps me tap into new writing voices, some of which I can borrow to enhance my personal writing voice.

Here’s the prompt I am using to train it:

You are my personal "writing remixer." 

I am going to give you a sentence. Then you are going to rewrite it in 6 different "tones." 

- More formal 
- More serious 
- More sarcastic 
- More optimistic
- More pessimistic
- More lighthearted 

Here are the steps:
1. You will ask me for the sentence. 
2. Then you will rewrite the sentence in each tone. 
3. And finally you will store the output in a table. 

Here is the table output:
- Column headers: tone, rewritten sentence
- Rows: each of the tones I listed
- The first row should be the original sentence. 
- The “tone” for that one should be “Original sentence” 

Are you ready for the sentence?

And here is the output, generated instantly by our genius intern.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Ucdr!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F650dae99-a377-4e5a-b6d3-5edc6ce9eb53_1174x1232.png)

Same sentence, same core ideas, but now I can “see” different ways of expressing it.

For example, I like the word “potent” from the formal row and the word “transformative” from the optimistic row. It would have taken me a while to come up with each of these variations on my own, probably 10-15 minutes at least. But ChatGPT does it on demand, which frees up our time to continue writing, which ultimately leads to more output.

Note: asking ChatGPT to give you output as a “table” is a little-known hack when you want to compare the output across different versions of a variable. The only thing to keep in mind is it usually requires detailed instructions for the precise output you want, which is why I give it line-by-line instructions for the table output.

2. Rewriting your writing as different authors

Next, we’re going to rewrite it in the voice of 6 different authors.

This one is my personal favorite. Each author has their own secret sauce that makes their writing voice unique, something you can’t easily describe to ChatGPT. But once again, it gives you a 360-degree writing voice perspective you can pull bits and pieces from to enhance your own writing style.

Here’s the prompt I’m using to train it:

You are my personal "writing remixer." 

I am going to give you a sentence. Then you are going to rewrite it in 6 different "authors" 

1. Gary Vaynerchuk
2. Maya Angelou
3. George R.R. Martin
4. Winston Churchill
5. Brene Brown
6. J.K. Rowling
7. Ernest Hemingway

Here are the steps:
1. You will ask me for the sentence. 
2. Then you will rewrite the sentence in each tone. 
3. And finally you will store the output in a table. 

Here is the table output:
- Column headers: tone, rewritten sentence
- Rows: each of the authors I listed
- The first row should be the original sentence. 
- The “author” for that one should be “Original sentence” 

Are you ready for the sentence?

3. Rewrite your writing with different “goals”

Now, we’ve knocked out “tones” and authors. Let’s move on to “goals.”

You are my personal "writing remixer." 

I am going to give you a sentence. Then you are going to rewrite it in 6 different "goals." 

1. More concise
2. More emphatic
3. More humorous
4. More descriptive
5. More persuasive
6. More informative
7. More action-oriented

Here are the steps:
1. You will ask me for the sentence. 
2. Then you will rewrite the sentence in each tone. 
3. And finally you will store the output in a table. 

Here is the table output:
- Column headers: tone, rewritten sentence
- Rows: each of the authors I listed
- The first row should be the original sentence. 
- The “author” for that one should be “Original sentence” 

Are you ready for the sentence?

This one is great for writing emails or website copy. Figure out the ideal voice that resonates with your target market, then ask ChatGPT to rewrite certain sections with that goal in mind.

4. Rewrite your writing for different demographics

And speaking of demographics, you don’t even have to figure out the ideal voice yourself. Simply nail the demographic you’re targeting, and then ask ChatGPT to rewrite it specifically for them.

Here’s the last and final prompt:

You are my personal "writing remixer." 

I am going to give you a sentence. Then you are going to rewrite it in 6 different "demographics." 

1. For 3rd graders
2. For 8th graders
3. For a college student
4. For a busy 40-year-old mom
5. For an 80-year-old who reads slowly
6. For an audience where English is a second language

Here are the steps:
1. You will ask me for the sentence. 
2. Then you will rewrite the sentence in each tone. 
3. And finally you will store the output in a table. 

Here is the table output:
- Column headers: tone, rewritten sentence
- Rows: each of the authors I listed
- The first row should be the original sentence. 
- The “demographic” for that one should be “Original sentence” 

Are you ready for the sentence?

And these examples are just the tip of the iceberg.

I encourage you to take these prompts and look for different ways to both apply them and improve them.

Because remember, this is where your time is best spent as a Digital Writer. In the past, you would have had to come up with the idea to rewrite something, then do the actual rewriting yourself. Now, you spend the same 10% of the time coming up with the rewrite idea, but then save that other 90% of the time by training ChatGPT to do it for you, instantly.
