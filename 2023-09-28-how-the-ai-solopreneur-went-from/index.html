Ahoy, Digital Writers!

We’re coming up on our 6-month anniversary here at Write With AI (can you believe it???).

In that time, we have not only attracted over 10,000 Digital Writers interested in leveraging AI (congrats—you’re living in the future!), but we have also built a library of AI-powered Digital Writing prompts that continues to help more and more writers every day.

So, if you’ve been a reader of ours for a while now, first of all… THANK YOU.

And second, we would love to hear from you.

Please leave a comment on this post and let us know:

  • Which ChatGPT prompt or framework/idea have you found most helpful so far?

  • And if you could request a ChatGPT prompt or framework for a problem you’re running into in your writing (or your business), what would it be? (We’ll make it for you!)

Now, with that in mind…

Having ChatGPT prompts and AI frameworks for specific problems is great—and we plan on creating many, many more.

But one of the most common questions we get asked (and it’s a big one) is:

“How do I create an entire content strategy & system using AI?”

Good question!

And since we didn’t have a great answer to this question, we went out and found the person who did: The AI Solopreneur.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!iB2_!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc3a9f9b3-f431-4507-946e-fd006450efbd_2562x1810.png)

If you’re not familiar, The AI Solopreneur is one of our favorite creators on Twitter/X these days.

And he’s also been one of the fastest-growing new accounts on Twitter/X over the past year. Clearly, his content resonates with a lot of people! (The AI Solopreneur is an alias account run by Ole Lehmann.)

As far as we know, Ole is the first (and one of the only) creators to put together an entire content strategy & system with AI at the center—and as much as we love manually creating our own content, we have to say… this is pretty cool.

And for credibility: this system is also how Ole has been able to:

  • Go from 0 to 100k+ on Twitter/X in record time (65 days!).

  • Launch a digital product that has received rave reviews.

  • And build a digital education business to $250k+ in <4 months.

Insane!

And, a lot to be learned from Ole & his AI Solopreneur account.

So, we reached out to Ole and asked if we could partner up and share his course with our loyal readers here at Write With AI.
