The number one mistake people make online is that they don't confirm their product or business ideas.

Why?

Because most people don't know how to study a market...

...Or find demand.

If you want to start a business, build a product, or even do something as simple as write a LinkedIn post, you need to attract the attention of your target audience.

Which means you need to find problems people have.

When you describe a pain someone knows they have—but haven’t solved—they experience mental discomfort. Their brain wants to resolve the gap between “what is” and “what should be.”

So when you provide a specific solution to that problem...

...Boom you've got a click, a reader, a buyer, or a brand new business.

But where do you start?

Research!

Now, you might be thinking…

“Boring! I don’t have time to scroll through mounds of Google search results, expand 1,000 Reddit threads to find one ‘golden nugget’, or spend 2 days filtering out all the Amazon reviews in search of the Holy Grail of problems.”

I’m with you.

It's easy to get lost in research when you have the entire internet at your fingertips.

But all great marketing starts with research.

You can’t avoid it.

Which is why I’m excited to bring the you the Mad Scientist Research Library.

With this library, you don’t have to waste hours throwing-darts blind folded at the internet.

You’ll be able to find laser-focused insights in half the time.

Inside this library you'll find prompts to help you:

  • Uncover profitable niches effortlessly

  • Pinpoint what your audience craves

  • Validate offers before you build them

  • Write copy that turns attention into revenue

It's a collection of battle-tested AI prompts that turn your research from scattered chaos into strategic gold mining.
