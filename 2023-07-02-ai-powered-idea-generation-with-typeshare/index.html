    One of the toughest aspects of becoming a prolific writer is constantly coming up with fresh ideas.

    Finding new angles to write on daily is far from easy. Even with AI powered writing tools like ChatGPT you are forced to start with a blank page and a blinking cursor. You have to know what to “input” to get the “output” you want. It’s one of the main reasons we started Write With AI—to help you create better inputs for your Digital Intern.

    And now with the latest version of Typeshare you can generate writing ideas on autopilot.

    Let’s take a look!

    3 Steps To Generate Ideas For Any Topic With Typeshare

    It’s super simple to get started.

    You can test drive the Idea Generation feature right from the homepage by clicking on the “Generate Ideas” button. Or you can create your FREE account, log in, and click the “Generate Ideas” button from your Typeshare homepage.

    Once you click the button, it’s time for you to decide what you want to write about.

    Step 1: Decide What You Want To Write About

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ju-R!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F526bc82f-794c-4702-908a-c5345fa4534f_946x428.png)

    Enter a topic or take a suggestion from Typeshare.

    • It can be broad.

    • It can be specific.

    The more you use Idea Generator, the move valuable it becomes.

    Every action you take helps Typeshare learn what you like to write about. Typeshare will recommend specific topics just for you based on what you’ve written in the past. If you are just beginning this can help you determine your niche and the topics you enjoy the most.

    It’s a good Digital Intern!

    Step 2: Generate Your First Idea With The AI Powered Idea Generator

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-mK3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F62ae4598-cbd5-4334-a967-fc3d69f37e03_940x676.png)

    Mash the “Generate Ideas” button.

    Typeshare will create 1 idea for you at a time.

    • Like what you see? Add it to your Idea File.

    • Don’t like what your intern served up? Generate a new idea.

    • Feel inspired by an idea? Start writing immediately.

    • Not feeling it? Create a new topic.

    You are the idea orchestrator.

    Step 3: Pick A Digital Format And Start Writing

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!7_K8!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8903a0d9-3941-45ba-8f64-ce21e0282e5e_2420x1114.png)

    • Fill up your Idea File with fresh ideas.

    • When you are ready to write, pick an idea.

    • Then pick a format, Twitter thread, Atomic Essay, etc.

    And in one click, you can immediately start writing!
