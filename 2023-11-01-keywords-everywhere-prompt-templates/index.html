Ahoy, Digital Writers!

Today, we want to share a helpful (and free) tool you can use as a part of your ChatGPT writing workflow.

If you’ve been around the internet for awhile or spent any time learning about SEO, then you’ve probably heard of Keywords Everywhere. Keywords Everywhere is a browser plug-in that allows you to do keyword research directly from Google’s search results. It’s perfect for doing SEO, content marketing, competitor research, and social media marketing right as you browse the web.

And with the plug-in you can access dozens of prompt templates—inside ChatGPT.

  • Prompts for SEO

  • Prompts for marketing

  • Prompts for copywriting

  • Prompts for social media

  • Prompts for productivity

All you have to do is choose a topic and Keywords Everywhere will do the rest.

Let’s dive in!

Why Use Keywords Everywhere Prompt Templates

The benefit of using this tool is not the quality of the output, but in learning how to use ChatGPT for content creation workflow.

Everything from:

  • Creating an outline

  • To expanding an outline into a post

  • To optimize a post for on-page SEO

  • To leverage proven copywriting frameworks

  • To rewriting and expand individual content sections

The prompt generator is a basic framework for learning how to improve your own prompt writing and content production process.

How To Use Keywords Everywhere For ChatGPT

Head over to Keywords Everywhere and install the plug-in for your preferred browser.

Once you have the extension installed, refresh ChatGPT. After you refresh, you will see a “Templates” button in the lower lefthand corner and a “Continue” button in the upper right hand corner. You will be using both of these during the writing process.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!2bBx!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F271d516e-8bcd-43c9-b1df-2a106a581279_2856x1402.png)

Click the “Templates” button to access the prompt templates.

Here you will find all the different options you can choose from to create your prompt. Each category breaks down into a sub-category, and then further into a list of specific prompts you can choose to create and run. Once you’ve made your selections, you will be presented with a screen to add your inputs.

For example, to write a blog post outline you would fill in the the title, and then choose the tone and style you want to write in.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!LBr4!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1d537a8a-d168-455b-a26e-af448c3a972e_1600x1016.png)

After you've picked your options and typed in your topic, you'll see a text prompt at the bottom of the popup. This is the prompt that Keywords Everywhere sends to ChatGPT. When you click "Execute Template" the prompt is run from ChatGPT.

From here you can refine the outputs using the “Continue” button with options like “Expand, Explain, or Rewrite.”

Steal The Keywords Everywhere Prompt Template To Improve Your Own Prompt Writing

Let’s take a look at the structure of a Keywords Everywhere prompt to see what we can learn.

Example prompt for generating a blog outline:

Please ignore all previous instructions. You are an expert copywriter who creates content outlines. You have a Friendly tone of voice. You have a Conversational writing style. Create a long form content outline in the english language for the blog post titled "Sacred Hours: How to Make Time for Work That Matters". The content outline should include a minimum of 3 headings and subheadings. The outline should be extensive and it should cover the entire topic. Create detailed subheadings that are engaging and catchy. Do not write the blog post, please only write the outline of the blog post. Please do not number the headings. Please add a newline space between headings and subheadings. Do not self reference. Do not explain what you are doing.

A few things to notice here as you read through the prompt.

  1. Hard to read. These prompts are one big blob of text, which makes them challenging to understand as a human. Even though ChatGPT is a machine, we can help it to understand what we want by using clear labels and some white space.

  2. Ignore previous instructions. ChatGPT remembers everything you talk about in a single chat—unless you tell it to ignore previous instructions. This is a helpful prompt to start fresh in a single session without creating hundreds of new chats. But consider removing this portion of the prompt if you are running multiple prompts to accomplish one goal.

  3. Act as an expert. Direct ChatGPT to be a knowledgable resource for the specific subject you want to know about. “You are an expert copywriter, marketer, fiction writer, etc.”

  4. Explicit do’s and don’t’s. Any decision you would make as a writer, you need to tell ChatGPT. Otherwise ChatGPT will make the decision for you. What topic do you want to write about? How many points do you want to make? What tone and style do you want to write in? How would you like the output to be formatted?

The key to prompt writing is thinking in constraints.

Everything you prompt ChatGPT with helps it to niche down on the universe of data it knows about. The more specific you are with your prompt, the better your output will be.

Reusable Keywords Everywhere template:

Optional: Please ignore all previous instructions.

Act as a {Type} of {Role} with {experience}.
- You have a {Tone} tone of voice.
- You have a {Style} writing style.

I want you to {what you want ChatGPT to create for you}.

Please do the following:
- List of rules to follow

Please avoid the following:
- List of rules to avoid

And here are a few rules we’ve found helpful for better outputs.

Things to do:

Please do the following:

- Use imperatives
- Vary sentence length
- Readability grade X or lower.
- Use 12 words maximum per sentence.
- Make your takeaways implicit and impactful.
- BE SPECIFIC. Use the most descriptive nouns and verbs possible.

Things to avoid:

Please avoid the following:

- Avoid jargon
- Avoid questions
- Avoid analogies
- Avoid enthusiasm!
- Avoid being salesy
- Avoid using terms like {List of terms}
- Avoid obvious, cliche, and overused phrases.
