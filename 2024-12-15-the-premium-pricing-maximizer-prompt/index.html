The Premium Pricing Maximizer Prompt

Author: Nicolas Cole
Date: 2024-12-15

This should be every writer's pricing strategy:

Avoid the middle.

You should either be very cheap or very expensive relative to the tier that you're in depending on what product or service you're selling.

Unfortunately, most writers follow one of two flawed strategies:

  1. They either pluck a random number from thin air

  2. Or they look at their competitors' prices and undercut them slightly.

Both of these are really really bad ways to think about pricing.

In this video, I'll walk you through the different business models that work best for writers, how to determine your optimal pricing strategy, and why your choice of business model has a bigger impact on your earning potential than almost any other factor.

Whether you're a freelance writer, editor, or content creator, understanding these principles could transform how you think about monetizing your expertise.

Now, to help you put this into action (and charge more for your products/services), I put together the Premium Pricing Maximizer Prompt to help!

This prompt uses these 4 pricing rules:

  1. Make your service the most valuable for a no-brainer price. How can I create an irresistible offer by bundling value-added services and anchoring the price to something my client values (e.g., time or money)?

  2. You can either lower the price or increase the value. If my client objects to the price, what additional services could I include to increase perceived value without adding too much extra effort?

  3. Create two offers to force a decision from the client. What are two potential packages I could create at different price points to give the client a clear choice?

  4. More volume = price break. If the client wants to add more services, how can I offer a discount for bundling while ensuring it’s still profitable for me?

All you need to do is, replace the square brackets with your:

  • Service

  • Target Client

  • And the outcome your service delivers

And AI will help you come up with some different ways you can improve the pricing and packaging of your offer.

Here’s the prompt:

I provide [insert service] that delivers [insert outcome] for [target client]. 

Using the 4 pricing rules below, suggest ways I can charge more for this service while ensuring it’s a no-brainer for the client:

1. Make your service the most valuable for a no-brainer price: How can I create an irresistible offer by bundling value-added services and anchoring the price to something my client values (e.g., time or money)?

2. You can either lower the price or increase the value: If my client objects to the price, what additional services could I include to increase perceived value without adding too much extra effort?

3. Create two offers to force a decision from the client: What are two potential packages I could create at different price points to give the client a clear choice?

4.More volume = price break: If the client wants to add more services, how can I offer a discount for bundling while ensuring it’s still profitable for me?

Please provide detailed suggestions based on the service, outcome, and client type I’ve described.
