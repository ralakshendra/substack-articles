    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Today, we are going to use ChatGPT to create an outline for an Educational Email Course to help you start building a bridge to your newsletter—and eventually convert subscribers into paying customers.

    If you are a creator or entrepreneur, you know you live and die by the strength of your email list.

    It’s your “pool” of prospective customers.

    And to fill the pool, you need to get people on the list!

    There’s just one problem.

    Less than 10% of people who visit your website actually type in their email.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!WHyP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F62f4fb33-f75d-40a1-9ae4-ac7a43f3e1c9_511x410.png)

    Think about your own behavior online.

    Do you give your email to someone if you don’t know what you'll get in return? Probably not. Your audience is no different. They need a compelling reason to let you into their inbox.

    And even if a reader trades their email for your LinkedIn growth checklist, your Ultimate Guide To Remote Work eBook, or your free-guide to intermittent fasting—it’s likely to sit for months and even years, before the reader thinks, “Oh, yeah, what happened to the social media growth guide I signed up for?” The chances of your free download getting buried in the reader’s inbox or opened in a “new tab” and then forgotten about is extremely high. But hey, at least you sent them something for typing in their email!

    Do you want to know what our opt-in rate is for our Educational Email Course at www.startwritingonline.com is? It’s 70%.

    That’s unheard of.

    Educational Email Courses lead to the highest opt-in rate of any email offer.

    Why?

    Because people WANT to be educated on the Internet, especially before they buy anything. Which means the most effective way to get someone onto your email list & prime them to become a customer one day isn’t to give them a discount, a free eBook, or a package of templates.

    It’s to give them FREE EDUCATION.

    When you can solve a painful problem, or fulfill a desirable outcome for your reader in bite-size increments over the course of a few days two things happen: 1) you build trust and 2) you stand-out as a genuinely helpful person on the internet!

    And guess what?

    People open up their wallet for people they know, like, and trust.

    You’re not just sending emails—you’re building a revenue-generating asset.

    • You are educating the reader on a problem they have.

    • You are reminding them of the benefits of solving that problem (”Here’s why solving this problem is worth it to you”)

    • And then you are giving them “the bridge”/the method to get there: Educational Email Courses.

    It’s the single greatest opportunity as a creator/entrepreneur to build a highly engaged audience and extremely loyal audience on the internet today.

    Let’s take a look at one of the most versatile Educational Email Courses you can create.

    The 5 Day Educational Email “Crash” Course Outline

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!CIUt!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F06f49e22-0243-4c69-b5f4-f944258455e2_2118x1400.png)

    The “Crash Course” is a here’s-everything-you-need-to-know about a certain topic in 5 days.

    Technically, “anything” organized into 5 segments can be an educational email course. But this type of email course is great for beginners in your audience who “don’t know what they don’t know” and are looking for a way to get started.

    And you can make just about anything work using this structure.

    “Crash Course” Template Outline:

    • Day 0: Introduction & Email Course Outline - “Here’s what you can expect”

    • Day 1: X Most Important Things To Know

    • Day 2: X Mistakes Most People Make

    • Day 3: X Steps To Go From Zero to 1

    • Day 4: X Simple Upgrades To Accelerate Growth

    • Day 5: X Best Practices Moving Forward

    How To Use ChatGPT To Speed Up The Outlining Process

    Now, can you use ChatGPT to speed up the writing & outlining process?

    Absolutely!

    (In fact, using ChatGPT is how you’ll be able to create outlines without it taking you more than 5 or 10 minutes.)

    Here’s how:

    Step 1: “Think” about what’s most valuable for the reader.

    It’s your job to “think” and make a very clear offer to the reader as to what they’re going to “get” by typing in their email and giving you their attention.

    • What problems do they have? (e.g. don’t know where to start)

    • What outcomes do they desire? (e.g. getting followers)

    • What are their biggest questions? (e.g. what should I write about?)

    • What platforms or tools do they want to learn more about? (e.g. Typeshare, Twemex, etc.)

    Spend 3 minutes thinking about each question for the audience you serve. Then complete this simple template for your topic:

    “How to get {outcome} without {problem}”

    Once you have your topic, move on to the next step.

    Step 2: Train ChatGPT on the Educational Email “Crash” Course

    Copy/paste this script inside ChatGPT:

    I am going to train you to create 5-Day Educational Email Crash Course outline.

The "Crash Course" is everything you need to know to get started on a topic.

For example: here’s a template for a 5-day email “Crash Course"

- **Day 0: Introduction & Email Course Outline - “Here’s what you can expect”**
    - Introduction to the email course and what participants can expect to learn.
    - Overview of the 5 things that will be covered in the course.
- **Day 1: 3 Most Important Things To Know**
    - {Big Point Of View}
    - {Actionable Tip}
    - {The 1 Secret To Success}
- **Day 2: 3 Mistakes Most People Make**
    - {Mistake 1}
    - {Mistake 2}
    - {Mistake 3}
- **Day 3: Steps To Go From Zero to 1**
    - {Step 1}
    - {Step 2}
    - {Step 3}
- **Day 4: 3 Simple Upgrades To Accelerate Growth**
    - {Actionable Tip 1}
    - {Actionable Tip 2}
    - {Actionable Tip 3}
- **Day 5: 3 Best Practices Moving Forward**
    - {Best Practice 1}
    - {Best Practice 2}
    - {Best Practice 3}

I will give you a topic and some additional instructions, then you will use the template to create a Crash Course outline.

Are you ready?

    Step 3: Give ChatGPT a topic.

    For example:

    • “How to get 10K followers in 90 days with short-form content on X”

    • “How to get started as a Day Trader with $100 and 2X your money in 10 days ”

    • “How to get started writing on LinkedIn to land your next gig (without tipping off your boss)”

    Focus on the problems, benefits, and outcomes you are solving / creating for the reader.

    Feed your topic to ChatGPT, and ask it to create a similar outline.

    
Please follow the Crash Course template and create an outline for the topic: 

[INSERT TOPIC]

Please provide succinct, imperative, and action-oriented phrases or statements. (Use 8-12 words) 

Each phrase should convey a clear and direct piece of advice or instruction, related to {Topic}.

Use colloquial language.

Avoid jargon.

    Check out what ChatGPT came back with using that prompt!

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!UxnU!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe5d895c6-ce40-4603-a184-e4c45f5dea88_972x1144.png)

    Note: ChatGPT has a bad habit of labeling everything. If it does, instruct it to “please remove the labels and don’t change anything else!”

    Boom!

    Now you’re ready to start writing!

    Fill in your outline. Set up an email sequence in your favorite email provider (we recommend ConvertKit). Draft up a simple landing page using a tool like Carrd. Post the link to your social profile and start directing traffic to it—on auto-pilot.

    Easy!

    Readers will happily part with their email address and eventually their $$$ because you are giving away FREE education.
