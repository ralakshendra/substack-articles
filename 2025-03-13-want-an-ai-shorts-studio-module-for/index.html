"Is AI Shorts Studio connected to the monthly Write With AI subscription I already pay for?"

So…

If you’re an annual subscriber, you’ll need to upgrade to a Founding Member annual plan to get access to AI Shorts Studio.

Here’s a quick copywriting tip:

Before you hit publish, ask yourself—“Did I answer ALL of my reader’s questions?” It’ll save you (and your audience) a lot of back and forth.

Your job as a writer is to anticipate and answer every question your reader has. When you don’t, you create confusion, extra support emails, and even lost sales. I’ve been doing this for 10+ years, and I still don’t get it 100% right.

Now, here’s something else I realized…

Reminder: AI Shorts Studio Will Roll Out Over Several Weeks Starting March 17th

This won’t be one of those "drop everything at once" courses.

Instead, we’ll be releasing each module over several weeks.

Because cramming everything into one sitting doesn’t actually help you create videos.

Spacing it out gives you time to apply each step, test things out, and ask questions along the way.

That being said, I may reconsider the order based on how you vote.
