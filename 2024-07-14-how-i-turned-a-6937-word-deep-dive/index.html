Hey there, Digital Writers!

Over the past 2 years, I've been on a mission to crack the code of what makes a truly great book title.

Not just a "good" title or a "clever" title. But a title that carves out a sliver of real estate in the reader's mind, creates word-of-mouth momentum, and positions a book for maximum success.

I’ve easily logged more than 300 hours of my life into thinking about this topic. I've written about it in different ways, talked about it endlessly, stress-tested it against countless examples, and used it myself in my own work. It's been a labor of love, fueled by my belief that a great title can make or break a book's success.

Here’s my latest post for reference (all 6,937 words):

[

](https://artandbiz.substack.com/p/the-perfect-book-title)

Now here's the thing about frameworks: they're only as good as their application.

That's why I believe in turning frameworks into prompts. And that's exactly what I did with my Perfect Book Title framework.

The Easy Way To Turn Any Framework Into A Prompt

I took my deep dive, my years of research and refinement, and asked AI to transform it into a reusable prompt. A prompt that could generate amazing book title outputs, time and time again.

Here's how I went about it, and how you can think about creating prompts for yourself—whether it's for your own frameworks or for other deep dives you find from writers and creators you admire.

Step 1: Feed AI Your Framework

I simply fed Claude my post "The Perfect Book Title."

First, I primed AI with what to expect.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!tMc3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F26be40af-8f1d-4458-8516-f70a06fec4f1_924x194.png)

Then I copy/pasted my entire post in the chat.

This post contained all the nuances, all the details, all the hard-earned insights I'd gathered over those two years.

And now that AI has the framework. AI can work with it.

Step 2: Prompt AI To Create A Prompt

Then, I prompted Claude like this:

Act as a prompt engineer and turn my book title framework into a meticulously crafted prompt that generates amazing outputs for book titles.

It’s prompt inception.

But voila, it did the job. Here's the prompt it created:

You are an expert book title generator with deep knowledge of publishing trends and marketing psychology. Your task is to create compelling, market-ready book titles that capture attention and drive sales.

Follow these guidelines to craft the perfect title:

1. Use a Title-First approach, considering the book's positioning before content.

2. Choose one of these combinations, favoring the first two:
   a) Non-Obvious Main Title x Obvious Subtitle
   b) Obvious Main Title x Non-Obvious Subtitle
   c) Obvious Main Title x Obvious Subtitle

3. Ensure the book fits one of these 7 best-selling non-fiction mega-categories:
   - Personal Development
   - Personal Finance
   - Insights/Thinking
   - Leadership
   - Case Study/Allegory
   - Functional Excellence
   - Relationships

4. Decide if the book leans on Personal Credibility or Borrowed Credibility.

5. Determine if the book aims to be Timely or Timeless.

6. Create differentiation while being both Specific & Universal.

7. Employ one or more of these languaging techniques:
   - Pun Languaging
   - Category Languaging
   - Trend-Jacking Languaging
   - Portmanteau Languaging
   - Acronym Languaging

8. Choose between Implicit or Explicit audience naming, considering the book's niche vs. mainstream potential.

9. Use Tangible language over Intangible language. Make outcomes feel concrete and measurable.

10. Promise Objective Outcomes rather than Subjective Outcomes.

11. Ensure the title creates a clear "sliver of real estate" in the reader's mind, solving either:
    a) The same problem with a different solution
    b) A different problem altogether

12. Craft language that is memorable, repeatable, and likely to ignite word-of-mouth marketing.

Generate 5 unique book titles (main title and subtitle) for the following topic: [INSERT TOPIC HERE]

For each title, briefly explain:
- The Obvious/Non-Obvious combination used
- The mega-category it fits
- The languaging technique employed
- How it uses Tangible language or promises Objective Outcomes
- Its unique positioning or "sliver of real estate" in the reader's mind

Step 3: Put Your Prompt To Work

Now, all you have to do is plug in your topic idea, and AI will generate a variety of different book titles for you.

It also provides explanations on the different components I defined as inputs for a good book title.

Let's look at a sample output for the topic “Poodle Training:”

This is a great way to start brainstorming ideas with AI.

Step 4: Iterate And Improve

You could go further and improve the prompt by adding examples to help clarify the concepts and get AI to dial in the results.

As it stands right now, the AI makes some judgment calls about Obvious and Non-Obvious content, which are close but don't always hit the mark. By providing clear examples of Obvious and Non-Obvious content, you can help the AI better understand and apply these concepts.

For instance, you could add:

Examples of Obvious Main Titles: “The Complete Guide to...”, “How to...”

Examples of Non-Obvious Main Titles: “Atomic Habits,” “The Tipping Point'"

You could do the same for each key concept in the framework, providing concrete examples that illustrate the thinking.

This process—of deep diving into a topic, distilling your insights into a framework, and then turning that framework into an AI-powered prompt—is a powerful way to amplify your expertise. It allows you to scale your thinking, to apply your hard-won knowledge in new and exciting ways.

So, what framework will you turn into a prompt?

The possibilities are endless.
