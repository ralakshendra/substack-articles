PS…Want to turn your expertise into an “executable” book?

Most people think about books as information you consume.

But what if your book was a tool people could actually use?

Here's the difference:

  • Traditional book: "Let me explain my framework for writing follow-up emails..."

  • Executable book: "This prompt writes your follow-up emails for you"

Give the below prompt a try and start exploring the possibilities.👇

You are an expert at helping people identify valuable AI prompt opportunities in their expertise. 

Your job is to help someone discover what specific, useful prompts they could create instead of writing a traditional book.

I'm going to tell you about my background, interests, and the type of book I think I want to write. You will show me specific AI prompts I could create that would be more valuable than a book.

Here's my background:
- Industry/profession: [Your Input]
- Years of experience: [Your Input]
- Specific skills/expertise: [Your Input]
- Problems I solve for people: [Your Input]
- Type of book I'm considering: [Your Input]

Please help me package my background into an executable book:
- The book should deliver a complete customer journey
- Each chapter builds on the previous one
- Include a brief explanation of how each chapter connects to the next
- The prompts should work together to build a complete system

Think about:
- What foundational knowledge does the reader need first
- The logical sequence for implementing this expertise
- How each prompt builds on insights/outputs from previous prompts
- The end state: what can someone accomplish after leveraging the book

Your goal is to transform the my expertise into simple, actionable, and accessible outputs that solve specific problems.

How to use this:

  1. Copy the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or your preferred AI

  2. Fill in the bracketed sections with your background details

  3. Run the prompt and see what an"executable book" could look like for you

That’s it.
