Hey there Digital Writers!

In the last year, our team has nearly doubled.

More people means more moving parts—and the more critical it becomes to keep communication tight and get new folks up to speed fast. Otherwise, things can quickly fall through the cracks, and suddenly, instead of speeding up, it takes you twice as long and costs twice as much.

The easiest way we’ve found to avoid those headaches is by creating SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures).

Every time you explain a new task or process—create an SOP.

An SOP keeps everyone on the same page, following the same playbook.

It’s a fancy way of saying “step-by-step instructions” for how to get something done, especially if you’re running a team or working with an assistant. We’ve been creating SOPs for everything from sending follow-up emails to tracking key marketing data. These processes make sure things run smoothly without having to explain every detail repeatedly.

Here’s a peek at a few of the SOPs we’ve put together this year:

[

So, whether you’re working with a VA, a team spread across time zones, or managing a growing client list, creating SOPs will save you time, reduce confusion, and ensure consistency in your business.

The cool thing is, you can create these SOPs using AI without spending hours writing them out. It’s fast, easy, and ensures clear, repeatable processes for everyone.

Let me show you how in 4 easy steps.

Step 1: Record A Loom Video Walking Through Your Task/Process

For example, in the below video, Daniel explains our daily process for tracking visitors, subscribers, and conversions for each of our Educational Email Courses.

Step 3: Prompt ChatGPT/Claude With The Loom Transcript + Task Name

Add your Loom transcript to the SOP prompt and run it.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!NkB-!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F34071833-68c3-47ca-be43-a38fb5457ee8_1147x687.png)

Here’s the prompt:

Hey ChatGPT, I just recorded a Loom video walking my EA through a new recurring task I'd like her help with.

Now, even though the video explains all the details she needs to know in order to complete the task, I'd love to also create a written SOP so she doesn't have to watch the whole video every single time she's running through this process.

So, here's what I'd like your help with:

I'm going to share the transcript of this Loom video I mentioned earlier as well as the "name" of the task I break down in it.

Then, I want you to create a lightweight SOP for this task (based on what I share in the video).

Now, the SOP should have the following sections:

1/ Context & Objective

In this section, we want to include some context so my EA can easily understand why we're doing this task in the first place.

2/ Frequency

In this section, we want to specify how often (and when) the task should be completed as well as any dependencies .

3/ Checklist

In this section, we want to specify the different tools & resources my EA will need to complete this task and break down all the steps involved in completing the task
in an easy-to-follow checklist format.

- - -

The video transcript I'm providing should already contain all the information you need to create the SOP with these 3 sections - but please let me know if you need any additional information.

The name of the task is: 

**{Insert task name}**

And here's the transcript of the video:

**{Insert Loom video transcript}**

What Makes A Good SOP?

Now that you know how to quickly create an SOP, let’s talk about what makes them effective.

A good SOP is clear, concise, and easy to follow. Your goal is to create an SOP that anyone—regardless of experience—can pick up and complete the task successfully.

Here are a few key elements of a great SOP:

  1. Context & Objective: Start with the “why.” Why is this process important? What’s the end goal?

  2. Frequency: How often does the task need to be done? Are there any dependencies or specific times it should be completed?

  3. Checklist: Outline each step in a simple, easy-to-follow checklist. Include any tools or resources needed for the task.

These help your team understand what to do, why it’s important and when it should be done.

By walking through the task on video, you can capture all the details just like you would explain the job in person. Then, when you hand the transcript over to ChatGPT, it can quickly organize it into a clean, professional SOP—without you having to write it from scratch.

And if there’s something unique to your process—like troubleshooting steps or approval workflows—just add that to the ChatGPT prompt. It’s fully customizable and will adapt to whatever you throw at it.

That’s it!

So, next time you find yourself explaining a task—fire up Loom, hit record, grab the transcript, and let AI handle the rest. It’s the fastest way to keep your team aligned and your processes running smoothly.
