The biggest mistake digital writers make with AI?

Expecting AI to replace them completely.

We don’t believe in letting AI take over your writing. Instead, we believe in making AI work for you, so that you can still be you, while getting more done in less time, without sacrificing your voice.

But…

“When you try to do everything on your own—you don’t have time to create everything you want.”

Which is why, we want you want to write smarter.

By training AI to write like you.

So you can avoid the overwhelm that comes with trying to create everything from scratch or editing everything AI writes for you for hours on end.

With our approach, you’ll learn how to maintain your voice while speeding up your content production process.

Here’s how:

The Digital Writer’s Voice Lab uses a proven method to train AI to match your voice, tone, and style.

In this course, we guide you through every step of the process:

  1. Discover Your Voice DNA How to break down your writing into distinct elements that make up your voice.

  2. Train AI With Custom Prompts Get plug-and-play prompts that teach AI to replicate your voice in any format—social posts, articles, emails, and more.

  3. Refine Your Outputs Use our "Mimic and Modify" method to perfect AI drafts without spending hours on rewrites.

By the end of the course, you’ll have AI that writes like you—saving you time and letting you create more content, faster.
