The 1-5-50 Method: How To Turn A Topic Into 5 Proven Twitter Hooks (And Transform Them Into 50 More)

*This is how you serve up tasty hooks in a fraction of the time *

Author: Dickie Bush
Date: 2023-06-07

[

Ahoy, Digital Writers!

Today we are going to train ChatGPT to write 5 different types of proven viral Twitter thread hooks in a single prompt.

Hooks will make or break you on Twitter. And not just on Twitter—any medium. You may have amazing ideas and a fire in your belly to win, but if your hooks fall flat. So, will your reader’s attention.

So, let me ask you…

  • Are your hooks falling flat?

  • Do you get a pit in your stomach just hearing the word “hook?”

  • Are you starting to wonder if you're cut out to be a Digital Writer?

Don't worry, we're here to save you from the paralyzing gaze of the "hook abyss.”

Writing hooks isn't a mystical ability exclusive to "The Chosen Copywriters," who were gifted this power at birth.

It's a skill.

A skill that, with practice, you can improve. And that's exactly why in Ship 30 for 30, we spend an entire week helping students break down and craft punchy, attention-grabbing hooks and headlines.

Let’s dive in!

You can fast-track your mastery of hooks on Twitter with the magic of AI and the power of ChatGPT.

Before we chat with ChatGPT, let’s chat about Twitter threads.

Your goal is to get the reader to click, expand your Twitter Thread, and start reading. And the Lead-In, or first tweet in any thread, is the most important Tweet you need to write. It’s the hook.

You have to get to the point lightning fast.

  • Who is it for?

  • What is it about?

  • Why should they trust you?

  • What will the reader “get” in exchange?

  • After they read, how is their life going to be different?

It’s like a headline and a single-sentence opener combined.

If you aren’t successful at grabbing the reader’s attention in your Lead-In Tweet, then it doesn’t matter how “great” the content is—the reader is going to skip right over your Thread and move onto the next piece of content in their feed.

Readers don't’ read threads. They read to get something in return.

So, let’s give it to them!

5 Twitter thread Lead-In hooks that never fail.

We've trained ChatGPT to write five different types of viral Twitter thread hooks that make it crystal clear who this is for, what it’s about and what the reader can expect.

Here they are:

1. Give me 2 minutes and I’ll show you how to X without Y

A double-whammy hook that preys on the reader’s desire for a quick ROI “2 minutes” and avoid their biggest obstacle “Y.”

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!v4EQ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F59bc7566-1978-49d6-8aad-bb0402411df3_1182x240.png)

2. If you struggle with {problem}, read this.

You better believe, if I have the “problem” I’m going to give this my attention. Calling out the reader’s problem is a never-fail hook.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ymr3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fabcc9d7b-f2e8-466e-be94-b1a8a7cc47b5_1170x930.png)

3. How to X without Y: The ABC framework.

There are two things happening in this hook. One, teaching the reader how to get something they want “X.” And peaking their curiosity with packaged solution, the “ABC framework.”

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Lesa!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9c2b0223-e206-4d80-b82c-d2f2521bff62_1178x274.png)

4. X sentences that will teach you more about [Y] than any [Z].

Here we are calling out the establishment (school, college, a 9-5) and promising a shortcut. Reader’s love shortcuts!

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Nnp1!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F57fb15fe-5183-40f8-9c06-e7cdedac780a_1170x226.png)

5. X free tools that will help you X without Y.

Anytime you can promise an outcome with the pain or problem that a reader identifies and then offer something else, like a tool. The reader will love it—especially because they are FREE! Works every time.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!B9ON!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7e092b8a-0de0-435b-a2de-884d149d7454_1182x304.png)

These hooks will get the reader to stop-scrolling and click to read more.

Ohhhh, Digital Intern, where are you?!

Time to Write With AI: 3 steps to 50 hooks.

We are going to tackle these hooks in 3 quick steps.

Here we go!

Step 1: Grab a subtopic from your Idea File.

For this prompt, you just need a goal-oriented topic (or subtopic) to work with.

  • Developing a running plan

  • Planning a software project

  • Building a daily writing habit

  • Etc.

If you need help, run the Endless Idea Generation prompt for any topic you are interested in writing about to generate ideas on 7 different subtopics.

Pick a subtopic you're excited about.

Step 2: Train ChatGPT to write the 5 Lead-In hooks.

Once you have your subtopic in hand, it’s time to Write With AI!

  • Fire up ChatGPT in your browser.

  • Copy/Paste the below prompt.

  • Mash the “send a message” icon.

  • Wait for your intern to ask for you subtopic.

When you are ready, your hook writing Digital Assistant will be ready to go!

Here’s your hook prompt:

I am going to train you to write viral hooks. 

To start, I am going to show you the different types of hooks. 

Then, you will ask me what subtopic I want to write about. 

When I respond, you will reply with a table with 2 columns:
1. The type of hook template
2. A hook using that template and actionable/curation 

For example, one row might be:
1. The "give me 2 mins" hook
2. "Give me 2 mins and I'll teach you the 6 key steps to landing your first ghostwriting client."

Obviously the hook itself you will apply to my subtopic.

Now, the "hook" we use to introduce the content will vary. 

Here is the list of hooks templates

1. [X] free [y] to ...

[X] free [tools/websites/movies/books/newsletters/podcasts] to [achieve outcome]

Example: 
- 9 free design tools to make your presentations beautiful
- 9 free client outreach tools to help you land your first ghostwriting clienets

2. Statement, 99% don't know, here are...

Example:

The internet is free university.

But 99% don't know the best spots on this virtual campus.

Here are the top websites to accelerate your learning. 

3. Things I wish I knew

[X] [hacks/lessons/skills] I know at [after Y milestone] that I wish I knew at [before milestone]

Examples 
- Life hacks I know at 30 I wish I knew at 20 
- Lessons I know after 1000 ghostwriting clients I wish I knew at 0 clients:

4. How to [x] without [y], The [ABC] framework:

Example:

How to work 60 hours a week without burning out, The Focus Framework: 

5. The "give me 2 mins..."

Give me 2 minutes, and I'll teach you [x steps/rules/tactics] to [achieve outcome]

Give me 2 minutes, and I'll teach you 6 rules to be charismatic.

Here are a few rules:
- You should write the hooks in regular sentence case, not title case.

ChatGPT will generate a variety of hook suggestions for you. The results will be organized into an easy-to-read table format, so you can quickly choose the hook that resonates best with your content.

Prompt Writer’s Note: This prompt follows a simple, but powerful design that you can easily extend with different types of hooks. Find a hook you like, then replace an existing one in the prompt or simply add it to the list! Once you find a proven hook. Reverse engineer it. Then create a few examples so ChatGPT understands. Boom! Imagine if you had 10, 20, or 50 of these!

Step 3: Ask ChatGPT to make 10 more of each type of hook.

ChatGPT is great at making more, once it understands how to make one.

Use is to your advantage. If you see ChatGPT generating good results for any prompt you write, always, always, ask it to make more. You will have more things to choose from or you just add more to your library!
