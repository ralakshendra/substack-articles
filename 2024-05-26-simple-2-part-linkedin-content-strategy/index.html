    Hey there, Digital Writers!

    Today, I want to walk you through a powerful content strategy we've been using for years to increase engagement on LinkedIn and X and how you can use AI to help.

    It's called "Engagement Bait Questions."

    This technique involves asking questions that compel your audience to think, "I have a unique answer to share!" By doing so, you prime them to respond, which creates a flywheel of engagement. As more and more people chime in with their own responses, the engagement continues to grow.

    Let’s dive in.

    Why Engagement Bait Questions Work

    The key to crafting effective engagement bait questions is to ask something that will elicit a variety of answers.

    For example:

    • What's the single best book you've read this month or this year?

    • What's your favorite YouTube channel right now?

    • What's the biggest lesson you've learned recently?

    • What's your favorite online course you've purchased?

    • Where's the best place you've visited lately?

    As your audience starts responding, something interesting happens.

    People see others' answers and think "I have a different recommendation." This prompts them to add their own response to the mix. Then another person comes along, sees the existing answers, and is motivated to contribute their own unique perspective.

    Suddenly, you have a long thread full of varied, interesting responses—and a ton of engagement on your post.

    How To Create An Engagement Bait Question

    An Engagement Bait post follows a simple two-part structure:

    1. A one-sentence question as a standalone piece of short-form content

    2. A response to your own post with your personal answer

    The key is that the question prompts individualized answers from each person.

    Now, you can generate these questions on your own, or you can use AI to help.

    The Engagement Bait Prompt

    This prompt helps you:

    • Generates a variety of questions to choose from

    • Creates questions that resonate with your niche

    • Ensure the questions are concise and engaging

    Here’s the question prompt:

    Generate 5 engaging, thought-provoking questions designed to encourage responses and discussion among [your niche/audience]. 

Each question should be short, punchy, and open-ended, prompting users to share their unique experiences or opinions.

For example:
- What's the single best book you've read this month or this year?
- What's your favorite YouTube channel right now?
- What's the best small habit you've built this month?

    And if you need help coming up with a response, you can use the below script.

    All you need to do is fill in the question number you want the AI to answer.

    Ok, now please give me a sample response to Question {Number} using the below template.

<Template>
For me, it has been:

[Answer]

Why?

• [Reason 1]
• [Reason 2]
• [Reason 3]

Highly recommend
</Template>

Be succinct. Use 8-10 words for any variables you fill in.

    How to Use the AI-Generated Engagement Bait Questions

    1. Replace "[your niche/audience]" in the prompt with a description of your target audience or niche (e.g., "food bloggers," "productivity enthusiasts," "marketing professionals").

    2. Run the prompt through your preferred AI language model, such as Claude or ChatGPT.

    3. Review the generated questions and select the ones that best fit your content strategy and audience.

    4. If needed, make minor tweaks to the questions to better align with your voice or specific goals.

    5. Use the questions in your content calendar, aiming to post 1-2 engagement bait questions per week or every other week.

    The beauty of engagement bait questions is that they don't feel gimmicky or inauthentic. You're not tricking anyone. You’re prompting people to share and discuss. And as a bonus, you get exposed to all sorts of interesting recommendations from others' responses.

    It's a win-win.

    The crowdsourced answers expose you to books, courses, podcasts, etc. that may have never been on your radar before. Suddenly you have a fresh reading list or new content to explore, all while fostering lively discussions in your community.

    Engagement bait questions truly check all the boxes—driving audience interactions while facilitating your own growth and learning.
