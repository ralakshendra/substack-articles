    It’s a brand new set of Skills built from the Category Newsletter Creator (“CNC”) curriculum, so you can work through it directly inside Claude.

    But first, quick backstory.

    One of the biggest problems with courses is getting your students to “do the work.”

    I know because I’ve been sitting on a newsletter idea for months.

    • I’ve been through CNC.

    • I have all the training.

    • I know what to do.

    And still, I haven’t launched.

    Even with a detailed roadmap, full of practical examples and action steps which make writing and launching a newsletter super simple to implement, I still struggle to put the knowledge into action.

    Which got me thinking…

    What if I hired Claude to be my personal Category Newsletter Creator Coach?

    It had been several months since I first went through CNC and I wanted a refresher.

    We’d been building Skills internally for our content operations, so I knew what they could do. I figured if I trained Claude using Cowork on CNC the same way, I could work through the course with Claude pushing me through each step.

    So I loaded CNC into Claude.

    I wanted to see if I could make a newsletter launch even easier with Cowork.

    Cowork didn’t disappoint.

    Here’s what I did:

    • I trained Claude on all 5 mini-courses inside CNC—50,000+ words.

    • I broke the modules down into reusable Skills.

    • I taught it to tutor me and create the newsletter assets

    I’m oversimplifying a bit, but you get the idea.

    You go through it once, set everything up, and it remembers your configuration because it’s taught to do that.
