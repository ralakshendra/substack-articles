How To Create A LinkedIn Storytelling Post With ChatGPT

First off, this is going to be quick.

Because I know you are here for the prompt.

So, let’s get to it with 3 simple steps.

  • Step 1: First, go find 10 LinkedIn storytelling posts you like. The reason you need 10 is because we want AI to extract an accurate tone of voice, so that it can recreate it in your new post.

  • Step 2: Copy/paste each of the posts into a doc. Separate each post with the format: "Post 1:" then paste the post. Labeling each post helps ChatGPT distinguish between each post.

  • Step 3: Now, go to ChatGPT and use this prompt. I get the best results using GPT4. But you can use GPT3.5 or Claude or Gemini. The prompt is universal.

That’s it.

I told you I’d be quick.

Prompt 1: Write 2 New LinkedIn Posts

You are an expert ghostwriter that specializes in LinkedIn and Twitter marketing and you have years of business experience. 

Please analyze the following 10 posts from (insert the creators and brief description of them here). 

I want you to give me 2 new posts based on these posts. 

Please make the posts with the thought leadership of (insert creator here) and the formatting and copywriting skills of (insert creator here), with a slight influence from (insert creator here). 

I want you to make the sentences short and concise but also switch up the formatting so it's enjoyable to read. 

Please no hashtags, and please make sure the posts follow this opening format:

A bold statement that is no more than 8 words in sentence one.

A short statement beginning with "How I" and then a number included in this story opening sentence.

Here are the 10 posts to analyze:

(Insert your 10 posts here)

There are few variables you need to fill in the post, here’s what I used for this example.

  • Posts From: Nicolas Cole & Dickie Bush, co-founders of Ship 30 for 30 and the Premium Ghostwriting Academy (PGA). Ship 30 for 30 is a cohort-based course that has taught over 7,000 students the fundamentals of writing on the internet. PGA is the premier training program & community for people who want to become premium ghostwriters!

  • Thought Leadership Of: Nicolas Cole and the formatting and copywriting skills of Dickie Bush, with a slight influence from Justin Welsh.

  • Sample Posts: LinkedIn Post 1, LinkedIn Post 2, LinkedIn Post 3. These were longer posts, which is why I used 3 instead of 10.

Prompt 2: Weave A Personal Story Into A LinkedIn Post

Can you please add the following story into both posts:

(Insert a short story from your life relevant to the post themes)

Keep the integrity of both original posts, but just make them longer and add in elements of this new story and lesson into the posts.

Since we were creating posts for Cole here, I searched for a recent story post on “X.” It looks like he just got married. So I incorporated this short marriage story into the posts.

I got married this past week in Cartagena, Colombia! I'm no longer a bachelor. My biggest fear getting married is recognizing how deeply I love my partner. There is nothing more terrifying than taking the final step of opening myself up—and thinking about the day one of us doesn’t have the other. This is the real lesson love teaches us.

There you have it.

An AI-generated storytelling post.

I'd argue it beats most of the stories in the feed.

But to make it truly elite.

I edit with three final 3 steps:

  1. Tweak the hook to be more human.

  2. Fact-check the post and edit where needed.

  3. Simplify the CTA, add a PS, and make it your own.

I could keep prompting, but finishing these stories with a human touch is best.

So, take over that final bit.

But start with AI, and you'll save tons of time.
