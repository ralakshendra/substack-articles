    The “Tiny But Mighty” Viral Twitter Thread Hook: Promising Readers HUGE Outcomes For SMALL Effort

    This single hook has been working for 10,000 years.

    Author: Dickie Bush
    Date: 2023-05-14

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U_2X!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F82d5737e-5049-40d1-babd-24842cbc1c7b_1260x900.png)

    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Today we are going to play "Mad-Libs" with our Digital Intern and transform ChatGPT into a fill-in-the-blank template maestro.

    In the last 3 years, we've written thousands of tweets. And if there has been one thing that has 10X'd our ability to write more hits, it's reverse-engineering our best work into templates.

    • Templates defeat the blank page

    • Templates save time when you sit down to write

    • Templates take the pressure off creating something new from scratch

    You simply follow the formula and plug in the specifics—like LEGOs with words.

    And because this is Write With AI—rather than YOU spending time executing a template, we want to leverage the power of ChatGPT to do the executing for you, at will, forever, for any topic.

    Steal The Framework. Fill It In With Your Ideas.

    Here’s how:

    • Grab a proven tweet. (We are partial to the Ship 30 for 30 library of content!)

    • Reverse-engineer why it works and determine how you can use it.

    • Train your Digital Intern to steal like an artist.

    Let's dive in!

    The “Tiny But Mighty” Hook

    This template is called “Tiny But Mighty.” You can use it to turn your everyday small things: habits, lessons, tips, etc. into a BIG hook that captures tons of attention.

    Here’s what it look

    s like in the wild (link to thread):

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!o3rZ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1b2f9a79-42e4-4577-a67c-169dc084ce76_1188x642.png)

    Here’s the template:

    Over the past {Time Period}, I have {Action} hundreds of small (but powerful) {Kind Of Tips} Tips.

    But these {Number} accumulated more than {Big Result 1} and {Big Result 2}.

    Here they are all in one place

    Let’s break down what’s working in this hook.

    Why The “Tiny But Mighty” Hook Works

    • It captures the reader’s imagination with a moment in time, “Over the past year.”

    • It establishes credibility with a large number of tips shared during the time period.

    • It hooks the reader with desire,“1,000,000 views,” and curiosity, “19 small (but powerful) tips!”

    • It packages up the tiny tips in a single place—making it easy for the reader to learn!

    How To Use The “Tiny But Mighty” Hook

    Find something small that you’ve personally leveraged or experienced that a reader can take action on immediately.

    • 5 minute daily habits that give you massive energy

    • 5 small purchases that help you earn a 1000% return

    • 5 phrases you use to attract the most powerful people on the internet

    The trick is to juxtapose your “Tiny But Mighty” thing against ALL the things you’ve tried.

    When you do this, you signal to the reader you are giving them the MOST valuable things. It’s not just 19 writing tips, it’s 19 out of “hundreds” of tips. And while the hundreds of tips generated tons of views, these 19 are the most powerful, making up the lion’s share of all the views.

    Feel the difference?

    All you have to do is fill in the blanks with your topic. Small things wrapped in a big package always deliver more clicks.

    Use ChatGPT To Fill-In-The-Blanks Of “The Tiny But Mighty” Hook Template

    Run the ChatGPT prompt to start a game of Mad-Libs with your Digital Intern!

    • We’ve trained it on the “Tiny But Mighty” hook.

    • The prompt works best with a broad universal topic. For example, use “Copywriting” instead of “Copywriting for beginning entrepreneur’s to make their first $10K.”

    • Play along by giving ChatGPT your topic: Dancing, Running, Leadership, Ghostwriting, etc.

    Let’s go!

    Here’s the prompt:

    I am going to train you to write a "Tiny But Mighty" short-form content (280 characters or less).

I am going to give you a topic and you will write a "Tiny But Mighty" short-form piece of content following the "Tiny But Mighty" TEMPLATE.

You MUST follow the format of the template.

You MUST fill in all the variables and follow the template precisely to effectively capture the reader's imagination, establish credibility, and create curiosity.

---
Here is the "Tiny But Mighty" TEMPLATE:
---
### Tiny But Mighty Hook

Over the past {Time Period}, I have {Action} hundreds of small (but powerful}) {Kind Of Tips} Tips.

But these {Number} accumulated more than {Big Result 1} and {Big Result 2}. 

Here they are all in one place
---
Here is an EXAMPLE of what the template looks like when it's completed so you know what I'm looking for:
---
Over the past year, I have tweeted hundreds of small (but powerful) Writing Tips.

But these 19 accumulated more than 1,000,000 views and thousands of comments & shares. 

Here they are all in one place
---

Remember: follow the template precisely to effectively capture the reader's imagination, establish credibility, and create curiosity.

Are you ready for me to give you the topic?
