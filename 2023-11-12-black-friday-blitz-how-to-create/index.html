    How To Create Your First Superniche Product

    Your first digital product (say, an eBook) does not need to be a "masterpiece."

    In fact, your digital product might only be 10 or 20 pages. It's not how long the digital product is, or how many words you've written, that determines its value. All that matters is that your digital product answers a valuable question to your target reader/customer.

    The person buying your product isn't buying "a bunch of words" or even "a PDF."

    What they're buying is an answer to a super specific question that when answered solves 1 problem and 1 problem only.

    The Black Friday Superniche Product Launch Plan

    In this guide you will find a 10-Day plan that you can execute before Black Friday.

    You can easily create your first digital product in a week—especially if you’ve been following the Lean Writing approach. Ideally you will start 12 days prior to Black Friday, to give yourself enough time to promote your product before the “big shopping day.” Day 1 is Monday.

    Here’s everything we are going to cover:

    • Day 1 - Monday: Find a Superniche product idea and outline it

    • Day 2 - Tuesday: Create Your Superniche product framework

    • Day 3 - Wednesday: Prep The Page for your Superniche product

    • Day 4 - Thursday: Design the product (graphics, layout, etc.)

    • Day 5 - Friday: Create the landing page and set up the payment system

    • Day 6 - Saturday: Run a small beta test of the product

    • Day 7 - Sunday: Make revisions based on beta feedback

    • Day 8 - Monday: Launch a viral giveaway/contest to build your email list

    • Day 9 - Tuesday: Black Friday promotion and make your first $1!

    • Day 10 - Wednesday: Put your marketing on auto-pilot

    7 Questions Every Reader Asks Before Investing In A Product

    When creating a “How To” digital product, you need to take the reader by the hand and guide them FROM where they are TO where you want them to end up by answering the 7 questions every reader has before they invest their time, attention, and money into you and your products.

    So, let's create a quick outline to help you answer the questions.

    I'm creating a product for {AUDIENCE} to help them {OVERCOME PROBLEM/ACHIEVE OUTCOME}.

I need help answering the following questions:

1. What problem are we solving?
2. What 3 benefits get unlocked when this problem gets solved?
3. How "expensive" is this problem, and how can we provide the solution for a fraction of the normal cost?
4. What myths keep people from solving this problem in their lives?
5. What is real lived moment that people with the problem typically first experience/became aware of this problem—and how have they overcome it? What's the unlikely answer?
6. What 3rd-party examples, case studies, or stories can you point to that prove it's possible to overcome this problem (and if they do, what amazing things happen)? What does life look like on the other side?
7. What 3-7 action steps/framework can we walk the reader through to help them solve this problem in an actionable, highly effective way?

A few rules:
- Avoid jargon.
- Get to the point.
- Use simple and direct language.
- Use bullets. 10-12 words per bullet.

    If you can answer the above 7 questions, you essentially have 80% of the work done.

    Day 2: Create Your Superniche Product Framework

    A framework is really just a step-by-step way of thinking about something. So whenever you are trying to articulate a big idea/product to readers, chop it down into bite-sized pieces and present it as a framework they can learn and implement in tiny chunks.

    For example:

    What 3-7 action steps, mindset shifts, or mistakes are you going to walk the reader through to help them solve this problem in an actionable, highly effective way?

    Then name the framework and describe it as concisely as possible, so your reader will remember what it is, what it does, and you can claim it!

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!H13E!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa5feb5f0-4c5c-4597-bd13-f69573c96588_1024x1024.png)

    Here’s a prompt to help you create your own framework (and visual too)!

    Act like an entrepreneurial thought leader.

You are going to create a new framework for my Superniche Product Idea:

{YOUR SUPERNICHE PRODUCT IDEA}

1. You will create a new framework by combining a framework type with a strategy diagram. 
2. You will reframe the question to align it with the benefit of the visualization and ground it in the actionable nature of the "framework-y" question. Create curiosity by using the name of the framework in the question.
3. You will name the framework.
4. You will describe the solution. Empathize with someone who’s hearing your idea for the first time. Try to make the non-linear a bit more sequential, so others can follow along.
5. You will describe how to use the framework with the illustration. Example: fill in blanks, draw lines, choose something, etc.
6. You will write a DALL-E prompt to create a "back of the napkin" worksheet using the framework. It must be a rudimentary black and white hand-drawn illustration with simple shapes and lines. Leave blank space for the reader to fill in their own notes, action steps, plot points etc. Leave plenty of white space. Do not include any additional elements or text.
7. Run the DALL-E prompt.

There are 3 types of frameworks:
- Mindset: The mindset it takes to succeed / the proper way to think about things
- Mistakes: The mistakes people make and why it's wrong
- Method: The right way to to do something / the practical behaviors and steps.

The visualization should create 1 of the following types of transformations:
1. Raise Awareness: From I don’t know to now I know
2. Build A Skill: From I couldn’t to now I can
3. Change An Action: Stop doing one thing and start doing it another way
4. Shift A Belief: Understand themselves or the world in a new way

Please respond with:
- Framework Type
- Framework Name
- Framework Description, Objective, How it works
- Example Of How It Can Be Used In Daily Life
- What It Helps You Avoid
- How To Apply It / The Benefit / The Transformation

    Note: You will need GPT-4 to run this prompt and create an image with DALL-E.

    Day 3: Prep The Page For Your Superniche Product

    Now that you have a product idea and a framework it’s time to create the skeleton/outline. We do this by introducing the thinking, emotions, and myths about the topic. Then we move into the actionable steps, examples, and tips that you want to give the reader.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YQ2h!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5b3ffc53-f0e2-4313-aff9-0c3ca9804146_600x600.png)

    Here’s the prompt:

    Act like an expert Product Manager and UX Designer.

I need your help to outline my Superniche Product:

{YOUR SUPERNICHE PRODUCT}

The goal is to have 3-7 subheaders.

The Introduction:

- Thinking: Every Superniche product starts by introducing net-new thinking: Explain the new problem, the old problem, reconsidered and  the unique & different POV
- Emotion: After new thinking is presented, the reader has lots of emotions swirling around inside them. Empathize with  reader's emotions—so the reader knows you understand them
- Myths: Before the reader can take Action Steps forward, they need to know where they went wrong (backward). Explain the faulty beliefs that are still holding the reader back, what they think is true, that isn't, and where the reader "seeing clearly"

The Action Steps:

- Only once the reader trusts you to take them in a new direction will they be reader to take action. Do this. For this reason. To unlock X. Empathize with someone who’s hearing the idea for the first time. Make the non-linear a bit more sequential, so others can follow along.

Each Action Step Should:

1. Explain the idea in very simple terms

2. Show the potential of what applying this idea will help them achieve

3. Give a concrete example of how this works. Explain these steps, what success looks like, and  what happens if you don't take these steps

4. Conclude with a simple action step the reader can take in 5 minutes or less in each section to make it actionable and give them a helpful template to follow.

Example 1: If one of the subheaders shows YouTubers how to increase their thumbnail click-through rate, actionable advice would be about creating 2 variations of a thumbnail for a video they already have uploaded and then using a tool to set up an A/B test.

Example 2: If one of the subheaders shows busy managers with sleep problems how to decrease their caffeine levels, actionable advice would be to stop drinking coffee after 1PM and replace it with plain or sparkling water.

Please provide me with an outline I can use to create my Superniche poduct with these specific tips:

{Your Framework Description, Example, Application}

    All you have to do is fill it in!

    And of course, you can get some help from ChatGPT.

    Expand your outline using the 10 Magical Ways.

    To do this, plug one Main Idea (Subheader) from your outline into the script below.

    Decide how you want to expand it (Reasons, Tips, etc.) Then list them below each sub-point. Optionally, add your own ideas.

    For example:

    Step 2: Stretch And Strengthen

    • Reason: Reduce strain on the overworked plantar fascia

    • Mistake: Neglecting calf stretching and focusing only on the foot

    • Tip: Warm up your legs before you stretch with 10 air squats.

    I need help expanding my outline.

There are 10 ways you can expand any piece of writing.

- Tips
- Stats
- Steps
- Lessons
- Benefits
- Reasons
- Mistakes
- Examples
- Questions
- Personal Stories

I will choose which of these 10 I want you to use in each section of my outline, I just need your help filling it in. 

Please do not change the formatting. Just insert responses.

Here is the outline:

Topic = {SUPERNICHE PRODUCT}

Main Idea = {OUTLINE SUBHEADER}

{OUTLINE SUB-POINT}*
    - {REASON/TIP/MISTAKE/EXAMPLE/ETC. 1:}
    - {REASON/TIP/MISTAKE/EXAMPLE/ETC. 2:}
    - ...

    Turn your “expanded” outline into writing.

    Great, now expand the outline into {NUMBER} words.

Topic = {SUPERNICHE PRODUCT}

Write the section.

Use this format:

In 1 sentence, make a strong declarative statement about the section.

In 5 sentences, clarify the opener, add context, and elaborate.

In 1 sentence, make a strong conclusion or transition to the section.

*************************

Please:
- Avoid jargon.
- Get to the point. Avoid fluff.
- Use markdown to format the post.
- Use a simple, clear, direct, and relatable tone.
- The post should flow naturally from point to point.
- Bias toward short, concise sentences.(12 words max per sentence)

    Day 5: Write Your Landing Page Copy

    Most people would stop here and immediately leap to package up the product.

    This is a mistake. You don’t know if anyone wants it yet! Which is why we want to write some simple sales copy to see if we can attract someone to the offer.

    To do this, answer the questions from Step 2 in your sales copy!

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!eHS4!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4d4ab738-e6c4-4130-8b53-ea02836921a7_600x682.png)

    Here’s the prompt:

    Act as a Copywriting Expert with 20 years of experience.

Your task is to create a compelling Opt-In Landing Page that captures the attention of the target audience, ignites their desire, and guides them seamlessly towards taking action. The goal is to make the process easy and intuitive for them, without causing confusion.

For my Superniche Product Idea:

{YOUR SUPERNICHE PRODUCT IDEA + FRAMEWORK DESCRIPTION}

Craft an effective Opt-In Landing Page, including the sections outlined below. Instead of using labels, present the landing page contents directly.

The landing page should answer the following questions for the reader:

Title Dream Outcome: Make a bold promise to the {Target Audience} that they will achieve their Dream Outcome use 10 words or less. For example, "Become a Prolific Digital Writer in 7 Days"

1. Who is the target reader? Subtly call them out. Make it apparent who its for without being overly in your face.
2. Where is the target reader stuck in Struggle City? Enumerate their 5 biggest problems, how they feel about it, and how badly they want them solved.
3. Who can they blame? Help them feel urgency without feeling bad about themselves.
4. What are the myths holding them back from making progress? What advice do “most people” give them, and why is it wrong?
5. What’s the unlikely answer? Be specific. The devil is in the details. Give them something they aren't expecting.
6. What are the benefits of this “road less traveled?” What will the reader experience if they take this less-known road? What are the benefits of going the opposite way as everyone else?
7. What does their life look like “on the other side?” They need to know it's worth it. Don’t hold back. Tell them everything. Tell them what their life will be like. Tell them all the things that will be different (for good and bad). Be honest. Tell them what they can expect to find, who they can expect to meet, and what they can expect to feel. Paint the vision.
8. What do they need to do next?

FORMAT: Use Markdown formatting to structure the landing page. Utilize #H1, ##H3 for headings, bold for emphasis, and bullet points for lists.

Remember: Only provide the landing page contents without the section labels.

    The landing page for your Superniche Product should be very simple.

    Almost so simple that you think, “Wait… don't I need more?”

    Not really.

    The best free opt-in landing pages are minimalist. There are no links. There is only 1 choice for the visitor to make, and that’s whether or not they want to trade their email address for your free email course. That’s it. Ideally, your entire landing page should also fit “above the fold” so people don’t even have to scroll down to make a decision.

    Day 6-7: Run A Small Beta Test To Get Feedback

    For every Like, Comment, or Question you received on your most popular posts, DM the person who engaged with you.

    • Thank them for following you and for reading your work.

    • Let them know you are launching a new product.

    • Ask them if they would be interested in being part of your “Beta” launch team in exchange for feedback.

    • Once someone says yes, share the Gumroad page, the framework, and the outline with them (for free).

    • Ask them what else they would like to see included and take note of every question, objection, and compliment you receive.

    Incorporate everything you learn into your product.

    Do this until you have 3 people who are willing to join you Beta program for free.
