Save The Cat Writes A Novel With AI

A step-by-step prompt system to turn your idea into a fiction story

Author: Nicolas Cole
Date: 2025-01-30

[

Welcome to Save The Cat Writes With AI—a complete guide to writing your novel using the legendary Save The Cat methodology—powered by AI. 🥳

This as an exclusive bonus for Founding Members of Write With AI.

If you've ever wanted to write fiction but felt stuck on where to start, you’re in luck.

This course is your step-by-step toolkit for:

  • Crafting compelling story ideas from scratch

  • Nailing your genre and plot archetype

  • Building unforgettable characters with depth

  • Mapping out the iconic 15 beats of your story

  • Expanding those beats into a detailed 40-scene outline

  • Turning your novel into a page-turner, one prompt at a time

Whether you're a seasoned writer or starting your very first novel, these prompts will guide you through every phase of storytelling. You’ll save time, overcome creative blocks, and bring your ideas to life faster than ever before.

Great fiction writing is about structure AND heart. With these tools, you’ll have the confidence to tackle both.
