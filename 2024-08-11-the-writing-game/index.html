Hey there,

If you aren't familiar, we have another paid newsletter for storytellers called Fiction Writing With AI.

And last week, we rolled out a fun new product called “The Writing Game."

We thought it was worth sharing with you because one of the biggest struggles ALL writers (fiction or non-fiction) face is showing up to write. Writers love to trick themselves into not writing with a good old, “I’ll do that tomorrow…when I feel like it” excuse.

But, here’s a dirty little secret.

You can pull the wool over Procrastination’s eyes with a touch of reverse psychology, called Rewards.

Rewards tap into your brain's natural love for instant gratification—just like procrastination. But, instead of the gratification of an extra hour of Netflix, they give you that extra push to get started. And once you do, the satisfaction of earning something keeps you going. It’s a fun way to trick your brain into staying motivated.

Which is why we created The Writing Game.
