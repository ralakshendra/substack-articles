    Last week, we tried a new strategy on Substack Notes.

    It outperformed our most viral post by 50X. No surprise there. Because the same strategy works for us on LinkedIn.

    Between both platforms, we added 1,894 new subscribers to our email list in 7 days without spending a cent on ads

    Here’s what happened:

    Amazon banned my book publishing account (oops—long story), so I decided to give away 2 of my books for free.

    To do this, I created a Substack Note explaining the situation and asked people to comment “books” to get them. Then, I personally DMed everyone who commented with the free books. The engagement triggered the algorithm, creating a viral loop that lasted a week (most posts die after 24 hours).

    We call this a Viral Drop.

    Click the Note below to read the post (and grab the books) 👇 

    The Viral Drop Strategy

    The formula is simple:

    • You create a valuable free asset

    • Tease it in a social post (like a Note)

    • Then ask people to comment to get access

    It’s a giveaway.

    The engagement triggers the algorithm to show your post to more people, creating a viral loop that can capture thousands of eyeballs AND email addresses in hours. The reason this technique works so well is because people want what others want. When they see hundreds of comments asking for your asset, their brain thinks “everyone else wants this, so I should want it too.”

    With this strategy, one Substack Note could attract thousands of new eyeballs and brand new subscribers to your newsletter.

    How to create your own “Viral Drop” on Substack

    Now, here’s how you can replicate this (even if you don’t have a book to give away):

    • Find your valuable asset. Look through your Google Docs, hard drive, or files for something you’ve already created: a template, SOP, spreadsheet, ChatGPT prompt, checklist, or framework. If you need to create something from scratch, use this AI Lead Magnet Generator prompt.

    • Create your Note. Include a hook (mine was the Amazon ban story), explain what you’re giving away, and give clear instructions (comment “X” to get it). Add an image that repeats the hook.

    • Monitor and DM everyone. Personally DM each person who comments. Send them to a landing page to get your asset. Every DM is a touchpoint with a potential new subscriber.

    Now, we could have 10x’d our results if he hadn’t made this one mistake.

    We only asked people to comment to get the books. To get more engagement, we should have asked readers to comment + like + subscribe. All 3 actions will juice the algorithm even more.

    Note: The fact that you’re using this technique doesn’t mean your post is guaranteed to go viral, but it will improve your odds!

    How to write your Viral Drop Note

    Here’s a simple template you can use to create your own giveaway post:

    Want to [Desirable Outcome] as a [Target Audience]?

I spent [Time/Money Investment] creating [Asset Name] to help you:

- [Specific Benefit 1]
- [Specific Benefit 2] 
- [Specific Benefit 3]

Want access?

Like & reply “[specific word]” and I’ll send it for FREE!

    For example:

    Want to build a profitable writing career as a writer or content creator?

    Amazon just banned my book publishing account (sold 40,000+ copies over 10 years—now it’s all gone).

    So screw it—I’m giving away my 2 most popular books for free.

    I spent 10+ years creating The Art & Business of Online Writing and The Art & Business of Ghostwriting to help you:

    • Find your voice and build a daily writing habit (without starting a blog)

    • Get paid to network and learn from industry leaders in your niche

    • Escape the freelance writer hamster wheel and make real money as a writer

    Want access?

    Like & reply “books” and I’ll send them for FREE!

    Why this works:

    • It’s specific. The post isn’t vague about what you’re getting. It’s not “writing tips”—it’s The Art & Business of Online Writing and The Art & Business of Ghostwriting.

    • It’s valuable. Giving away two full books (that people normally pay for) builds massive perceived value. Plus, the context of “sold 40,000+ copies over 10 years” signals that these aren’t just random PDFs—they’re proven resources that thousands of people have paid for.

    • It’s easy to get. The ask is dead simple: comment “books.” Not “tell me your biggest writing challenge.” Not “explain why you want this.” Just one word. The easier you make it, the more people will do it. And the more people who do it, the more the algorithm pushes your post.

    However, don’t link directly to your asset in the post.

    This kills virality because the algorithm will punish you for including an external link. The whole strategy depends on people commenting to get access.’

    Viral Drop Post Generator

    I’ve trained this AI prompt to leverage the viral drop template.

    So all you have to do is feed it the details of your asset or giveaway and it will generate 3 different variations for you.

    Act like a direct-response copywriter.

You are an expert in attracting attention on social platforms.

I want you to write 3 different variations of a viral giveaway post to help me generate leads for my {main product  / service}.

Example template:

Want to [Desirable Outcome] as a [Target Audience]?

I spent [Time/Money Investment] creating [Asset Name] to help you:

- [Specific Benefit 1]
- [Specific Benefit 2] 
- [Specific Benefit 3]

[Reason]

Want access?

Like & reply “[specific word]” and I’ll send it for FREE!

The reason this template works is because:

- It includes a hook with a big specific promise
- It includes a reason why you are giving the asset away for free
- It’s clear what the benefits of opting into the offer are
- It’s requires the reader to engage to get the free asset (the CTA)

Understand?

When you’re ready, I will give you the details of my lead magnet and you will write the posts.
