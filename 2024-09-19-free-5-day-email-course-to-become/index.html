    ⁉️ The Biggest Problems In The Age Of AI

    Chances are, if you’re reading this page right now, you stumbled across our newsletter because you were looking for a solution—a solution to one of the following problems.

    (We know, because we have and are experiencing all of these same problems! And Write With AI is our way of building solutions for ourselves—and sharing them with you.)

    • Problem #1: Prompt paralysis — Not knowing what to ask or how to phrase it, so they stare at a blank input box and give up.

    • Problem #2: Vague outputs — Getting generic, surface-level responses because they don’t yet know how to give context, constraints, or examples.

    • Problem #3: Over-reliance too soon — Using AI to write for them before developing their own voice, which stunts growth and creates dependency.

    • Problem #4: Mistrust of the output — Not knowing when to trust AI-generated content, leading to either blind acceptance or reflexive rejection.

    • Problem #5: Tool overwhelm — Too many AI tools, models, and platforms to choose from, making it hard to commit to learning one thing well.

    • Problem #6: No feedback loop — Treating AI as a one-shot tool instead of a back-and-forth collaborator, so they never iterate toward better results.

    • Problem #7: Skill attribution confusion — Not knowing what they contributed vs. what AI did, making it hard to improve or take ownership of their work.

    • Problem #8: Context collapse — Failing to give the AI enough background (audience, goal, tone, format), leading to results that technically answer the question but miss the mark.

    • Problem #9: Fear of “cheating” — A mental block around using AI for creative or professional work, which creates guilt and inconsistent usage.

    • Problem #10: No system or workflow — Using AI randomly and reactively instead of building repeatable processes that compound over time.

    Now, let’s talk about how to solve all of these.
