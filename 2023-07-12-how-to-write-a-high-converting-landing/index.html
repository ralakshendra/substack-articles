    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Today, we’re going to share a secret with you…

    What we’re about to share with you is a strategy 99% of Digital Writers, Digital Creators, marketers, and even heads of entire content marketing departments inside multibillion-dollar companies don’t know (and certainly don’t understand).

    Are you ready?

    It’s called The Rule Of 1:

    • 1 page

    • 1 offer

    • 1 choice (subscribe or don’t)

    Here’s how it works:

    If you want to move people from social media to your email list, avoid sending them to a cluttered website with a bunch of links. Instead, send them to a dedicated page where they can easily sign up for your eBook, Ultimate Guide, Email Course, or whatever you're offering. Sending them to your website with 87 different things going on just makes it 10X harder for them to find what you want them to find and type in their email address.

    Keep it simple.

    Keep it focused.

    And you'll have a much better chance of turning free followers (rented audience) into loyal email subscribers (owned audience).

    The landing page for your free opt-in should be very simple.

    Almost so simple that you think, “Wait… don't I need more?”

    For example, take a look at how simple our Start Writing Online landing page is.

    • 1 page: startwritingonline.com

    • 1 offer: “Download our Ultimate Guide to start writing online.”

    • 1 choice: Type in your email or don’t. (No, you can’t navigate anywhere else.)

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4clv!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4dad7a4a-b1f7-44bc-99fb-c4390d4bda28_2000x1078.png)

    No other links, no other pages, nothing.

    This is the key to increasing the opt-in rate (%) and number of people who download your product—and ultimately make their way onto your email list/newsletter.

    You cannot give people more than 1 option.

    Either type in your email and download this free asset.

    Or don’t.

    That’s it.

    The 5 Sections For Your Opt-In Landing Page

    OK, now that you understand the strategy (drive readers to 1 page, with 1 offer, with only 1 choice), let’s dig into the tactics.

    Here is the information you should strive to have on your free opt-in page:

    H1: {Dream Outcome}

    This should be the biggest text on your landing page.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!W_O_!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F81bdefe8-a8f7-4ed7-aec5-eb311d0f0303_1186x184.png)

    The Dream Outcome the promise you are making to the reader. And the reason why someone will choose to give you their email address is because they are confident in your ability to help them achieve that Dream Outcome.

    Big Email Button {CTA}

    Next, make it very obvious what you want the visitor to do.

    “If you want this Dream Outcome in 7 days, type in your email here.”

    No need to get fancy with it.

    What Does The Person Get?

    List out 3-5 “things” the person gets, or is going to learn, after they put in your email address.

    For example:

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Z4A5!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdb3cc9a9-e748-46af-a0fb-6424397a80e9_1394x274.png)

    The more you can emphasize “this is what you are going to receive,” the more likely someone is to subscribe.

    “Everything You Need To…”

    Finally, tell the visitor that if they subscribe, they will get everything they need to achieve 3 desirable outcomes related to their overarching Dream Outcome.

    For example:

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!QOWO!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F604ffff2-65dd-4fda-9de9-81582ab6f920_1376x566.png)

    And remember: whatever promise you make, you better keep it!

    Bonus: “Want to make sure this is worth it?”

    This last part isn’t required, but it’s a nice touch.

    Create a final section that tells people (verbatim) what they are going to receive/learn. This helps them see exactly what they’re signing up for and what they can expect in exchange for giving you their email address.

    Let’s build your landing page with ChatGPT!

    Writing Your Opt-In Landing Page With ChatGPT

    You could write this on our own OR you could enlist the help of a Digital Marketing & Copywriting expert with over 20 years of experience—ChatGPT.

    Your choice.

    Before you call your Digital Intern, gather the following the inputs for your Opt-In Landing Page:

    Topic = {e.g. iPhone Photography}

Target Audience = {e.g. People who want to take photos of street scenes}

Outcome = {e.g. Take Award Worthy iPhone Photos In 5 Days}

Product/Service = {e.g Ultimate Guide To Incredible iPhone Photography}

    Run the prompt.

    Act as a Digital Marketing & Copywriting expert with 20 years of experience.

Your task is to create a compelling Opt-In Landing Page that captures the attention of your {Target Audience}, ignites their desire, and guides them seamlessly towards taking action. The goal is to make the process easy and intuitive for them, without causing confusion.

Here's what you need to do:

I will provide you with a {Topic}, a {Target Audience}, an {Outcome}, and a {Product/Service}.
Craft an effective Opt-In Landing Page, including the sections outlined below.
Instead of using labels, present the landing page contents directly.

Here are the Opt-In Landing Page sections:

1/ Title Dream Outcome: Make a bold promise to the {Target Audience} that they will achieve their Dream Outcome use 10 words or less. For example, "Become a Prolific Digital Writer in 7 Days"

2/ Visual Suggestion: Provide a suggestion for a visual representation that instantly communicates what the {Product/Service} is about, helping your {Target Audience} understand its value at a glance.

3/ Social Proof: Enhance credibility by including a succinct quote from a satisfied customer, industry expert, or reputable source.

4/ Opt-In Button CTA: Encourage the {Target Audience} to take the next step with a clear and concise call-to-action. Use 5 words or less. For example, "Send me the ultimate guide!"

5/ What You Get: Make concrete the tangible FEATURES you get in 4 succinct bullets using 8 words or less. For examples, the number of emails, frameworks, etc.

6/ Everything You Need: Explain how you'll create the Dream Outcome in 1 specific sentence starting with the phrase "Everything you need to..." achieve 3 outcomes.

7/ More Social Proof: Strengthen credibility further with an additional succinct quote from a satisfied customer, industry expert, or reputable source.

8/ Creator's Note: Establish trust with your {Target Customer} in one succinct sentence by highlighting your credibility, expertise, or relevant experience.

9/ Want to make sure it's worth it?: Create a final section informing the {Target Audience} of exactly what they will receive or learn upon signing up in a bulleted list. This allows them to see the value they'll gain in exchange for providing their email address. For example, if they are getting a 5 day email course, use each day as a bullet.

10/ 2nd Opt-In Button CTA

FORMAT: Use Markdown formatting to structure the landing page. Utilize #H1, ##H3 for headings, bold for emphasis, and bullet points for lists.

Remember: Only provide the landing page contents without the section labels.

Confirm you understand and then I give you the {Topic}, a {Target Audience}, an {Outcome}, and a {Product/Service}.

    And away you go!

    Boom!

    That’s everything you need to create a high-converting opt-in landing page.

    Once your landing page is written, it becomes a game of continuing to tweak, optimize, and improve each one of these sections over time. But, with each of these sections in place, it will be much easier for you to know how to make strategic changes without feeling like you need to rewrite the entire thing.

    Just take it section by section, and make improvements as you see fit.
