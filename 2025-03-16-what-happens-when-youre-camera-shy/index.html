Do you remember the first time you thought about starting to make videos?

Maybe it was years ago. Maybe it was last week.

But I bet I know what happened next...

You start thinking about what everyone else thinks.

Your family.

Your friends.

Your coworkers.

Some random person from high school who might stumble across your video.

It's called the Spotlight Effect. And it’s a paralyzing belief that everyone is watching and judging your every move.

The truth?

Most people won’t even notice.

When you scroll YouTube, do you obsess over how someone else looked in their first videos?

No—you watch, take in the info, and move on.

That’s how people will treat your videos too.

Fear of the spotlight keeps thousands of would-be creators from ever pressing record.

Now if you’re really against being on camera…
