    You know from yesterday that people buy to achieve an outcome. They want to "build an audience" or "lose 20 pounds" or "make $10K/month."

    But as soon as you promise you can help them get their desired result, their brain immediately goes:

    "Yeah, but…how is THIS different?"

    If you don’t answer that question and show them exactly how you’ll deliver the outcome, they’ll scroll right past your offer and hunt for something new.

    They’ve heard all those “get-rich,” “get-fit,” and “get-famous” claims before.

    And they've tried other solutions that didn’t work.

    Your product is their new “mechanism.”

    A mechanism is your named, systematic approach to solving their problem.

    It's how you deliver your solution.

    For example:

    • Lose 20 pounds → The 6-Week Metabolic Reset Method

    • Make $10K/month → The 90-Day Revenue Recovery System

    • Get more clients → The 5-Touch Client Magnet System

    For Ship 30 for 30, people wanted to start writing online (the outcome), and Ship 30 for 30 immediately told them exactly how they'd be doing it: shipping 30 pieces of content in 30 days. Our mechanism is very different from"just write" advice.

    When you can explain step-by-step what your product does and why it’s different, you separate your offer from every other generic claim out there.

    This clarity helps your prospect believe your solution can get them the result they want.

    Here's why named mechanisms work so well:

    A few reasons:

    1. They feel doable. "Ship 30 for 30" immediately tells someone exactly what they'll be doing. It’s concrete. And they can visualize following the process. It also doesn't sound overwhelming or impossible—and people love easy!

    2. They sound different. When everyone else is saying "lose weight," you're saying "reset your metabolism" which it stands out because it’s different.

    3. They make you curious. Your brain wants to know: "What exactly happens in those 6 weeks? How does the metabolic reset work? What are the 5 touches in the client magnet system?"

    The mechanism creates intrigue around your method, which pulls people toward your solution instead of you having to make a bigger better promise than your competitors.

    Now, let's use AI to generate 5 different mechanisms.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Vg8H!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3b5574eb-daca-4858-af3e-a6037ec7b5be_1648x1068.png)

    The AI prompt I'm about to share will take your niche, problem and outcome and generate 5 different mechanisms you can choose from.

    Each mechanism will be:

    • A different product format (course, toolkit, membership, etc.)

    • Targeted at a different segment within your niche

    • A different business model

    This gives you options to pick the mechanism that fits your strengths, schedule, and goals.

    Here's the prompt:

    Copy and paste this into ChatGPT or Claude (remember to add your niche details).

    I want to build my first digital product. 

You are a business strategist and product-development expert who’s helped coaches, consultants, solopreneurs, and entrepreneurs launch successful digital offers. 

Generate 5 distinct digital-product mechanisms that solve my target customers’ main problem and deliver their desired outcome.

- My niche-within-niche: {niche} 
- My main problem: {problem}
- My main outcome: {outcome}

Each mechanism must:

1. Be a digital product I can build as a first-time creator (e.g., online course, membership community, toolkit, template library, digital download, mini-SaaS, etc.).
2. Serve a different customer segment or vertical within my niche (for example, one targets busy coaches, another targets corporate consultants, another targets new freelancers).
3. Create value through a different economic principle (for example, one uses a one-time license, another uses a subscription, another uses royalty or affiliate revenue, another uses a freemium/ad-supported model).
4. Require distinct skills, risk/reward profiles, and time investments so I can choose the option that fits my strengths and schedule.
5. Generate income via different methods (for example, one sells directly to users, one charges a recurring fee, one splits revenue with partners, one uses ad or affiliate income).
Make sure mechanism #1 and mechanism #5 are so different that the same person wouldn’t choose both.

For each of the 5 mechanisms, include:
- Name (a brief, memorable title)
- Primary promise (the core benefit or transformation I’m delivering)
- Unique mechanism (exactly which digital-product model this is)
- Best format (course, membership, toolkit, template pack, mini-SaaS, etc.)
- Key components (3–5 steps, modules, or pieces of the product)
- Why it works (what makes this approach uniquely effective in my niche)
- Ideal customer profile (which niche segment (who) this appeals to most)


Finally, recommend which mechanism would be:
- Most appealing to my target customer
- Easiest for me to create and deliver as my first digital product
- Most likely to generate consistent sales

    Once you have your 5 product ideas, pick your favorite and start building your landing page.

    When you write copy for a landing page, you quickly realize how much you do or don’t understand the problem you’re solving.

    This is good news because it helps you build a better product.

    Why?

    Because you'll start asking questions like: "What's really going through my audience’s head at this moment? What are they afraid of? What's the real reason they haven't solved this problem yet?"

    The deeper you understand their journey from problem to outcome, the better you can position your mechanism as the bridge that gets them there.

    Boom!

    You now have everything you need to generate a profitable product idea.

    And if you want to take the next step and test demand for your product, I recommend you start writing about the problem you’re solving on X, LinkedIn, or other relevant social community.

    You can use this prompt to help you come up with different angles to test:

    "Write 5 different viral social media hooks for a product that [brief description of your mechanism]. Make each headline focus on a different benefit: time saved, money made, stress avoided, status gained, or skill learned."

    The one that gets the most engagement?

    That's your winner.
