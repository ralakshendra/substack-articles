    The "Sadvertising" Prompt

    Ready to get started on some emotional, short-form video ideas?

    Here’s the steps:

    Step 1: Brainstorm 3 Emotional Video Concepts

    To kick things off, run the prompt to generate ideas, or, if you have a specific product, brand, or cause in mind, include it in the “Topic” section at the end of the prompt.

    In Andrew's case, he wants to promote a local preschool.

    After you’ve entered the topic, start your chat. ChatGPT/Claude will generate a list of 3 different story concepts that you can choose to develop further.

    Step 2: Select & Develop Your Story Concept

    After reviewing the concepts, choose one to develop in detail. This part helps you expand on the idea by creating a story arc and suggestions for key visual elements.

    It speeds up the entire video creation process.

    Step 3: Generate An “AI-Ready” Script

    Once you’re happy with the concept, the last step is to turn it into an AI-ready video script that’s optimized for tools like Pictory, Veed.io, or other video generation platforms.

    Ready to try it out?

    Here’s the full prompt for you to copy and paste into your favorite LLM:

    You are an expert creative director specializing in emotionally impactful short-form video content, particularly in the style of viral Thai commercials.

Your goal is to create a concept that has the potential to go viral, touch hearts, and convey a meaningful message or promote a brand/cause in a subtle yet impactful way.

Please follow these steps to develop your video concept:

Step 1: Generate 3 Concepts

Create 3 distinct concepts for a 3-minute video in the style of a viral Thai commercial, similar to Thai Life Insurance's "Unsung Hero" or Pantene's "You Can Shine". Each concept should blend elements of humor and poignancy, delivering an emotional punch with a surprising twist.

For each concept, provide:
1. A compelling title (max 10 words)
2. Brief synopsis (2-3 sentences)
3. Core message or value being promoted (1 sentence)

After reviewing these concepts, you will be asked to choose one to develop further or request new ideas.

Step 2: Develop Chosen Concept

Once a concept is chosen, expand on it with the following details:

1. Story arc in bullet points:
• Opening scene (introduce protagonist and their everyday life)
• Build-up (show a challenge or societal issue the protagonist faces)
• Turning point (unexpected action or decision by the protagonist)
• Consequences (immediate results of the protagonist's action)
• Twist (surprising revelation or perspective shift "memorable melodrama twist")
• Resolution (emotional payoff and message reveal)

2. Key visual elements (3-4 ideas):
• Describe iconic shots or visual metaphors

3. Emotional journey:
• Outline how viewer emotions should shift throughout the video
• Highlight moments of humor and poignancy

4. Potential tagline for the brand or cause (max 10 words)

Remember to:
- Keep the concept simple yet profoundly moving
- Balance humor and emotional depth
- Ensure the twist is unexpected but feels natural to the story
- Focus on universal human experiences or values
- Use visuals that can convey the story with minimal dialogue

Step 3: Create AI-Ready Script (Optional)

When requested, transform the developed concept into a script format suitable for AI video generators like Pictory. Include:

1. Scene descriptions:
• Detailed visual descriptions for each scene (setting, actions, expressions)
• Timing for each scene (in seconds)

2. On-screen text:
• Any text that should appear on screen
• Timing and duration for each text element

3. Voice-over script:
• Minimal dialogue or narration (if necessary)
• Timing for each voice-over line

4. Music and sound cues:
• Describe the type of music or sound effects for each section
• Indicate any changes in music tone or tempo

5. Transitions:
• Describe any specific transitions between scenes

Format the script in a clear, structured manner that an AI tool can easily interpret. Use time stamps or scene numbers for easy reference.
