Ahoy, Digital Writers!

Want to write like Hemingway?

For digital writers, emulating Hemingway is a must.

  • Bold

  • Clear

  • Direct

  • Simple

  • Succinct

He gets to the point and makes a big impact.

And that's exactly the kind of writing you need to master if you want to grab the attention of today's hyper-short, dopamine-chasing internet reader.

The man is a legend.

But his real genius?

“The silence between the notes.”

And tapping into his style can turn your writing from meh to unforgettable.

Unfortunately, you can’t hire Hemingway to be your mentor. But you know who you can hire?

ChatGPT!!

So, I crafted a prompt to help you learn Hemingway’s “Iceberg Theory.”

Let’s dive in!

Hemingway’s Iceberg Theory

Imagine an iceberg.

You only see the top, but there's a lot more underwater. Hemingway writes the same way. He gives you just enough to get curious, but leaves a lot hidden for you to figure out.

Consider this example from the "The Old Man and the Sea:"

He was an old man who fished alone in a skiff in the Gulf Stream and he had gone eighty-four days now without taking a fish.

On the surface, it’s an old man's unsuccessful fishing streak. But, beneath it is unspoken emotion and struggle. He doesn't spell everything out, but you still get the big picture. It's like he's giving you clues to a puzzle, and it's up to you to solve it.

It keeps people hooked—because they want to know what's under the water, so they keep reading.

Now you might be thinking:

“Can’t I just tell ChatGPT to write like Hemingway?”

Sure, of course you can.

But here’s the thing:

What do you as an aspiring, growing, hungry, Digital Writer stand to gain when AI writes everything for you—and you have no filter for deciding if it’s actually any good?! Remember, you are in charge of AI. And if you don’t understand the fundamentals of what you are asking AI to write for you, then the machine is in charge!

There is one exception:

AI is excellent at teaching the fundamentals.

  • It’s available 24/7.

  • It can adapt to your pace.

  • It knows a lot about a lot of topics.

  • You can ask a million clarifying questions.

  • And you can get instant answers and immediate feedback.

When you embrace being a student instead of seeking a "fast pass" to knowledge, two things happen. First, what you learn really sticks. Second, you start building a solid skillset.

Let’s take a look at what ChatGPT knows about Hemingway’s style.

Hire ChatGPT To Be Your Hemingway Writing Coach

Every author has their own secret sauce that makes their writing voice unique.

Hemingway is no different. And ChatGPT knows this. Which means you can take advantage of it.

If you are curious, just ask: “What do you, ChatGPT, know about Ernest Hemingway?”

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Cp7N!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F04b5100a-0e22-456a-bcf7-13302206d6a8_1326x686.png)

Voilà! Now you know what you stand to learn.

But this is just “information.” And information without application is worthless.

So, let’s make this practical.

Step 1: Tell ChatGPT what your goal is: “Write a short story. Apply the Iceberg Theory.”

You decide what you want to learn.

Just like you’ve decided WHO you want to learn from—Ernest Hemingway. We are telling ChatGPT we want to learn the Iceberg Theory. Even though we are deferring to ChatGPT’s knowledge, you are still in control of the process.

This takes us to step 2, which is HOW you want to go about learning.

Step 2: Borrow a “well-known” learning framework.

ChatGPT is the G.O.A.T. of executing proven templates.

Again, you can use this to your advantage. Ask ChatGPT what learning frameworks it’s familiar with, choose one you are familiar, or one you want to learn!

For example, here’s few popular teaching frameworks for writers:

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!frMj!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F061a4b4b-d0c7-4e11-af7b-c5560d5eaf4f_1346x736.png)

For this prompt, we are combining the Feynman Technique and Copywork.

With Copywork, you jot down Hemingway's lines by hand. Think of it as learning a song note by note. And by copying Hemingway’s work, your brain will begin to internalize his style, word choice, emotion, sentence form, etc. until it becomes your own.

We also instruct ChatGPT to use the Feynman Technique because it directs AI to simplify concepts, as if explaining to a child. Which is helpful when you are learning something new.

And finally:

Step 3: Practice and respond to feedback.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!odxX!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F504a9f38-fe3e-4abc-8813-415fbac5a97a_1314x990.png)

When you run this prompt, ChatGPT will hold your hand through the learning process.

Treat it like a back and forth dialog.

  • ChatGPT confirms your existing knowledge

  • ChatGPT checks in to see if you understand

  • ChatGPT corrects any mistakes (and offer suggestions)

And you can practice for as long as you want!

ChatGPT never gets tired!

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!B-ci!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F89ea1d44-d6b9-49b7-8b15-7a19f46e8a4c_1348x830.png)

The beauty of this prompt is you can use it to learn how to write like any writer that ChatGPT knows about. All you have to do is swap out the author, change up the learning framework, and name your goal.

Here’s the prompt!

I want you to teach me to write a short 200 word story like Ernest Hemingway.

The goal is to learn the his style so I can improve my story writing.

I am interested in learning how to write concise, direct prose, as well as how to convey deep emotional truths with few words using the "Iceberg Theory."

Here is what we are going to do:

You will prompt me with exercises, I will complete them one at a time, and you will give me feedback until I'm sufficiently learning to write like Hemingway.

- Use the Feynman technique and the "Copywork" technique to help me learn.
- I want you to give me 3 different "Copywork" exercises 1 at a time (2 paragraphs each) before asking me to write.
- Provide direct, critical, and helpful feedback.
- Go 1 step at a time. 

Before we get started, what questions do you have for me?

Remember: 1 step at a time.

Boom! That’s it.

You can always ask ChatGPT to write like Hemingway for you.

Your Digital Intern will politely oblige.

BUT if your goal is to become a better writer: learn and “internalize” the writing techniques of prolific writers.

First, WITH technology.

Second, BY technology.
