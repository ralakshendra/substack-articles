    Lean Writing: How To Quickly Repurpose An Article Into 10 Social Posts

    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Today, we are going to show you how to take a long-form article and turn it into short-form social content using ChatGPT.

    The mistake many writers make is thinking that every time you sit down to write, you need to start fresh—and say something new. The reality is, to write social content, you don’t need to start from a blank page when you already have a full page.

    • Do you have a blog?

    • Do you have a book?

    • Do you have podcast?

    • Do you have a newsletter?

    • Do you have a YouTube channel?

    Then you don’t need to write net-new content.

    This is thinking about content creation like “starting over.”

    Instead you should take an existing long-form asset, like an article, and pull out as much short-form social content as you can. Which will save you hours of writing time. And if you are rewriting content that’s proven, it’s the easiest way to create awareness on social and drive people to your blog, newsletter, email course, etc.

    So, ask yourself, “What have I already written? And how can I rewrite it over and over again!”

    Let’s take a look at the two ways you can leverage this for yourself.

    How To Compress An Article Into Short-Form

    There are two primary ways to repurpose content you’ve already created.

    All great “writing” is basically just a Jenga tower of ideas stacked on top of each other. And so the easiest way to compress the “ideas” in an article is by listing them out in short-form!

    Once you see it, you will see it everywhere.

    Way #1: Turn An Article Into An Outline As Content

    Grab any piece of writing.

    It can be your own or something you’ve read recently.

    Notice how it’s organized.

    • Is it a list of steps?

    • Is it a list of reasons?

    • Is it a list of mistakes?

    For example:

    Look at this article: 8 Books That Will Teach You All You Need to Know About Writing.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!a4Z2!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fca41918d-7e7b-48ba-9df2-e1ab98834c28_696x378.gif)

    What do you notice?

    It’s a list of books!

    And the easiest way to create your first piece of social content from this article is to reuse the headline as the hook, and then list the books.

    Like this:

    *8 books I recommend to every beginner writer:

    • On Writing • Atomic Habits • The War of Art • On Writing Well • Show Your Work • Elements of Style • The Creative Habit • Art and Business of Online Writing*

    Short-form content is just a compressed long-form outline.

    Simple!

    So, the very first thing you should do to expand your online library is turn your long-form assets into “Outlines As Content” using each of the main points in the article as bullets in your Tweet, LinkedIn post, etc. The thinking is already done! All you have to do is pull out each main point and transform them into a new format.

    Which takes us to the next way you can repurpose an existing asset.

    Way #2: Turn Each Main Idea Into An Outline As Content

    The other option for compressing an article is to zoom in on one single idea.

    Look at what you have already said for any one of the main points in a piece of content. Then break it down into an Outline As Content, using each sub-point in the main idea as bullets or prose in your short-form text.

    For example, let’s turn a section from the same blog post above into a piece of social content.

    We start with a long-form section.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!d3mz!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe300f5ca-4e13-45db-8a17-cfa621842a66_1582x1336.png)

    And then we break it down into a shorter post.

    Like this:

    Another super tactical writing book:

    The Elements of Style by Strunk & White

    • Active vs. passive voice

    • Positive vs. negative form

    • Concise vs. verbose writing

    • Lists of words to never use again

    Worth keeping a copy on your desk.

    Did we write anything new here?

    No. The idea was already there. We just formatted it in the form of a tweet.

    Inside anything you have already written there are dozens of ideas like this you can repurpose.

    Now you can either do this on your own, or your can hire ChatGPT to do it for you!

    How To Use ChatGPT To Turn An Article Into Short-Form

    Can you use ChatGPT to create Outlines As Content?

    You bet!

    Here’s how:

    Step 1: Train ChatGPT on the 2 was to compress long-form.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Y6AO!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa64c0cc7-403f-47ae-85d4-21ed177d4830_1190x406.png)

    Copy/paste this script inside ChatGPT:

    I am going to give you a long-form text and I want you to turn it into 10 short-form texts.

There are 2 ways you can create a short-form text from a long-form text.

- Way #1: Use sub-headers in the long-form text as bullets in the short-form text.
- Way #2: Use a sub-header in the long-form text as a main point in the short-form text.

Please create at least 1 of each type of short-form text in your response.

*******************

Here's a tempate to follow:

{HOOK POINT} 10-12 words.

List of supporting bullets for the POINT.

1 clear, poignant takeaway sentence.

*******************

For each short-form text hook the reader in the first sentence.

Here are some hook templates you can use for inspiration:

- {Number} X for Y
- Another {mistake/tip/step/benefit/reason/example/resource/framework} for X
- Another good X: Y
- Another helpful X for Y
- How to X so that Y
- Quick hack to X: 
- Stop trying to X, start Y
- Most people X, but the trick is to Y
- Less focus on X, more focus on Y
- One of the biggest mistakes I see is X
- Don’t do X, do Y
- The best X are really just Y

*******************

A few rules:
- A short form text must be exactly 280 characters, including spaces and punctuation.
- Keep the format and spacing of the template.
- Just give me the short-form text in your output (No labels).

Any questions?

    Step 2: Choose an article and copy/paste the text into ChatGPT.

    ChatGPT will write 10 short-form posts for you using the two ways to compress long-form.

    Not too bad.

    Should you copy/paste these to X/Twitter without editing? I don’t recommend it. But with a few small edits, you will have plenty of posts you can repurpose for short-form content in less than 10 minutes. The best part about this prompt is it helps you see how much juice you can squeeze out of a single long-form asset. You can compress anything you’ve already written in a 1,000 different ways.

    Go through your long-form content library and pull out as much social content as you can, don’t reinvent the wheel, and leave the blank page behind you.

    The more proven content you publish, the more momentum you build. The more your momentum compounds, the bigger your audience grows. Ultimately, you will "become known for a niche you own" that pays you financial and social dividends for a lifetime!
