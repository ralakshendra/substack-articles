How to recession-proof your writing

Are you playing Robot Roulette?

Author: Nicolas Cole
Date: 2025-11-19

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!tLbX!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4c4ed85d-b4a5-443a-954b-7fca78b048ad_2000x1428.png)

The easier AI gets to use, the worse most people get at thinking and writing with AI.

Because when you’re bombarded with 34GB of content every single day (that’s over 100,000 words), it’s easy to be paralyzed by options. And instead of making decisions, you outsource all your thinking to AI—because “it’s faster.”

Sure, ChatGPT gives you answers.

But you’re slowly training yourself OUT of critical thinking. And every time you ask AI to make a decision for you, you’re weakening your decision-making muscle. You’re literally eroding the skill that makes you valuable as a writer and thinker.

And it’s why we believe we are in the middle of a Clarity Recession.

People are paralyzed by choices that should be simple

And they’re drowning in options that all feel “good enough” but they don’t know which one to pick.

So to compensate, they ask AI questions like:

  • “Is this a good email?

  • “Should I pick up this client?”

  • “What’s the best business to start?”

  • “Which topic should I write about today?”

  • “Where should I invest my money in the stock market?”

Sounds like Robot Roulette, right?

Now to be clear, I am not immune. In fact, I ask AI to solve problems I should think through myself all the time. It’s hard not to fall into this trap when it’s so easy to pop a prompt into a chat box and watch as your answer instantly appears.

The good news is, it’s not your fault.

You’re not broken, indecisive, or lacking something “fundamental.” You’re operating in an environment literally engineered to keep you confused, consuming, and stuck. Every algorithm is optimized to keep you scrolling, refreshing, and scrolling some more.

You’re responding exactly how the system designed you to respond.

How to break this doom loop and take another path with AI

The best way I’ve discovered to “AI proof my thinking” is with a very simple fix.

Stop asking AI for answers. Start asking AI to help you ask better questions.

Good writing is rooted in critical thinking.

And critical thinking demands good questioning. A well asked question can cut to the heart of a complex challenge immediately and lead you to original insights, stronger writing, and clearer decisions.

The big problem is AI doesn’t know YOU. ChatGPT doesn’t know your values, your context, your personal situation. It knows patterns and probabilities. Which means AI picks the best decision for “most people”—and that’s rarely the best decision for YOU.

Which means you need to circumvent AI’s “what’ good for one is good for all” knee jerk responses.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3hKL!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff70bbba4-76e4-4637-9e27-bc0cb50ac9ff_1300x788.png)

How to use AI to become a better questioner (not a worse thinker)

All you have to do is add one simple line to the end of any prompt:

“Start by asking me 3 questions to help me clarify my thinking. After that, ask one question at a time until I say stop. Don’t solve anything for me. Only clarify, challenge assumptions, raise obstacles, and pressure-test my reasoning.”

That’s it.

Now instead of getting a generic answer, you get:

  • Questions that force you to think deeper about what you actually want

  • Clarity on gaps in your thinking you hadn’t considered

  • A chance to refine your request based on YOUR context, not AI’s assumptions

You’re training yourself TO think, not away from it.

Here’s a simple example:

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!E39E!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fea567bbf-7d55-46a9-8930-c5d230488d2b_1516x1166.png)

Boom.

Suddenly you can see the assumptions you (and even AI) are making.

Notice how each question forces me to get specific—not just “decision-making” but WHICH challenge. Not just “use AI differently” but what the actual behavior change should be. Not just theory, but my own experience.

This helps you quickly identify where you need to spend more time thinking before you ask AI to create anything for you.

So when you’re stuck on:

  • What topic to write about

  • How to approach a conversation

  • Which direction to take your business

  • What your next move should be

Don’t ask AI for the answer.

Ask AI to help you ask better questions.

You’ll get clearer on what you actually want. You’ll build your critical thinking muscle. And ironically, you’ll get better outputs from AI too—because you’ll be prompting from a place of clarity.

That’s it.

Next time you open ChatGPT, append this to your prompt:

“Ask me at least 3 questions to help me clarify my thinking.”

Then answer the questions to further refine your prompt. This is how you can use AI without outsourcing your brain.

Tomorrow, I’ll show you the exact questioning system I use to start every day with extreme clarity.

But for now? Just try the 3-question trick.

You’ll be surprised how fast you start catching your own fuzzy thinking and asking yourself these questions automatically.
