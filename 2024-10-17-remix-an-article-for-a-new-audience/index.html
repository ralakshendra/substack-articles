Remix An Article For A New Audience

The Angle Architect Prompt

Author: Nicolas Cole
Date: 2024-10-17

[

Hey there,

If you've ever read an article and thought, "This post is great. I want to share it, but I don’t know what to add to make it relevant for my readers?", today’s Pick & Go prompt is for you.

Most people quit when they hit a wall (and instead settle for easy curation).

  • Copy a headline.

  • Paste a link into LinkedIn or X.

  • Hit send, wipe their hands clean, and move on.

This might work.

But...

Curating content is more than pointing to a link and saying, “Hey, look what I found!”

It’s like showing up to a potluck with a bag of chips—sure, it’s food, but no one’s impressed.

You’ve got to add some flavor.

With a link, maybe you save your reader a little time and introduce them to a new idea, but your taste only goes so far. What your audience really wants is to hear your perspective. They want to know why you think it’s great for them.

So, whether you want to share a podcast, distill a long article, or remix the best X thread you’ve seen in months, don’t just serve the chips—be the chef. Give your audience the why, the how, and your take on what makes it valuable.

Now, on to the prompt.

The Angle Architect Prompt

To use this prompt, input an article URL or any post text you want to work with into the script below and run it in ChatGPT.

The prompt returns:

  1. A quick summary of the reference content.

  2. 3-5 key takeaways (framed for your target audience).

  3. 3 different angles you can write about.

For each angle, the prompt will give you a structured response with a Claim + Benefit + Argument + Proof format. The claim becomes your point of view, and the benefit shows why your audience should care. Then you back it up with a proof point, which reinforces your credibility.

Example:

Let’s say you offer a Premium Ghostwriting Service and your target audience is first-time YouTubers. You want to share the ideas in this article about why AI won’t replace ghostwriters, but you aren’t sure how to frame it for YouTubers.

Here’s what the prompt output looks like:

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GiCu!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1945a584-7bdf-491c-acbe-233b560ab2dd_1354x2412.png)

If you’re feeling uninspired, this is a great way to get 3 quick ideas—including how to present each one for your audience.

It’s worth pointing out here, the goal is to add your insight and not ChatGPT’s. But this is a great way to generate a few ideas when you’re in a pickle.

Here’s the prompt:

I want you to read the below post:

**{Insert a URL or text}**

Then given my target audience I want you to:

1. Write a 1-3 sentence core summary of the target content for my target audience.

2. Bulleted list of 3-5 key takeaways from the target content for my target audience.

3. Based on the target content and audience, create 3-5 new angles I could write about (be hyper-specific)

Follow this structure:

*Angle 1: {1 sentence summary}*
line feed
{Claim + Benefit + Argument + Proof}

*Angle 2:  {1 sentence summary}*
line feed
{Claim + Benefit + Argument + Proof}

*Angle 3:  {1 sentence summary}*
line feed
{Claim + Benefit + Argument + Proof}

**Target audience = {Insert Your Target Audience}**

Note: This prompt takes advantage of ChatGPT’s ability to read from the internet, so all you need is a URL. But if you don’t want to use ChatGPT or don’t have a pro account, you can use the post text instead of a URL.

That’s it for today’s Pick & Go.
