How to outline a non-fiction book in 25 minutes

Trick your brain into feeling 20% done before you write.

Author: Nicolas Cole
Date: 2026-05-27

My first book took me four years to write.

My third took two weeks.

But now?

I can write an entire non-fiction book in less than 14 days. (Because I stopped writing books the hard way.)

Here’s how:

Every actionable non-fiction book follows the exact same pattern:

  • The origin story

  • The reasons why

  • The mistakes to avoid

  • The benefits waiting on the other side

  • The how-to chapters, the actionable part people pay for

  • The insider tips and FAQs

That’s it.

Once you see it, the blank page basically stops being a problem. And writing your book becomes a fill-in-the-blanks exercise.

In the video above, I walk through the whole process and show you how I trick my brain into feeling 20% done before I’ve written a single sentence.

Give it a watch, it’ll change how you think about writing your first or next book.

—Cole
