    "Readers don't want AI-generated fiction. They want REAL stories from REAL writers."

    I 100% agree with you.

    Nobody wants to read soulless, generic stories churned out by a robot.

    That would be terrible.

    But there's a massive difference between:

    • AI-generated fiction: Where you type "write me a novel" and publish whatever comes out (yuck!)

    • AI-assisted fiction: Where YOU remain the author and creative force, but use AI to overcome common writing hurdles

    The Fiction Writing With AI Prompt Library is firmly in the second category.

    Everything you need to brainstorm characters, build worlds, outline your story, and finally finish your novel in record time.

    Inside this Notion workspace, you'll find 75+ carefully engineered prompts designed specifically for fiction writers—write everything from your first sentence to your final chapter.

    • Create a new, unique, and different Sub-Genre so you can become known for a fiction niche you own.

    • Quickly create a high-level outline of your novel using any of the 11 outline prompts.

    • Combine your Sub-Genre, Plot Archetype, and Outline, to build an entire, differentiated world for your story—from settings to characters—in record time.

    Whether you’ve been struggling for years, just getting started, or dusting off an old draft these prompts will speed up the writing process.

    Here’s everything that’s included:

    • Story Foundation Prompts: Lock in your genre, theme, plot archetype, and main characters—so every word you write supports the story you're really trying to tell.

    • World Building Prompts: Generate a world your readers can see, smell, and touch—complete with maps, mythologies, political systems, and even sacred holidays your characters fight to protect.

    • Character Creation Prompts: Build characters that walk off the page: flawed, driven, believable.

    • Outlining Prompts: Not sure which structure fits your story? Compare 11 world-class frameworks—then snap your plot into place with the one that makes your story click.

    • Scene Writing Prompts: These prompts take your outline and walk you through each beat—so you can write vivid, high-stakes scenes, fast.

    • Short Fiction Prompts: Write a complete story in under an hour. From 100-word flash to 5,000-word novelettes.

    Each prompt is designed to help you become a better storyteller.

    And, if you’ve ever said “I know the story in my head, I just don’t know where to begin,”these prompts were built for you.

    But…

    Let me be crystal clear about what these prompts will (and won't) do:

    What the prompts WON'T do:

    • Write your entire book for you

    • Turn you into a "cheater" or "fake author"

    • Create cookie-cutter fiction that readers will hate

    What the prompts WILL do:

    • Ask you provocative questions that spark fresh ideas

    • Help you break through decision paralysis when you're stuck

    • Save you from spending years on a manuscript that goes nowhere

    These prompts give you a head start (without taking over the wheel).

    You’ll still write every word.

    But you’ll do it with more clarity, more momentum, and a lot less second-guessing.
