The 6-Figure Email Prepper Prompt

The secret to writing 5 emails in 2 hours

Author: Nicolas Cole
Date: 2025-01-12

Nearly all of those sales came from email.

So, I recorded a video of myself writing those emails:

  • How I write each email

  • How I “Prep The Page”

  • How I come up with ideas

  • How I frame the value of the product

Watch the video below for a complete breakdown.

And when you’re done, grab the 6-Figure Email Prepper Prompt—so you can quickly map out an entire email campaign for yourself in minutes.

Here’s how you can write 5 emails in 2 hours 👇

The 6-Figure Email Prepper Prompt

Paste your product details, timeline, and offer into the INPUT section.

Hit run.

The prompt handles the rest.

In less than 5 minutes you’ll have:

  • An entire email campaign structure for 5+ days

  • Choices for your email subject lines and preview text

  • Complete email shells with clear subheads that tell a story

And a balanced series of emails that naturally transition between value and sales

All you have to do is:

  1. Copy/paste the shell for each email into your favorite editor.

  2. Fill in the details.

  3. Schedule the emails in your auto-responder.

  4. Hit send.
