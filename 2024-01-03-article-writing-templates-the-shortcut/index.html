    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Writing a great article takes time.

    And hiring AI to do 80% of the work for you can save you the time.

    BUT….

    AI’s “default mode” is not good.

    The output is flowery, full of jargon, and sounds like it was written in 1997. In the absence of instruction, you’ll get the average of what’s existed since the advent of the internet. Again, not good.

    If you don’t give AI any guardrails, you’re better off writing with pen and paper.

    Which is why today we want to show you how to prompt ChatGPT to write article sections, long-form articles, and even LinkedIn posts using proven article templates (complete with “nudge phrases”) so that you can write better content faster with AI.

    Better ingredients = better writing.

    Let’s dive in!

    Why Follow A Template?

    Article templates transform ChatGPT into a focused writing machine.

    Without a template, AI will make assumptions on your behalf (and then you will have to correct those assumptions). But when you prompt AI to write using a proven template you will get a higher quality initial draft. Which means far less editing on your end.

    So, instead of playing the role of editor, play the role of orchestrator.

    • Decide how you want to present your point to the reader—up front.

    • Then prompt ChatGPT to follow your guidance.

    Bottom line: You stay in control.

    And when Writing With AI, this means feeding it top-notch examples.

    3 Plug-And-Play Article Templates

    Every writer has trouble writing (even machines).

    And when you are first getting started, it helps to rely on proven content frameworks. It’s the simplest way to become more efficient. The good news is you can leverage AI to help you create a never ending supply of these templates for you.

    For example, here are 3 article templates you can plug into ChatGPT to kickstart a quality first-draft:

    1. Problem/Solution Template

    This format works perfectly for topics people struggle with:

    • Clearly state the key problem readers are facing

    • Share common unsuccessful approaches people waste time trying

    • Present your step-by-step solution process to resolve the issue

    Helpful for: tutorials, how-to articles, sales letters

    2. Common Mistakes Template

    An engaging article format especially for warning people away from pitfalls:

    • List the 3-5 biggest mistakes people commonly make

    • Explain the consequences of each mistake

    • Provide corrective steps to resolve issues

    Helpful for: evaluations, criticism, product reviews

    3. Step-by-Step How-To Template

    The perfect way to teach a process or method:

    • Explain the end goal or benefit upfront

    • Break down step-by-step instructions

    • Use examples and illustrations to clarify steps

    Helpful for: tutorials, recipes, assembly instructions

    Your goal as the orchestrator is to choose template that best matches what you want to communicate.

    From here, you can let ChatGPT work its magic!

    How To Write An Article Using A Template With ChatGPT

    Start with your topic, pick one template, paste it into the below ChatGPT prompt, and then watch a high-quality draft come pouring out.

    To start we will use plug-and-play “Step-By-Step How To Template” from above.

    How To Write An Article Section Using A Template With ChatGPT

    You could use the article you just generated, but let’s say you want to give the reader a little variety in each section. You can apply the same strategy for an article section that you do for an entire article.

    For instance, instead of describing how a founder can assess their strengths in Step 1 you could talk about the mistakes founders make to round out your point. To do this, you tell ChatGPT what section you want to modify and what template you want it to follow. You can also adjust the total number or words you want ChatGPT to write.

    Boom!

    You are in total control of what ChatGPT writes in each section. You will have to do some editing and clean-up, but it’s much faster than trying to work with you get out-of-the-box.

    Article templates help ChatGPT structure high-performing drafts for you to refine and finalize. Take advantage of this simple hack to write faster and write better.

    Let’s create more templates!

    How To Create Article Templates With ChatGPT

    This is a Mega Prompt, so we are going to explain in 3 steps and then give you the full prompt.

    Step 1: Train ChatGPT To Create Article/Section Templates

    First we teach ChatGPT about “Engaging Formats.” In Ship 30 for 30, we talk a lot about the 10 Magical Ways and an engaging format is how you string these “ways” together to make your content engaging.

    Here is an example of a Engaging Format:

    1. Introduce a problem

    2. Give reasons it's a problem

    3. Offer a solution

    4. Give tips

    This format combines problems, reasons, and tips.

    And when combined together, it gives the reader something “meaty” to sink their teeth into.

    Step 2: Add Sentence Starters To The Templates

    The next step is to teach ChatGPT to add "Signal Phrases" or “Sentence Starters” to prime ChatGPT, so it knows what we want to write. We give ChatGPT some example opening lines or transitional phrases to use within each section. Think of it like creative boost to get you writing when you don’t know what to say next.

    For example, when talking about “reasons” you might use the following words to let the reader know what you are about to give them:

    • Since

    • Because

    • The reason for this is

    Each of these words or phrases are like breadcrumbs for the reader to follow along.

    Reader’s love it when you tell them what they are about to get: “here’s a tip,” “watch out for this mistake,” or “for example!”

    Step 3: Create 10 Proven Article Templates

    Now we combine steps 1 and 2 together into a Mega Prompt. This prompt will create multiple templates that you can use for any article, post, or section of writing.

    For example:

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!fVPj!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F98acb6ed-c589-499f-97aa-7b46bca9aeb4_1096x492.png)

    Here’s the prompt:

    Can you help me brainstorm engaging formats and signal phrases to help a reader follow the engaging format?

An engaging format is an order of events that a reader experiences a piece of writing.  An engaging format connects compelling components in an order that flows, pulls a reader into a piece with curiosity, creates drama with pattern interrupts, and creates a well balanced argument. 

A signal phrases is a word or cues that helps a reader understand what you are about to give them. 

Here is an example of a ENGAGING FORMAT:

2. Give reasons it's a problem
3. Offer a solution
4. Give tips

Here are your instructions:
- I want you to create 10 different engaging formats for me that include 3 to 7 compelling components each.
- Name each compelling format you create.
- For each engaging format include 1 sentence giving the reader direction on when to use the format, and then a 1 sentence example.
- For each compelling component in each engaging format I want you to give me 1 signal phrase immediately next to the compelling component so I have a cue to help me start writing. Signal phrases MUST be 5 words or less.
- Use a natural, conversational, helpful, and actionable tone.
- Use markdown formatting

Here is an example of what you will be creating so you know what to create:
# The Problem-Solution Format:
Use this format to address a problem and guide readers toward a solution.

1. Introduce a problem: "Today we are tackling..."
2. Why it's a problem: "The problem is..."
3. Offer a solution: "To solve this..."
4. Give tips: "Consider theses tips..."

Do you understand?

    This prompt requests 10 templates, but you can create as many as you like. And you tell direct ChatGPT the kind of template you want to create.

    For example, if I wanted to create a variety of article introduction templates I could say something like, “Can you create 3 more formats, but focus on engaging introductions for an article?”

    You can create a compelling format for anything you can imagine. All you have to do is ask!

    And once you have a template, plug it into the Article or Section prompt above to help you write.

    Following proven formats enables you to skip right to high-level editing instead of wordsmithing. Structure matters, whether generated by human or machine. And when you supply ChatGPT with an intentional template, you equip it to produce focused, clear writing.

    In short, article templates let you orchestrate better AI writing from start to finish.
