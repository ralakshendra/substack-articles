    Video is a great foundation for your content ecosystem.

    But let’s be honest.

    It’s intimidating.

    It takes time to:

    • Set up and record

    • Edit and refine

    • Get comfortable on camera

    That’s why most people never start.

    So, I want to show you an easy way to get started without spending hours editing, hiring a team, or sitting in front of a camera longer than you want to.

    The “Shortcut” To Getting Started With Video? Shorts!

    Open any social platform right now.

    What do you see?

    Shorts. Reels. TikToks.

    YouTube now serves over 70 billion Shorts every day. Instagram has essentially become a short-form video app. Even LinkedIn is pushing video content to the top of feeds.

    Shorts require less production time, fewer technical skills, and lower expectations for production quality.

    A single video can:

    • Bring thousands of new subscribers to your channel overnight

    • Drive more traffic to your business than months of newsletter articles

    • Get your ideas in front of entirely new audiences

    They're the perfect low-pressure entry point into video.

    Plus…

    Video Content Creation Is Easier Than Ever With AI

    You can create a compelling 60-second video in under an hour, compared to days for longer content.

    Think about what goes into a full-length YouTube video:

    • Scripting (30-60 min)

    • Filming (1-2 hours, multiple takes)

    • Editing (4-8+ hours)

    • Thumbnails, titles, metadata (30 min)

    Total time: 6-12+ hours per video.

    Now compare that to creating a short-form video:

    • Scripting → AI helps generate a clear, concise script in minutes

    • Voiceover → AI tools create professional-quality narration instantly

    • Visuals → AI generates footage, animations, or stock images in seconds

    • Editing → AI-assisted editing tools automate cuts, captions, and effects

    Total time: 30-60 minutes per Short.

    And instead of publishing one video per week, you now have the bandwidth to post multiple times per week.

    This is the real unlock with AI and Shorts: more output, more reach, less effort.

    Here's the really good news:

    Scriptwriting Is The Secret To Creating Addictive Shorts

    After creating 350+ short-form videos in the past 15 months, here’s what I’ve realized:

    Every great video—whether it’s 60 seconds or 60 minutes—starts with a strong script.

    • A clear message

    • A hook that keeps people watching

    • A structure that delivers value fast

    And if you’re on this list, you already have these core skills.

    You’ve written LinkedIn posts, tweets, and newsletter articles. You already know how to write something that connects with your reader.

    Which means you can…

    Reach A Brand New Audience On YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok—Without Spending 8+ Hours Producing A Single Video Or Even Being On Camera.

    But turning a great script into a great video still requires a few extra steps.

    You need to:

    • Find the right tools for editing and assembly

    • Learn how to stitch together visuals, audio, and text

    • Understand the formats that perform best on different platforms

    • Figure out which AI tools actually save time—and which are just hype

    Most people get stuck before they even publish their first video.

    They overthink the tools. They waste time testing software. They get overwhelmed by the process instead of just hitting publish.

    Which is exactly why I'm building AI Shorts Studio.

    • How to transition from Shorts to long-form without losing engagement
