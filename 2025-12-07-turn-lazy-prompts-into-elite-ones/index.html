    This tool takes your lazy, one-sentence prompts and turns them into elite bangers in seconds.

    • It extracts your intent.

    • Adds context and structure.

    • Primes you with clarifying questions.

    And gives you a copy-paste-ready prompt that works with ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool you’re using.

    It’s useful for writers, marketers, designers, coaches, consultants—or anyone working with AI and wants better outputs in less time.

    The tool is called Prompt Cowboy, and it’s completely free to try with no sign-up required.

    Here’s how it works:

    You type your rough idea into the “Lazy Prompt” box.

    Hit enter.

    And voilà—you get a great prompt.

    For context, a lazy prompt is a simple prompt that doesn’t provide much detail or instructions for the AI.

    For example “write a welcome email for my Substack newsletter” is lazy.

    It’s sloppy because AI has no idea what your newsletter is about, who it’s for, or what you want to write in the email. Which ultimately means when you run your prompt, you get sub-part results.

    But Prompt Cowboy won’t let you phone it in.

    Improve Your Prompt By Answering 3 Follow-Up Questions

    This is the most valuable feature of Prompt Cowboy.

    After the tool analyzes your request and automatically preps your prompt. And it gives you 3 custom follow-up questions. This helps you improve your prompt by forcing you to see your assumptions.

    Improved welcome email prompt

    Here’s what my new and improved prompt looks like:

    Note: I swapped out the Write With AI details with variables so you can use this for your own welcome email.

    **Situation**
You are running “[YOUR NEWSLETTER NAME],” a [PLATFORM] newsletter that [YOUR NEWSLETTER’S CORE VALUE PROPOSITION]. You need to send a welcome email to new subscribers that introduces them to your newsletter and sets clear expectations for the valuable content they’ll receive.

**Task**
Write a warm, engaging welcome email for new [YOUR NEWSLETTER NAME] subscribers that introduces the newsletter’s mission, explains the practical value they’ll receive, and sets clear expectations for the [FREQUENCY] content delivery.

**Objective**
Create a positive first impression that builds trust with new subscribers, clearly communicates how the newsletter will help them [PRIMARY BENEFIT/TRANSFORMATION], and encourages them to engage with and apply [THE TYPE OF CONTENT YOU’LL BE SHARING].

**Knowledge**
The welcome email should:
- Express genuine appreciation for the subscriber joining [YOUR NEWSLETTER NAME]
- Clearly communicate the newsletter’s core mission: [YOUR NEWSLETTER’S MISSION STATEMENT]
- Emphasize the practical, actionable nature of the content
- Set specific expectations: [QUANTITY] [TYPE OF CONTENT] delivered [FREQUENCY]
- Explain the value proposition: [HOW SUBSCRIBERS WILL BENEFIT/TRANSFORM]
- Include a clear call-to-action that encourages immediate engagement
- Match the tone and style of your previous welcome emails
- Be concise while providing essential information about what makes [YOUR NEWSLETTER NAME] unique

The assistant should structure the email with:
- A compelling subject line that captures the newsletter’s value
- A warm, enthusiastic greeting
- A clear statement of the newsletter’s mission and what subscribers will receive
- Specific details about frequency ([FREQUENCY]) and content ([QUANTITY] [TYPE OF CONTENT])
- Emphasis on the transformation subscribers will experience
- A specific call-to-action
- A friendly, encouraging sign-off

**Examples**
Your life depends on you mimicking the tone, style, and structure of this welcome email for [YOUR NEWSLETTER NAME]. Here is the example you are using as a reference for the new welcome email:

“”“<Replace this text with the PREVIOUS WELCOME EMAIL or upload a file>”“”
