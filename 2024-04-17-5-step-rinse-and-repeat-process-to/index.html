    5 Step "Rinse & Repeat" Process To Pump Out A Quick How-To Guide With AI

    Author: Nicolas Cole
    Date: 2024-04-17

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!y6px!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5983a859-6b52-441b-8cf7-f582137b4c02_1260x900.png)

    Hey there, Digital Writers!

    Want to clearly articulate to readers how to do something specific in a step-by-step format?

    Let me show you how.

    Most people struggle to write effective how-to content. They ramble on without structure, burying the actionable steps in walls of text.

    This leaves readers confused and frustrated, without learning anything useful they can actually apply.

    The Secret To An Effective How-To Guide

    The best how-to writers take a completely different approach.

    Rather than brain dumping everything they know, they focus ruthlessly on the core steps that drive results. They break the process down into a clear, easy-to-follow framework. Each step builds logically on the last. The guide becomes a straightforward roadmap anyone can follow along to reach the promised outcome.

    That's the power of a well-crafted step-by-step how-to guide that you can rinse and repeat for any framework or process you want to teach to your reader.

    Here's how you can create your own along with the help of AI to get it done in under 5 minutes:

    Step 1: Define A Tangible Outcome

    Everything hinges on promising the right result to your target audience.

    Consider what your readers desperately want to achieve. What juicy outcome are they craving so badly they'd give their left arm for? What would radically transform their life or business? What would make their eyes light up like a kid on Christmas?

    A few examples:

    • "How to fall asleep in 10 minutes or less"

    • "How to get your first 1,000 email subscribers"

    • "How to make $5,000 per month as a premium ghostwriter"

    The more specific, tangible, and results-focused, the better.

    It needs to be an outcome your audience believes is attainable. Avoid broad or vague topics like "how to be healthy" or "how to start a business."

    Readers will tune out if they can't visualize a concrete end result.

    Step 2: Map Out Your Framework

    Now that you have a compelling outcome, map out the high-level steps to get there.

    Aim for 5-7 clear steps max. Don't get bogged down in details yet. Simply outline the sequential phases to reach the end goal.

    Here's an example for "How to sleep better:”

    • Establish a sleep schedule

    • Optimize your sleep environment

    • Wind down 1 hour before bed

    • Avoid sleep-disrupting foods and substances

    • Use relaxation techniques to fall asleep quickly

    Each step builds on the last to form a logical progression, which gives the reader an overview of the journey before diving into specifics.

    Step 3: Do The "Heavy Mental Lifting"

    With your high-level outline complete, now drill down into each step.

    Your hard-won experience or knowledge is what makes your guide unignorable.

    For each step, answer the following questions:

    • What's the core principle at play?

    • What specific actions make or break this step?

    • What mistakes do readers need to avoid?

    • What hacks shave the most time/frustration?

    Take the time to thoroughly explore each step.

    The actual writing will flow smoothly once you've done the legwork here. In this step, all you need to do is expand your list. Get all your ideas out on the page.

    For example, let’s break down the "Wind down 1 hour before bed" step for a how-to guide on getting better sleep:

    The core principle:

    Letting your mind and body gradually transition into sleep mode. Avoiding stimulation that keeps you wired.

    Make-or-break actions:

    • Dim the lights to signal your body it's night

    • Shut off screens (blue light wrecks your sleep hormones)

    • Read a book or listen to calming music

    • Do some light stretching or meditation

    • Sip on caffeine-free tea

    Mistakes to avoid:

    • No doom-scrolling the news or social media

    • No tackling Left Brain tasks like work or finances

    • No intense movies or page-turner novels

    • No chugging a ton of water (hello, 3am bathroom trip)

    Hacks to make it easy:

    • Set thermostat between 60-67°F.

    • Use blue light blocking glasses if you MUST use screens

    • Take a warm bath with Epsom salts to relax tense muscles

    • Put in ear plugs or turn on a white noise machine.

    • Use blackout curtains to eliminate light.

    See how much more there is to to say than just "relax before bed?”

    All your prep work sets you up to write out detailed, step-by-step instructions—for each step. But before you get to the writing, spend and extra 5 minutes to flesh out the bullets you decide are most critical to finish the step successfully. Explain exactly what to do in insane detail, sharing specific tactics, tips, and tools you've learned.

    For example, you might expand the following tips:

    • Set thermostat between 60-67°F. Air temperature in the 60s is proven to benefit your circadian rhythm. During the day, set the thermostat 3 degrees higher than your desired sleeping temperature to optimize energy in your home.

    • Use blackout curtains to eliminate light. We recommend Sun Zero theatre grade. You can buy them at your local hardware store for less than $50.

    • Wear ear plugs or use a white noise machine. Blocking out disruptive noises (snoring partner, anyone?) and enveloping yourself in soothing, consistent sound helps lull your brain into sleep mode. Plus, it's like having a "Do Not Disturb" sign for your ears!

    The goal here is to walk the reader through your process as if you were teaching your former self and make their “implementation” as easy as possible.

    Step 4: Put It All Together

    Now it's time to take all the pieces you've gathered together.

    Start by reviewing your outline and the key points you identified for each step. Consider the logical flow of information and how you can transition smoothly from one step to the next.

    Here's how to do it:

    1. Begin each section with a brief recap of the step you're discussing. Remind readers of their progress and why the current step is important.

    2. Organize the step into its main components, using your outline as a guide. Use bullet points, or numbered lists to make it easy to read and follow.

    3. Incorporate relevant examples or anecdotes to illustrate your points. This helps make your advice more relatable and easier to understand.

    4. Include specific tips and warnings throughout the section. This gives readers valuable insights and helps them avoid common mistakes.

    5. End each section with a brief summary and a transition to the next step. This helps maintain a logical flow and keeps readers engaged.

    Repeat these steps for each section of your guide, and you'll have a complete, polished piece that your readers will find valuable and easy to follow.

    Here's an example of how you might put this into practice for the "How to sleep better" guide:

    Step 1: Wind Down Before Bed

    The first step to getting a good night's sleep is to create a relaxing wind-down routine. About an hour before bed, start to dim the lights and engage in calming activities.

    Some ideas:

    • Read a book
    • Take a warm bath
    • Do some gentle stretching
    • Listen to soft music
    • Write in a journal

    The key is to avoid stimulating activities like watching TV, scrolling through social media, or working. These activities can make it harder to fall asleep.

    Step 2: Optimize Your Sleep Environment

    Your bedroom should be a haven for sleep. Take steps to create a cool, dark, quiet environment that promotes relaxation.

    Some tips:
    • Set your thermostat to around 65°F (18°C)
    • Use blackout curtains or an eye mask to block out light
    • Wear earplugs or use a white noise machine to minimize disruptive sounds
    • Choose comfortable, breathable bedding
    • Remove clutter and electronics from the bedroom

    By optimizing your sleep environment, you'll signal to your body that it's time to rest.

    …

    As you write, keep your target outcome in mind and make sure each step is moving the reader closer to that goal.

    Once you've finished a draft, go back and review it for clarity, flow, and overall effectiveness. Make any necessary revisions to ensure the guide is as actionable and easy to follow as possible.

    Step 5: Close With A Motivating Call-to-Action

    You've given your audience a proven roadmap.

    Now encourage them to put it into practice. Summarize the key steps and reiterate the outcome they can expect. Then challenge them to take the first step right away.

    For example: "You now have a science-backed formula for falling asleep quickly and waking up refreshed. Start tonight by implementing a wind-down routine one hour before bed. You've got this!"

    By ending with a clear call-to-action, you propel readers to apply what they've learned.

    And that's how your advice sticks.

    To recap, creating a standout how-to guide is all about:

    1. Define a compelling outcome

    2. Break the process into clear steps

    3. Identify the key principles and nuances

    4. Provide tactical, step-by-step guidance

    5. Close with a motivating call-to-action

    Follow this approach and your how-to content will leave readers equipped and energized to put your advice into action

    And once you have this process down…

    The real magic happens when you feed it to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Because AI will spit out 80% of a finished guide FOR you...in just seconds.

    Write A Step-By-Step Guide With AI

    Now that you've learned the 5-step process for creating a how-to guide, it's time to pour gasoline on it with AI.

    To get started, make sure you have the following:

    1. Your "how-to" topic

    2. Your target audience

    3. The promise or outcome of your guide

    4. A list of any specific steps or tips you want to include

    5. Any additional context that will help the AI better understand your goals

    The more information you provide, the more accurate and tailored the AI-generated content will be. However, at a minimum, you should include the topic, target audience, and desired outcome.

    Once you have this information ready, simply input it into the AI prompt provided below. Feel free to adjust the writing style or the total number of words you'd like the AI to generate. I recommend you use Claude for this prompt, but Gemini and ChatGPT work too.

    Here’s the prompt:

    
# INPUT

Topic = {Your topic}

Audience = {Your target audience}

Outcome = {The outcome you are promising}

Steps = {List the steps}

Context = {Any additional details.}

# Writing Style

Plain and simple. Keep the language straightforward and concise.

# TASK

Please create a new 1000 word text using the INPUT and TEMPLATE I've provided.

# TEMPLATE = 

## {Number} Step {Framework, System, or Process} To Get {Big Result} In A {Short Time Period}

Hey there!

Begin with a bold statement that promises to transform the reader's approach to the topic at hand. Use 1-2 sentences.

Describe the common challenge or frustration that most people face when attempting the task. Use 2-3 sentences.

Explain why this conventional approach is ineffective and leads to suboptimal results. Use 1-2 sentences.

## Use a subheading to introduce a contrasting perspective that challenges the status quo. Bold the subheading.

Reveal the key insight or secret that separates the most successful individuals from the rest. Use 1-2 sentences.

Provide a vivid analogy or metaphor that illustrates the limitations of the conventional approach. Use 1-2 sentences.

Introduce the alternative approach used by the most effective practitioners. Use 1-2 sentences.

Provide a high-level overview of how this superior approach works. Use 1 sentence.

Signal that you're about to dive into the details of this approach. Use 1 sentence.

Let's dive in!

## {STEP 1}

Declare why it's so critical. Use 1 sentence.

Expand on the key insight about why this step is so important. Use 1-2 sentences.

Provide 3 examples that illustrate the nuances or implications of this step.

Explain how each example differs and what that means for the reader's approach. Use 3-4 sentences.

Describe the negative consequences of not following this step. Paint a vivid picture of what could go wrong. Use 3-4 sentences.

## {STEP 2}

Emphasize the impact of successfully completing this step. Use 1 sentence.

Provide a simple definition or explanation of what this step entails. Use 1-2 sentences.

Caution against jumping ahead or skipping this step. Use 1 sentence.

Provide a bulleted list of 2 specific things the reader should do to complete this step.

Explain the purpose and benefit of following this process. Use 1-2 sentences.

## {STEP 3}

Reinforce the impact of successfully completing this step. Use 1 sentence.

Caution against moving forward prematurely. Use 1 sentence.

Provide a bulleted list of 6 specific questions the reader should answer to complete this step.

Explain the benefit of investing time in this "brain dump" process. Use 1-2 sentences.

Emphasize how much easier the actual writing will be once this step is complete. Use 1-2 sentences.

## {STEP 4}

Reinforce the cumulative impact of completing all the previous steps. Use 1 sentence.

Explain why this final step will feel easy and natural. Use 1-2 sentences.

Describe how the reader's previous work will guide them through this step. Use 1-2 sentences.

Provide a brief example or illustration of what this step looks like in action. Use 1-2 sentences.

Restate the core action of the step and emphasize its importance. Use 1-2 sentences.

## {STEP 5}

Explain how following the steps pays off at this stage. Use 1-2 sentences.

Provide a clear and concise instruction for the final step. Use 1 sentence.

Celebrate the completion of the steps and emphasize the reader's success. Use 1-sentence.

Now, remind the reader what they just learned.

Leave them with a final poignant takeaway.

# CONSTRAINTS

Avoid metaphors.
Avoid antithesis phrases.
Avoid purple prose AT ALL COSTS
Avoid words like "imagine, streamline, realm, game-changer, unlock, discover, skyrocket, abyss, you're not alone, in a world where, revolutionize, etc"

# Now, take a deep breath, relax, and try your hardest.
