    Turn dusty history into attention-grabbing LinkedIn hooks

    Why the past is the key to future success

    Author: Nicolas Cole
    Date: 2024-07-07

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!QhZx!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc3db0052-f925-46ca-a6d1-1db2e1ed73cc_1260x900.png)

    Hey there, Digital Writers!

    The best content on the internet sits at this Golden Intersection:

    • It tells a story

    • And it solves a reader’s problem (with a lesson, answer, or realization)

    If you have these two elements, you can create viral and relatable content over and over again.

    You might be thinking, “Nothing story-worthy ever happens to me.” I get it. But let’s put that to the side for a minute. Because I’m going to give you an easier way to tell a story—history.

    History is full of wild stories most people don't know about. It contains drama, plot twists, and stuff crazier than any Netflix show. The past is a goldmine of content ideas.

    For example, did you know:

    • A chicken once lived for 18 months without a head? "Mike the Headless Chicken" survived from 1945 to 1947.

    • Liechtenstein's army once went to war and came back with more men than they left with? They made a friend and brought him home.

    • Ernest Hemingway was a KGB spy. What?!

    Crazy—and perfect for grabbing attention online.

    But who has time to dig through dusty old books looking for this stuff?

    Most people don't, which is why we're going to make it super easy for you. And give you a ChatGPT prompt to quickly find those "Oh wow, I can't believe that happened" moments in history and instantly transform them into a viral hook for your next X Thread or LinkedIn post.

    Let's get to the prompt.

    The “Arresting Hook” Prompt

    This prompt helps you write an opener that “arrests” your reader’s attention.

    Like a plot twist in a crime show, a surprising historical fact can shatter their assumptions, and create a mental "train wreck" they can't look away from.

    For example:

    Curate Stories From History

    There are 3 ways to use this prompt.

    1. Go broad: Ask for "5 shocking historical facts." ChatGPT will give you 5 random facts, but it will get you thinking about different ideas for your niche. And you might learn something new.

    2. Niche down by industry: Focus on a specific field for more targeted results. Example: "5 surprising facts about the history of Hollywood"

    3. Zoom in on different time periods: Narrow your focus to specific eras. Example: "5 inspirational success stories from the Renaissance period"

    Each option will give you an endless supply of hooks from history.

    Simply swap out the Content Type, Historical Period, or Industry variables.

    I’ve include some options below to get you going.

    [CONTENT TYPE] examples:

    1. Inspirational success stories

    2. Little-known celebrity trivia

    3. Surprising scientific discoveries

    4. Unexpected business innovations

    5. Hidden gems in world history

    6. Remarkable personal transformations

    7. Groundbreaking technological advancements

    8. Unusual cultural practices around the world

    9. Counterintuitive psychological findings

    10. Extraordinary acts of human kindness

    11. Unexpected origins of everyday items

    12. Fascinating animal behaviors

    13. Remarkable feats of human endurance

    14. Surprising health and wellness tips

    15. Lesser-known historical turning points

    [HISTORICAL PERIOD] examples:

    1. Ancient times

    2. Middle Ages

    3. Renaissance

    4. Industrial Revolution

    5. 20th century

    6. 21st century

    7. 2021

    [INDUSTRY] examples:

    1. Technology

    2. Medicine

    3. Art and culture

    4. Business and commerce

    5. Science and exploration

    6. Politics and governance

    7. Sports and entertainment

    8. Education

    9. Agriculture

    10. Transportation

    Once you know what you want to write about. Plug your variables into this template (the first sentence in the prompt):

    “Write 5 different [CONTENT TYPE] from [HISTORICAL PERIOD] related to [INDUSTRY] that would make great viral content.”

    For example:

    "Write 5 different surprising innovations from the Industrial Revolution related to Transportation that would make great viral content."

    or

    "Write 5 different remarkable achievements from the 20th century related to Medicine that would make great viral content."

    Then run the prompt.

    And when it’s done…

    Check The Facts

    Keep in mind AI loves to hallucinate, so make sure you fact check.

    Try prompting ChatGPT or Perplexity to find sources for verification. As long as the LLM you use can access the internet, this fact checker prompt will work.

    Please cross-reference key facts and claims with multiple reputable sources to confirm the accuracy and legitimacy of these hooks.

Cite your sources with the relevant links.

    Ask ChatGPT To Finish The Post

    Found a hook you like, but need a little help to keep going?

    Prompt ChatGPT to write the first draft: "Please write a write a 100-150 word LinkedIn post for hook number 1. Add 3 key points, expand each point using the PAS framework, and end the post with a poignant single-sentence takeaway."

    You’ll need to do some editing, but you’re not staring at a blank page!

    Try the prompt.

    Write 5 different [CONTENT TYPE] from [HISTORICAL PERIOD] related to [INDUSTRY] that would make great viral content.  

Follow these guidelines:

1. The first 2 words must be a name that's easily recognized.
2. The first sentence must only be 5 words and make a surprising claim that's easily backed up.
3. The second sentence must provide evidence to support the first sentence.
4. The third sentence must be a compelling transition sentence to bridge your lede into the main content of your post. This sentence should:
   * Use a hook phrase like 'Here's how,' 'Here's why,' or 'Here's what'
   * Be written in the first person
   * Tease a transformation or benefit
   * Trigger curiosity
   * Speak to the reader's interests
   * Create anticipation
   * Make the content's relevance clear
   * End with a colon (:) instead of a full stop

5. The transition sentence should convince the reader that this content is personally valuable to them, increasing the likelihood of engagement.

6. Incorporate one or more of these elements that readers love:
   * Obstacles
   * Transformations
   * Changes
   * Meaningful people/places
   * Lessons
   * Breakdowns
   * Businesses
   * Clients
   * Relationships
   * Failures
   * Wins
   * Experiences
   * Frustrations
   * Goals
   * Inspirations
   * Aspirations
   * Mentors
   * Places
   * Moments
   * Fears

Remember to make each hook relatable, inspiring, and relevant to personal growth or success. Be spartan.

    That’s it.

    Remember: History is a secret weapon for storytelling.

    So, the next time you struggle to find a "story-worthy" moment in your life, turn to the pages of history (and ChatGPT) instead. There's an endless supply of shocking, surprising, and mind-blowing facts to capture your reader's attention.
