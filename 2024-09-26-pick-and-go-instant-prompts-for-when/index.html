The Juxtaposition Jab

I’ve written over 1,000 hooks that turned into millions of views and thousands of paying customers.

The secret?

A dead-simple copywriting technique I call "The Juxtaposition Jab."

You take two contrasting ideas—like success vs. struggle—and slam them together into two, sharp sentences. This creates a powerful "Wait, what?" moment for your reader.

Example:

"I used to spend hours chasing likes. Now, a 5-minute email brings in clients on autopilot."

The contrast shocks your audience, grabs their attention, and pulls them in—every time.

Effortlessly Write Scroll-Stopping Hooks With The Juxtaposition Jab Prompt

Copy/paste the prompt below into your favorite LLM.

Act like a social media copywriting expert.

<INPUT>
Target audience = {Insert your target audience}
Topic = {Insert your topic or challenge}
</INPUT>

I want you to write 3 hooks that use juxtaposition to grab my reader's attention.

Start with a contrasting statement or concept to catch the reader’s attention.

Use different types of juxtaposition, such as:
- Contrast in tone or emotion
- Personal vs. professional stories
- Success vs. struggle
- Old vs. new approaches
- Big ideas vs. small actions
- Serious vs playful
- Past vs present
- Hard vs easy
- Misconception vs truth
- Expectation vs reality

Write the juxtaposition into a two-part sentence, where the first part introduces one element and the second introduces the contrasting element.

The transition between the two contrasting elements must be smooth but sharp enough to create a punch.

Use relatable language that resonates with a social media audience (e.g., LinkedIn or X).
- Direct, uses personal anecdotes. 
- Short to medium sentences, contractions, everyday metaphors, first-person perspective addressing the reader as 'you'. 
- Write in short paragraphs, often just one sentence long for emphasis. Use line breaks between paragraphs for easy reading.

Constraints:
280 characters or less.

Examples:
“A year ago, I was struggling to get 10 likes on a post. Today, my content reaches 10,000 people.”
“In 2019, I was a nobody on LinkedIn. Today, I help others grow their audience by 10k in just 3 months.”
"You think you need 1,000s of followers to grow your brand? You can actually convert 50 loyal readers into clients faster than you think."
"My first product launch flopped. My second one generated $50k in 2 weeks. What changed? (Hint: It’s simpler than you think)."
"Writing blog posts used to take me hours. Now, I draft them in 10 minutes using AI."

Follow the below steps to jab your reader with juxtaposition.

Step 1: Input your context (a challenge or topic your ideal audience will relate to).

Need help finishing your post?

Add one of these options after the hook:

  • Here’s how: Walk them through the steps.

  • Here’s why: Build the logic behind your advice.

  • For example: Show a case study or real-world example.

Each phrase gives your audience direction, making your content easier to digest.

End with a quick takeaway to reinforce the value.
