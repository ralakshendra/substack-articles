    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Today, I’m going to walk you through a simple framework for using ChatGPT to expand an outline into an Ultimate Guide using the “10 Magical Ways.”

    To create an Ultimate Guide, all you have to do is expand something already "working" on social: a Twitter thread, a LinkedIn post, a Medium article, etc. These were the “short” versions of your topic. But that doesn’t mean the reader is done!

    As long as they have that problem, they will always want more!

    Build Your Ultimate Guide Outline

    Start by going into your analytics and sorting by Likes, Replies, or Shares, and just start making a list of your highest-performing content in each individual bucket.

    Data should tell you: “This is what readers want!” Pull a few different proven pieces of content together into a cohesive whole. Take an outline from a Twitter thread, Atomic Essay, even a tweet, and build from there. However you get there, what you want is for the outline of your Ultimate Guide to be based on something that is “already working.”

    Once you have your outline, it’s time to start expanding each point.

    Expand Your Outline Using The 10 Magical Ways

    Every single section, of any type of content, can be expanded using 10 different ways.

    • Tips

    • Stats

    • Steps

    • Lessons

    • Benefits

    • Reasons

    • Mistakes

    • Examples

    • Questions

    • Personal Stories

    In short: you want to make each big idea in your outline into a long-form blog post. (Which means your Ultimate Guide is really just 5-8 long-form blog posts strung together.)

    The way you write your Ultimate Guide is to literally just go step by step and “expand” each point in your outline with Tips, Reasons, Mistakes, Lessons, Personal Stories, Case Studies, and/or Research. You don’t need all of these in each section. They are like LEGO blocks—and you can stack and assemble them however you see fit.

    Start by “expanding” each section with ideas, first.

    • What do you want to say?

    • What are some of the ideas you want to make sure to cover?

    • What are the examples/stories you want to use in each section?

    Just make notes to yourself, and get all the pieces out onto the page.

    You want to get all of this “thinking” out of the way and clarified, so by the time you start writing sentences, you are basically just cleaning up your “notes.” And the wonderful thing about ChatGPT is it can clean up your notes and speed up each step of the writing process for you!

    Now let’s walk through how you can use ChatGPT to do this in 3 simple steps.

    Train ChatGPT To “Expand” Your Outline For You

    Once you have a working outline, you are ready for Step 1.

    Step 1: Train ChatGPT on the 10 ways you can expand your outline.

    I need help expanding an outline.

There are 10 ways you can expand any piece of writing.

- Tips
- Stats
- Steps
- Lessons
- Benefits
- Reasons
- Mistakes
- Examples
- Questions
- Personal Stories

I will choose which of these 10 I want you to use in each section of the outline, I just need your help filling it in. 

Do you have any questions?

    Step 2: Add 3-5 bullets under each Main Point in your outline to expand it.

    These Sub-Points create the scaffolding for your writing.

    And once you have these Sub-Points inside your outline, it’s going to make the writing process exponentially easier. You can leave it up to ChatGPT to decide, but you will get better results if you tell it what you’re looking for—and that way, it just has to “fill in the blanks” for you.

    For example:

    Let’s assume after looking through your analytics you find “Growing A Solopreneur Business” is a proven topic for your audience. And based on a series of X/Twitter posts about “Working for free, Repurposing content, and How to promoting your products,” you decide “Auto-Pilot Content Strategies” should be one of your 5-8 long-form posts to include your Ultimate Guide.

    Now it’s your turn.

    Plug a Main Idea from your outline into the script below. Decide how you want to expand it (Reasons, Tips, etc.) and list it below each point. Optionally, add your ideas next to each element. For example: “Reason 1: People pay for organization and implementation.”

    Run the prompt!

    Great, here is the outline I would like you to fill in.

Please do not change the formatting. Just insert responses.

Topic = {ULTIMATE GUIDE MAIN IDEA 1}

  - *{MAIN POINT 1}*
      - {REASON/TIP/MISTAKE/EXAMPLE/ETC. 1:}
      - {REASON/TIP/MISTAKE/EXAMPLE/ETC. 2:}
      - ... 
  - *{MAIN POINT 2}*
      - {REASON/TIP/MISTAKE/EXAMPLE/ETC. 1:}
      - {REASON/TIP/MISTAKE/EXAMPLE/ETC. 2:}
      - ... 
  - *{MAIN POINT 3}*
      - ...

    Note: Use ChatGPT’s response as a starting point. Refine the outline with your ideas and language. Ask for more ideas from ChatGPT if you need them. It’s much better to clarify these things in the outlining stages and not when you’re neck-deep in the writing.

    Step 3: Turn your “expanded” outline into writing.

    Now it’s time to bring the whole thing together.

    You have your Main Points & Sub-Points bulleted out. Now all you have to do is ask ChatGPT to turn your bulleted outline into prose! Easy.

    Copy / paste your expanded outline from Step 2 into the below ChatGPT prompt.

    Great, now expand the outline into an 800-1,000 word blog article.

Topic = {INSERT TOPIC}

Outline = {INSERT OUTLINE}

A few rules:

1. Introduce the topic. Use short punchy sentences. Keep the line spacing.

Use this format:

In 1 sentence, make a strong declarative statement about the topic.

In 3-5 sentences, describe why there is a problem, why it needs to be solved. And that we are going to solve it.

In 1 sentence, tell the reader what you are going to talk about and point out the benefits for reading.

2. Write each section.

Use this format:

In 1 sentence, make a strong declarative statement about the section.

In 5 sentences, clarify the opener, add context, and elaborate.

In 1 sentence, make a strong conclusion or transition to the section.

3. Finally, at the very end, give the reader 1 final takeaway.

*************************

Please:
- Avoid jargon.
- Get to the point. Avoid fluff.
- Use markdown to format the post.
- Use a simple, clear, direct, and relatable tone.
- The post should flow naturally from point to point.
- Bias toward short, concise sentences.(12 words max per sentence)

    Note: In this prompt we give ChatGPT specific parameters on how to write each section: “Write 1 sentence, then 5 sentences, then 1 concluding sentence. (1/5/1)” This injects skimmability and rhythm into the writing. Experiment with these instructions. Try varying the number of sentences and blocks. For example 1/3/1, 1/2/5/2/1, etc.

    Overall, not too bad for an intern!

    Repeat the process for each Main Idea in your Ultimate Guide outline. Then assemble everything together into a downloadable asset. Host it on your website as a free opt-in for your audience. This is how you turn the crank your Digital Flywheel!

    You can use this framework for anything: articles, eBooks, newsletters, courses, etc.

    These 10 content sections can be used over and over again in just about every context you can imagine. Which is how savvy digital writers and creators are able to take a single Tweet and turn that Tweet into a 300 page book, if they wanted to. Because all they’re doing is keeping the Main Points (essentially) the same, and expanding within each one using the above content sections.

    Expansion of proven content is the whole secret to being “prolific.”
