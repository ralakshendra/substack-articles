    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Today we are going to walk you through a simple framework for using ChatGPT to write content that deeply engages your niche readers/followers in a highly specialized way.

    Inside Ship 30 for 30, we call this “Resonance” content.

    Resonance content helps your core audience fall in love with what you create. Because it is designed specifically for THEM—the people who really matter in your niche.

    Specificity is the secret.

    Let’s dive in!

    Resonance hinges on WHO you’re targeting

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!yg45!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcb2b68dc-5810-4c6b-81a9-29e2dbc125b1_1172x1090.png)

    If you want your readers to fall in love with your writing, you have to first know your reader (better than they know themselves).

    So…

    • WHO is your reader?

    • WHAT questions do they have?

    • WHY do they have these questions?

    Let’s walk through each question.

    WHO is your reader?

    You can answer this first question by using the FOR WHO / SO THAT prompt.

    For example, if your target reader was someone interested in yoga, then your WHO might be office workers with a sedentary lifestyle, pregnant women, or college athletes who want to improve their range of motion.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EsoW!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F44d68540-edf9-46da-9d76-7cf56092dd7b_1112x706.png)

    Maybe you want to reach all these different types of audiences. (You can!)

    But it’s easiest to start with one and then build from there.

    WHAT questions is your WHO (audience) asking?

    If you are your own target reader (aka: if you’re creating something you wish someone else would have created for you), then chances are you already know what a lot of these questions are—because you’re just listing off all the questions you had at the beginning of your own journey.

    But if you are trying to write for a different type of reader, maybe someone you don’t know too much about, then you should gather questions from as many places as you can.

    • Use ChatGPT to brainstorm

    • Search your niche on Quora and find popular questions

    • Scan through the “People also ask” section on Google

    • Start conversations on Twitter and listen to what people struggle with

    • Write down the questions are people already asking you

    The long and the short of it is: you have to do your research.

    In order to write highly valuable, niche content, you need to speak directly to that person's wants, needs, hopes, dreams, and desires. You need to answer their questions. Which means, if you don't know what questions they have, and why they have the questions they do, then you don't know how to Resonate with that person.

    So, what 10 questions is your target reader asking themselves?

    WHY do they have these questions?

    This is where you “sell” the reader on consuming the content in the first place.

    • IF you do these 3 yoga poses every morning and every night, you’ll never have knee or hip pain again.

    • IF you practice yoga daily, you’ll recover 3x faster than you normally do.

    • IF you join a local yoga studio, being part of a community will keep you motivated to work on your flexibility.

    Spelling out the outcomes/benefits for the reader is what excites them to keep reading.

    Thinking about the above, can you imagine WHO your target, most ideal reader/follower would be? Do you know what questions they are asking? If yes, great. You're ready to go (although we always like doing more research and digging deeper to understand them better!).

    If no, here's how you can hire ChatGPT to help.

    Train ChatGPT To Resonate With Niche Readers

    We are going to walk through this in 4 steps so you can understand the thinking behind the prompts (which we will leave you with at the end).

    Let’s go!

    Step 1: Ask ChatGPT to impersonate your WHO

    One of the amazing things about AI is you can train it to “act like” anyone you want. Which means you can use ChatGPT to embody your target audience and ask the questions your audience is likely to ask.

    For example:

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Gz4v!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F258a70ae-e413-45b4-bf89-8d78bb13b22a_1096x1092.png)

    These questions are fine (and worth answering), but they are broad.

    We want to get specific. Because specificity is what causes your audience to resonate with your content. And the way to get hyper-relevant questions is by giving ChatGPT more context and constraints.

    Step 2: Add “context” to find your niche audience questions

    To continue using our example, we want to help ChatGPT further understand “WHO and YOU.”

    For the college athlete:

    • What’s the current fitness, flexibility, and training level?

    • Have they been injured in the past?

    • What’s their position on the team?

    For you “the yoga instructor:”

    • What type of yoga do you specialize in?

    • What services do you provide?

    • How do you provide those services?

    With a just few simple questions, you can already see how much context ChatGPT is missing.

    Your goal is to fill in as many of the knowledge gaps as you can.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!gdwE!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2079444c-1a87-4035-b332-3b6bc7360565_914x1178.png)

    By adding context to the prompt, you will get more targeted questions.

    Now, let’s add a constraint.

    Step 3: Use the “Six Thinking Hats” to get a 360° perspective

    Context tells ChatGPT WHAT you are focused on.

    Constraints tell ChatGPT HOW to focus.

    A simple way to do this is to leverage a well-known framework. Remember ChatGPT’s knowledge comes from studying the internet. The more popular and proven a framework, the more likely ChatGPT will know how to apply it.

    For example, we can use the “Six Thinking Hats” to investigate a topic from a variety of perspectives (just like a real person would).

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!erAS!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd4678c6e-4e1f-43ab-bfdb-d79eae19ea91_918x660.png)

    The Six Thinking Hats takes into consideration facts, emotions, consequences, benefits, etc.

    The framework covers the 6 decision-making frames people use before doing anything new—like read your writing! But after you run this prompt, you’ll have a list of questions that cover a variety of angles that pinpoint what your audience is already asking themselves. Then it’s your responsibility to give your readers solutions to these questions—over and over and over again.

    This is how you Resonate with people in your niche.

    Which takes us to the last step!

    Step 4: Turn each question into a piece of Resonance content

    Go down your list of questions (that you know are going to resonate because they are literally the questions your audience is asking) and create content that answers each one.

    For example:

    • “How can yoga help me recover faster?” -> How to recover fast using yoga

    • “Will yoga help me squat deeper?” -> 3 steps to deeper squats by practicing yoga

    • “What are a few quick yoga poses I can do between classes to help me stay flexible throughout the day?” → 5 yoga poses you can do in-between classes

    See? Easy!

    And it’s even easier when you have ChatGPT do it for you!

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!k6bc!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F392675ab-33c4-4674-ade0-c1e2ddb324bd_714x1316.png)

    Boom!

    And away you go, a month’s worth of highly targeted and resonant content in a matter of minutes!

    Here are the reusable prompts so you can do this on your own.

    Resonance Content Prompts

    Prompt 1: Brainstorm Questions Prompt Template

    Imagine you are a {The Target: FOR WHO / SO THAT}

Using the Six Thinking Hats, what are 10 questions you have about {Your topic}?

{Context about your credibility and your ideal reader}

    Prompt 2: Turn Questions Into Headlines

    Now, for each question, I want you to write an “Irresistible Headline.”

An “Irresistible Headline” has these 5 fundamental pieces. Beside each piece, I am going to give you some examples so you can learn:

- HOW MANY (a number): 6 tips, 7 reasons, 11 mistakes, etc.
- THE WHO (an audience): for project managers, for therapists, for founders, etc.
- THE WHAT (a noun): tips, reasons, mistakes, lessons, examples, templates, case studies, etc.
- THE WHY (a reason/outcome): to get promoted, to not lose your job, to buy your first house, etc.
- “TWIST THE KNIFE” (2-3 additional benefits/outcomes): to attract your first 5 high-ticket clients and move into your dream office.

Do you understand?
