    Ahoy, Digital Writers!

    Do you struggle with:

    • Quality idea generation?

    • Idea organization and synthesis?

    • Having too many ideas and not sure which ones to pick?

    Every single person who wants to start writing (or even has been writing for a while) faces these same obstacles.

    We know these problems well because the vast majority of people who join Ship 30 for 30 enter their first cohort saying these are some of the biggest obstacles holding them back. Which is why we created a framework for overcoming them.

    It’s called…

    The 2-Year Test

    The 2-Year Test is a simple way of helping you answer the question:

    “What do I want to write about?”

    And whenever you say, “I don’t know what to say,” or “I don’t think I’m qualified to write about that, it’s not because you don’t have anything meaningful to share or don’t have enough credibility.

    It’s because you haven’t done the 2-Year Test on yourself!

    • What questions were you asking 2 years ago, that you now know the answers to?

    • What hadn’t you experienced 2 years ago, that you’ve since experienced?

    • Where were you at in life 2 years ago, and where are you today?

    People don't want to learn from experts.

    They prefer to learn from those just a few steps ahead of them on the same path.

    Once you realize this, it’s huge creative unlock. It’s like when you were in the 4th grade. There were 5th graders you looked up to and could learn from. And there were 3rd graders behind you that you could teach about the transition from 3rd to 4th grade (the best hiding spots on the playground, where Mrs. Jones hid her snacks, and how to move decimals two places to the right when you multiply). You were an expert on the 3rd grade, which is massively valuable to a future 3rd grader.

    The same is true today. You are someone worth learning from.

    You simply need to write for the version of yourself 2 years ago, before you knew what you know now.

    So, who were you two years ago?

    (And yes, you can do The 5 Year Test or The 10 Year Test, or even The 5 Week Test. The whole idea is: reflect on where you were, and where you are now, and then teach the person who is where you used to be.)

    Yourself 2 Years Ago

    Think back to who you were before—versus who you are today.

    • What were you struggling with back then, that you no longer struggle with today?

    • What skills do you have today, that you didn’t have back then?

    • What hobbies were you just starting to get interested in?

    • What life transitions had you not experienced yet?

    • What topics were you uneducated about?

    Pinpoint all the ways in which you’ve changed over the last 2 years.

    Here’s the list I came up with when I did this exercise for myself:

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!rtSt!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa59a00ff-37c1-48e3-b82f-a2f1b26c7d65_2785x3239.png)

    Work through each of these questions and you’ll have plenty to write about in no time.

    Be The Guide You Wish You Had 2 Years Ago

    OK, now consider how many people in the world are exactly where you were 2 years ago.

    Who are they?

    Imagine you met one of those people at work one day. What is their name? What do they look like? What are they struggling with? What are their hopes and dreams? What do you see in them, that you can also see in your former self?

    For example, here's how 3 of my topics evolved:

    • How to build a writing habit for complete beginner writers

    • Nuggets of wisdom for ambitious 20-somethings interested in personal growth

    • Journaling for ambitious entrepreneurs who aren't yet journaling but know they should be

    All of these are quality ideas (because 2 years ago, I would have LOVED for someone to come along and give me this knowledge)!

    So, your job is to turn around and show the person behind you the way.

    Be their 3rd grade mentor.

    You should be overflowing with ideas, but this wouldn’t be Write With AI, if we didn’t show you how to make this 10X easier.

    You can either do this manually by asking yourself these questions and writing your answers down on a piece of paper OR you can use ChatGPT to interview you and help you come up with ideas to write about—which is exactly what we’re going to do!

    Let’s go!

    Step 1: Use ChatGPT To Help You Uncover Everything You’ve Done Over The Past 2 Years

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!h-ZN!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F355aa29a-81f2-4c60-8061-419553080833_1328x254.png)

    Your Digital Intern knows a lot, but it probably doesn’t know a lot about you—yet.

    Which is why we are going to teach ChatGPT everything that makes you an expert to the people behind you. So you can generate ideas specifically to solve the problems of your target audience.

    Here is how it works:

    • Feed ChatGPT the below prompt

    • ChatGPT will “interview you” about your past: skills you’ve acquired, lessons learned, life experiences, etc.

    • Your curious Digital Intern will want to know at least 3 things about you for each question. Push yourself to come up with at least 3 ideas for every topic. If you don’t know, or are at a loss for ideas, you can tell ChatGPT to “skip” the question and it will politely move on.

    Note: In addition to encouraging you, your intern may challenge your answers and push you to be more authentic. Sometimes your intern can surprise you!

    Here’s the prompt:

    I need some help coming up with topics to write about. 

I want you to learn about my life, career, experiences, etc. over the last 2-5 years so that you can suggest differnt topics I can write about. 

We are going to break this into 2 separate steps.

Step 1: First, you will interview me and ask me these 10 questions, one by one, allowing me time to respond to each one before moving on to the next. Once I have provided you the answer, ask me if I am ready for the next question, and so on:

1. Over the last 2-5 years, what are 3 things you've done to improve your personal growth?
2. Over the last 2-5 years, what are 3 things you've done to expand your knowledge or skills?
3. Over the last 2-5 years, what are 3 things you've done to improve your overall health and wellness?
4. Over the last 2-5 years, what are 3 things you've done to improve your relationships with others?
5. Over the last 2-5 years, what are 3 things you've done to improve your financial stability?
6. Over the last 2-5 years, what are 3 new experiences that have broadened your perspective on life?
7. Over the last 2-5 years, what are 3 things you've done to push yourself out of your comfort zone?
8. Over the last 2-5 years, what are 3 things you've done to enhance your creativity or pursue new hobbies?
9. Over the last 2-5 years, what are 3 challenges you've faced and overcome, and how did they change you?
10. Over the last 2-5 years, what are 3 things you've learned about yourself and your values?

Ask me if I am ready to begin the interview, and when I agree, ask me each question in sequential order.

We must complete this Step 1 before moving on to Step 2.

Once I have answered the above 10 questions, we will move on to Step 2 where you will generate a list of 20 headlines of content pieces I could write based on the answers I provided you in Step 1. 

Do you understand? If so, let’s begin with Step 1 and ask me if I am ready to begin the interview—and then ask me the 10 questions, one by one, allowing me time to respond to each one before moving on to the next.

    Once you've completed your “interview,” ChatGPT will ask you if you're ready to generate 20 headlines based on your interview answers.

    Just say “Yes.”

    Turns out you have plenty to write about!

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!LM-l!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F36cb8970-0399-4372-9b43-1ddd5ae89509_1734x1528.png)

    From here, your goal is to narrow it down to just 3 headlines.

    So, which of them jumped out the most?

    Step 2: Plug Your Headlines Into Your ChatGPT Personal Idea Generation Machine

    With the 2-Year Test in mind, your next step is to take your topic and run them through the 4A Framework to generate headlines and ideas to write about.

    We covered the 4A Framework and ChatGPT Prompt for Endless Idea Generation here.

    In short, you can express your topic in 4 different ways:

    • Actionable (here's how)

    • Analytical (here are the numbers)

    • Aspirational (yes, you can)

    • Anthropological (here's why)

    Once you do that you’ll be sitting there with a ton of ideas.

    [

    ](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!dCKj!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdc8c4b53-b2a5-4a7e-b6c9-a3b1bf1ae717_1000x1310.png)

    Pick 3 ideas (your next 3 days of content).

    Then get going! The rest will take care of itself.

    And just like that, you’ve gone from zero ideas, to unpacking the last 2 years of your life, to enumerable topics and ideas that you can start writing about today!
