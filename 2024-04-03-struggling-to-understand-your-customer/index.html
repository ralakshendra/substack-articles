A good visual explains the core ideas much more quickly and concisely than a bunch of text or talking ever could.

Now, why am I showing you all these visuals? Because ChatGPT/GPT-4 can interpret and apply visual frameworks. Which means, when you find a “visual framework” you like, you can ask AI to execute it for your niche context.

Pretty cool, right?

Let me show you how this works in 3 simple steps.

  • Step 1: Screenshot your favorite visual framework

  • Step 2: Drop the image into ChatGPT/GPT-4

  • Step 3: Prompt ChatGPT to apply the framework

That’s it.

Let’s walk through an example using Clayton Christensen’s Jobs To Be Done “JTBD” framework so you can see how this works.

The Jobs To Be Done Framework

Before we get to the example, who the heck is Clayton Christensen?!

Christensen was the best-selling author of ten books, including The Innovator's Dilemma, which received the Global Business Book Award for the best business book of the year in 1997 . You might be familiar with the What-How-Why Framework. Well, way back in the late 1900s, Clayton said, “to understand what motivates people to act, you first must understand what it is they need to get done.”

It’s the same idea.

You need to know the why behind the what.

And his JTBD theory seeks to answer the question: what is the job a person is hiring a product to do? If you can solve that mystery, then you can create products people love... like milkshakes for breakfast

Watch the video to get the story.

What does this have to do with you?

I’ll tell you.

As a content creator and Digital Writer, you want to provide value that truly helps your audience achieve their goals. But most content only covers the final desired outcome, missing all the smaller steps and needs involved in getting there.

JTBD looks at the full journey your audience takes, beyond just the obvious end goal.

It covers:

  • Related tasks

  • Emotional needs

  • Potential struggles with their desired goal itself

All around a single “Job” to be done.

Imagine you want to take an online course on public speaking. On the surface, you might think that you're hiring the course to teach you the skill of public speaking. This is the functional job—the practical, tangible outcome you hope to achieve.

But, there are other jobs the course might serve as well.

  • Related Job: You might hire the course to help you advance your career.

  • Emotional Job: You might hire the course to build your confidence and overcome your fear of speaking in front of others.

  • Consumption Job: You might hire the course because it fits well into your schedule.

The challenge is to understand all of these different jobs.

And by taking a holistic, “jobs-focused" approach to your product/service, you'll be better equipped to create something that your audience will eagerly “hire” to achieve their goals.

Clayton, and those after him have “shown us” how all these pieces fit together.

Here’s the visual:

So, as a creator, by mapping your content strategy to comprehensively address all of these interconnected "jobs," you can create solutions and content that provide utility and emotional support at every stage for your reader/customer.

Ultimately helping your audience to feel understood, supported, and able to successfully reach their desired outcome.

If you want to read up on JTBD theory, start here. But, a word of warning. If you do latch on to the JTBD theory, it is extremely powerful, but it’s a worm hole of content with loads of debate. And it’s way too much for me to cover in a single post. So, be careful with your time before you dive in. Plus, you’re here to learn how to turn the “wheel of jobs” image into something helpful for your audience!

So, let’s get to it.

Use ChatGPT To Apply The JTBD Visual Framework So You Can Understand The “Forces” Influencing Your Customer

We’ll use the example of a solopreneur who wants to create their first cohort-based course.

I dropped the image above into ChatGPT into filled in the below prompt.

It’s a simple prompt with big results.

Please complete the {FRAMEWORK NAME} Framework in the attached image.

My target audience is {AUDIENCE} who want {GOAL} so that {OUTCOME}.

Let’s do 1 more example from JTBD theory.

The Forces Of Progress

The Forces Of Progress are the forces that shape your customers' demand for your products/services.

There’s a push and pull. A push toward the desired outcome. And a pull toward to remaining in the “status quo” because there is a fear of change…and old habits are had to change.

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!p5bE!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff3bf064d-32df-4241-b50f-d0b51ae422ea_2048x1311.png)

This is a great framework to help you understand your audience better and how your product meets their needs. Because it helps you figure out what really motivates them to make a change. And by understanding these “forces,” you can create products/content that really speak to their needs while overcoming the objections that might be stopping them from adopting your product.

To do this for your product/service, feed ChatGPT the image and ask:

“Great, now can you help me fill the Forces Of Progress diagram for my target audience and job.”

ChatGPT does a good of filling in the framework for you in a single prompt.

The result looks like this:

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!PKu6!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4cc77336-cf54-47ca-8174-19d2981c48cb_984x1162.png)

Boom.

You can see all the "forces" impacting a solopreneur who wants to create their first course, from doubts about how long it will take to being "okay" with continuing 1:1 work to dreaming about financial freedom. This kind of customer research would take you weeks to finish if you did it manually. But with AI, you can quickly see what your target audience is experiencing without conducting hours of interviews or sifting through mounds of notes.

Amazing.

Now, whether you use the JTBD framework to research your audience or not, you have a new tool in your AI prompting toolbox to save you time applying your favorite framework (and maybe you learned a little about the JTBD framework too).
