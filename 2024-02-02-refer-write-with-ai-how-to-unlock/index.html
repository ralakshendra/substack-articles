Top 5 Ways To Get Your Friends To Join WWAI

Here are the top 5 ways you can get your friends to sign up for the Write With AI newsletter and unlock new prompts, score sweet merch, and VIP consulting for your biz.

1. Forward Write With AI To Their Inbox

Everyone is trying to figure out how to save time, make more money, and outsource the most “manual” parts of the writing process with AI.

  • Hooks & headlines? It’s in the newsletter. Forward it to your friends.

  • Struggling with long-form? It’s in the newsletter. Use that forward button!

  • Custom GPT’s and apps? Build your friend a Bot!

When you’re forwarding, make sure to include your referral link and tell them to sign up using it.

That way we can keep track of your signups.

2. Text Them Individually

Email may not be dead, but texts get read.

  • Send a DM.

  • Make a group chat.

  • Turn yourself into a talking robot emoji!

It’s a great way to let everyone know they should sign up for Write With AI using your referral link.

3. Post On X

Social Media a great place to post your referral link.

  • Search up a popular AI post: Then get a conversation going. Once you’ve added to the conversation (and made a new friend) casually mention the newsletter and drop your link. Cha-ching!.

  • Write a long-form post: Here’s your headline “5 Reasons Why Write With AI Is The Only Newsletter I Never Delete.” Add your referral link at the end!

  • Shall we DM?: This is literally one of the best features of X. Find people in your search from above and take it to the DMs. Share a prompt. Help them solve a a problem an ask if they’ve heard about Write With AI. Again, new friends!

But watch the spam.

You want people to join, not run!

4. Link In Bio

Do you know what the most valuable piece of real-estate is on social media?

I’ll tell you. It’s your Bio. And it’s the first thing people check when they discover you online. Instead of telling people your are a hamburger savant, point people to your referral link. (And “X” just expanded the real-state in your profile section, which means there is plenty of room).

Use this to your advantage.

5. Shoot A Short

Oh yeah! Turn on your camera and flip it around around so all your followers can see and hear from you about your experience Writing With AI.

  • Share your newsletter.

  • Break down a hook that crushed.

  • Air your dirty little secrets on how you write (with AI).

And of course, let everyone know they should sign up for Write With AI using your referral link.
