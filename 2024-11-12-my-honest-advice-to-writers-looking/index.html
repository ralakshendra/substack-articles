My honest advice to writers looking for "financial freedom"

PLUS: Everything we published last week (ICYMI)

Author: Nicolas Cole

In the last 10 years I have:

  • Founded 3 multimillion-dollar writing businesses

  • Grown 2 paid newsletters to multiple 6 figures

  • Co-founded a 6-figure SaaS writing platform

  • Sold tens of thousands of self-published books

And the biggest lie society tells us about being a writer?

"Nobody makes a living as writer."

Yes, you absolutely can.

Now, here’s my honest advice to writers looking for "financial freedom."

And instead, follow this roadmap to a 7-figure, one-person writing empire.

  1. You have to build a daily writing habit and start writing online

  2. Start ghostwriting on the side

  5. You have to write a landing page and sell your first digital product

  6. One product, one avatar, one channel to one million in revenue

  7. Reflect each year and always be looking for the next vehicle

Trust the process.
