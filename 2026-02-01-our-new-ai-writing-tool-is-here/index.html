    How To Get Started With Ghostbase (3 Steps)

    You can go from signup to published content in about 20 minutes.

    Here’s how:

    • Step 1: Create Your Client Space (2 minutes) Click the “Build Your Client Model Now” button and sign up for the 7-day free trial (or sign in if you already have an account). Set up a Client Space for yourself. If you’ve published content online before, check the box to import your published works automatically.

    • Step 2: Add Content To Your Knowledge Base (5 minutes) This is where you teach Ghostbase what you know and how you write. You can upload files, import YouTube videos, connect Google Drive, or manually add text from websites, blog posts, and articles. If you’ve been writing online, use the “Generate Memory” function. It automatically searches for and pulls in all your published content. (This feels like magic the first time you see it work.)

    • Step 3: Configure Your Writing Voice (3 minutes) Upload 2-3 writing samples that represent your best work, or choose a default voice to start. Ghostbase trains itself on your voice using these samples. You can refine this later as you use the tool more.

    Now you’re ready to start writing.

    From here, you simply select the content type you want to create: newsletter, LinkedIn post, article, or tweet.

    Then start a chat, just like you would with Claude or ChatGPT (or pick one of the ideas Ghostbase automatically generates for you).

    Easy!

    The more you use it, the better it gets at understanding your voice and style. Ghostbase learns from the edits you make and the feedback you give.

    Custom Playbooks

    Here’s where Ghostbase gets ridiculous.

    You can build playbooks for repetitive writing tasks.

    Let’s say you write a weekly “Case Study Breakdown” newsletter. Same format every time: problem, approach, results, lesson learned. You can create a playbook that captures that exact structure, your analysis style, and the questions you always ask.

    Now every time you write that newsletter, you just select your playbook, input the topic, and G1 handles the rest.

    Same thing works for your specific LinkedIn post formats, product launch emails, customer onboarding sequences - anything you do on repeat.

    Take any of your favorite content prompts and turn them into a playbook.

    Create it once, use it forever.

    Here’s what some of our users have already built playbooks for:

    1. Newsletter/email: Subject lines, welcome sequences, story-based emails

    2. Social platform templates: LinkedIn hooks, viral giveaway posts, Instagram designs

    3. Video script frameworks: Short-form video structures, narrative scripts

    4. Industry-specific guides: Mental health content, legal newsletters, faith-based writing

    5. Copywriting: Outreach strategies, SEO frameworks

    6. Client voice/style guides: “Write like this person” playbooks

    7. Content idea generators: 52-week newsletter planners, sales call → content extractors

    Once you see how much time custom playbooks save, you’ll start creating them for everything.
