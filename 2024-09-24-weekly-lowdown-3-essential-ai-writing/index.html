Bonus Prompt: The “If I Only Had 1 Sentence” Prompt

Use this ChatGPT prompt on any post to generate a concise takeaway with a clear "why" before you hit publish. It's a final check to ensure your message is clear. (If ChatGPT doesn't get it, neither will your reader.)

For example, I ran the prompt on one of our Ship 30 for 30 blog posts, How to Write an Atomic Essay—and it’s spot on."

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!v5Gi!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9cdc99e6-b129-4ea5-8418-6c883c18d379_1626x382.png)

Here’s the prompt:

Summarize this text in one actionable sentence that the reader can immediately apply, while giving the reason "why" to back it up.

<POST>
{Insert your text}
</POST>

Keep it tight. Get to the point. Be concise and punchy.

Remember: 1 sentence

That’s it.
