The “Secret Templates” Hook: How To Turn Your Niche Knowledge Into A Scroll-Stopping Hook

Author: Dickie Bush
Date: 2023-05-28

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Kf_s!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fda2e412e-f813-4af0-9650-9f7920ea7509_1260x900.png)

Ahoy, Digital Writers!

Today we are going to play another round of "Mad-Libs" with our Digital Intern—and transform ChatGPT into a fill-in-the-blank template wizard.

Everything we teach inside Ship 30 for 30 starts with a template. Templates are the ultimate game-changer to combat the plague of the blank page. And once you start using them, you'll find ideas you never thought possible.

Templates are THE cheat code to getting ahead online.

And because this is Write With AI—we are going to take the burden of “doing” off your shoulders.

Here’s where we are headed:

  • We are going to grab a proven tweet from our Ship 30 for 30 content library.

  • Then we will reverse-engineer why it works and show you how to use it.

  • Then we will train our Digital Intern to steal like an artist.

Let's dive in!

The “Secret Templates” Hook

We first wrote this hook in 2021.

Since then, it has racked up over 1.3 million views and countless more likes and followers for hundreds of other creators who have stolen it.

Want to know a secret?

It’s a template!

Yes, we just used the hook as the intro for this section. And yes, we are giving you a template to share your templates! But, did you care that we have used it before? No. Because what you want is the prompt for the TEMPLATE!!

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Q5_z!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa1893e5b-9043-479f-93dc-542d58aad7df_1640x1148.png)

Which is exactly why this hook works.

Why The “Secret Templates” Hook Works

Let’s break down what’s working in the hook.

  • It opens with a compelling topic simplified—“Ghostwriting 101.”

  • It builds credibility and attention with a huge accomplishment, “2,000 articles in 4 years”

  • It promises an “easy button” to success with proven templates offered from an authority.

Here’s another example:

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!5o9u!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2e9461dc-aad2-4695-87a4-1b26a75b8489_1640x1148.png)

It’s the exact same template for a completely different topic.

And guess what? It went viral too. Why? Because templates are always big winners for readers.

And your “insider” templates, frameworks, and proven processes are like sharing your directions on Google—giving readers a pre-traveled “proven” route to take.

How To Use The “Secret Templates”

Everyone wants a shortcut—so package yours up!

  • The 3 point meal plan to lose 20 pounds in 6 weeks

  • The step-by-step guide to finish your first novel in 30 days

  • The only 5 templates you need to write 500 articles every year for the rest of your life

Literally anything you’ve figured out for yourself in your niche is perfect for sharing as a “secret template.”

Use ChatGPT To Fill-In-The-Blanks Of The “Secret Templates” Hook Template

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!CWiO!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F97643997-a39e-4083-9ce5-dbe79f32c779_1296x254.png)

Run the ChatGPT prompt to start a new game of Mad-Libs with your Digital Intern!

  • Choose a goal-oriented subtopic from your Idea File. “Developing a running plan,” “Researching the local real estate market,” “Finding high-ticket ghostwriting clients,” etc.

  • Run the ChatGPT prompt below.

  • Play along by giving ChatGPT your subtopic.

Let’s go!

Here’s the prompt:

I am going to train you to write a "Secret Template" short-form content (280 characters or less).

I am going to give you a topic that I have credible experience with and you will write a "Secret Template" short-form piece of content following the "Secret Template" TEMPLATE.

You MUST follow the format of the template.

You MUST fill in all the variables and follow the template precisely to effectively capture the reader's imagination, establish credibility, and create curiosity.

---
Here is the "Secret Template" TEMPLATE:
---
### My Secret Templates

I started {Credible Experience} in {Year}.

Since then, I've {Big Accomplishment 1} and {Big Accomplishment 2}.

Want to know a secret?

I (pretty much) use the same {Number} {Type} templates every time.

---
Here is an EXAMPLE of what the template looks like when it's completed so you know what I'm looking for:
---
I started writing threads on Twitter in 2019.

Since then, I've written more than 200 threads and accumulated over 50,000,000 views on Twitter alone.

Want to know a secret?

I (pretty much) use the same 7 thread templates every time.
---

Remember: follow the template precisely to effectively capture the reader's imagination, establish credibility, and create curiosity.

Are you ready for me to give you a topic?

Boom! It doesn’t get any easier than this folks.
