The #1 reason people quit making videos?

It takes too damn long.

You sit down to create a Short, and suddenly you're:

  • You’re 30 tabs deep into YouTube tutorials.

  • You’re googling “best export settings for Instagram.”

  • You’re staring at an unfinished video, wondering if it’s even worth it.

By the time you're done, you've spent half a day on a 60-second clip—and at that pace, it's impossible to stay consistent.

So you quit.

The good news?

You Don’t Need To Spend 8+ Hours Shooting A Short

Everyone knows a better process leads to better results.

But the reason you don’t have a good process yet isn’t because you’re lazy or undisciplined.

It’s because there are too many tools to choose from.

So you try them all… and realize none of them do everything perfectly.

That’s just life.

But what actually works isn’t finding some all-in-one magic tool—it’s knowing which tool to use for which problem. And more importantly, knowing exactly how to use it—without wasting hours figuring it out yourself.

Let me show you two that will save you hours starting today.

Create And Publish A Short In 30 Minutes (2 Tools)

Here’s how:

1. Turn a Tweet Into An Instagram Video With CapCut

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!aEXT!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F48827be2-7c87-44d4-a510-8b4f0a7bca4d_966x1584.png)

Use this if you want to repurpose a written post into a high-quality Short.

  • Pick a high-performing tweets (It can be yours or from someone else.)

  • Export the tweet to Instagram. This optimizes your image.

  • Download CapCut on your phone (It’s free to try and $11.99 to export.)

  • Start a new project & upload the image of your tweet from your photo gallery.

  • Remove the background (so it's clean for video).

  • Browse CapCut’s library to add a new dynamic stock video background.

  • Record a quick voiceover explaining your post.

Alternatively, record yourself talking about the tweet, remove your background, and use a static image behind you.

Time to create: ~15-30 minutes.

2. Extract A Viral Clip From Long-Form Content With Descript

[

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9MTb!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fad19503c-253b-4440-aa59-4c35f46322c2_1282x878.png)

Use this if you want to create Shorts from a long-form video or podcast.

  • Upload your long-form video to Descript.

  • Let Descript’s AI auto-generate viral clips for you.

  • Drag, drop, and refine (edit text, tweak clips, adjust captions).

  • Export & post.

If you can copy and paste, you can edit a video in Descript.

Time to create: ~30 minutes.

PS…Want To Turn A Tweet Into A Script For Your Short?

Try this AI prompt👇

I want to transform my post into a script for a short-form video (YouTube Shorts/TikTok/Reels).

ORIGINAL POST FROM [PLATFORM]: 

[PASTE YOUR POST HERE]

Please create a 30-60 second video script that:

1. Starts with a powerful hook that:
   - Grabs attention in the first 5 seconds
   - Creates immediate curiosity or tension
   - Makes a bold claim or challenges an assumption
   - Promises clear value that will be delivered

2. Restructures the content to:
   - Follow a clear, linear narrative (viewers can't skip ahead)
   - Use conversational, spoken language (contractions, simple sentences)
   - Include natural pauses and emphasis points
   - Build momentum toward a clear payoff

3. Ends with:
   - A concise, memorable takeaway
   - A simple call-to-action (if appropriate)

Also include:
- Any key phrases that should be emphasized on screen as text overlays
- Notes on where to add emphasis in delivery

Format the script with timestamps (00:00 - 00:05, etc.) to help with pacing.

Enjoy!
